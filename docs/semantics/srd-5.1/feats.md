# Feats

<a id="srd51-feat-acquisition"></a>
## Optional feat acquisition

`srd51.feat.acquisition`

When a class level grants Ability Score Improvement and the optional feats rule is in use, the player may forgo that improvement to choose a feat instead. The character must meet every prerequisite at acquisition. A character can choose each feat once unless the feat permits multiple selections. If the character later loses a prerequisite, it cannot use that feat until it meets the prerequisite again. [SRD p. 75, Feats](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-075.md)

## Grappler

`srd51.feat.grappler`

Prerequisite is Strength score 13 or higher. While you maintain a [grapple](combat.md#srd51-core-grappling), your attack rolls against its captive have advantage. You may spend your action to attempt to pin a creature you are grappling. Make another grapple check against that creature, using the ordinary contested checks. If you win, both you and your captive become [restrained](conditions.md#srd51-condition-restrained) until that grapple ends. On failure or a tie, the attempt leaves the previous grapple state in place. [SRD p. 75, Grappler](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-075.md) · [SRD p. 95, Grappling](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-095.md) · [SRD p. 77, Contests](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-077.md)

**Acceptance cases.** Strength 13 meets the acquisition prerequisite. A reduction to 12 suspends use and restoration to 13 restores eligibility. A successful pin restrains both participants. Releasing the underlying grapple ends the pin's restraint on both. [SRD p. 75, Feats and Grappler](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-075.md) · [SRD p. 95, Grappling](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-095.md)
