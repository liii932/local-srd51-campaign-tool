# Magic-item rules

<a id="srd51-item-rules-item-description"></a>
## Item descriptions

`srd51.item-rules.item-description`

A magic item's entry gives its name, category, rarity, and properties. Use its own description for the activation requirements, charges, limitations, and other overrides. Items can grant new capabilities or enhance existing ones. [SRD p. 206, Magic Items](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-206.md) · [SRD p. 207, Magic Items A–Z](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-207.md)

<a id="srd51-item-rules-attunement"></a>
## Attunement

`srd51.item-rules.attunement`

An item that requires attunement provides its ordinary nonmagical benefits until a creature attunes, subject to any exception in its entry. Meet the item's prerequisites. A named class requires membership in that class, though a monster can meet a spellcasting class requirement with spell slots and the use of that class's spell list. A generic spellcaster prerequisite requires the ability to cast at least one spell from one's own traits or features. Item-granted casting alone cannot qualify. [SRD p. 206, Attunement](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-206.md)

To form the bond, spend a [short rest](exploration.md#srd51-core-short-rest) focused solely on the item and in physical contact with it. Use a suitable activity, such as practice with a weapon or meditation with a wondrous item. The short rest used to learn the item's properties cannot also establish attunement. An interruption fails the attempt. On completing the required rest, the creature attunes and intuitively learns how to activate the properties, including any command words. One item can have only one attuned creature, and one creature can have at most three attuned items. An attempt at a fourth fails. Attunement to two copies of the same item is also prohibited. [SRD p. 206, Attunement](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-206.md)

The bond ends if the creature loses a prerequisite, the item stays more than 100 feet away for at least 24 hours, the creature dies, or another creature attunes to it. For voluntary termination, spend another short rest focused on the item. A cursed item prevents that voluntary procedure. The item's curse and any curse-removal spell define further consequences. [SRD p. 206, Attunement](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-206.md)

**Acceptance cases.** An eligible wearer with three bonds cannot attune to a fourth item without first ending one. A separation of exactly 100 feet for 24 hours does not end a bond under the distance rule. A continuous separation of 101 feet for 24 hours ends it. A creature who can cast only from an item does not meet a spellcaster prerequisite. [SRD p. 206, Attunement](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-206.md)

<a id="srd51-item-rules-wearing-and-wielding"></a>
## Wearing and wielding

`srd51.item-rules.wearing-and-wielding`

Wear an item in its intended place and manner. For example, a ring goes on a finger, armor must be donned, and a shield strapped to the arm. Hold a weapon to wield it. A magic wearable item usually adjusts to a creature's size and build. The GM can restrict fit for a particular item's origin or shape and decides whether a nonhumanoid anatomy can wear and use it. [SRD p. 206, Wearing and Wielding Items](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-206.md)

The ordinary wearing limit permits one item in each of these categories: armor, headwear, and cloak. Footwear, gloves or gauntlets, and bracers each permit one pair. The GM can allow physically reasonable exceptions, such as a circlet beneath a helmet. Both matched pieces of a paired item must be worn for its benefits. A boot from one magic pair and a boot from a different pair grant neither pair's benefit. [SRD p. 206, Multiple Items of the Same Kind and Paired Items](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-206.md)

<a id="srd51-item-rules-activation"></a>
## Activation

`srd51.item-rules.activation`

Use the procedure in the category or item's entry. An action to activate a magic item is a distinct action from [Use an Object](combat.md#srd51-core-use-an-object). A feature allowing the latter, such as Fast Hands, cannot substitute for the magic activation action. [SRD pp. 206–207, Activating an Item](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-206.md) · [SRD p. 207, Activating an Item continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-207.md)

<a id="srd51-item-rules-command-word"></a>
### Command word

`srd51.item-rules.command-word`

Speak the required word or phrase if the item uses a command word. An area where sound is prevented, such as magical silence, prevents that activation. The individual item's rule determines the action cost, if any. [SRD p. 207, Command Word](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-207.md)

<a id="srd51-item-rules-consumables"></a>
### Consumables

`srd51.item-rules.consumables`

Swallow a potion or elixir, apply an oil to the body, or read the writing on a scroll as appropriate for the item. A scroll's writing disappears when read. After use the consumable loses its magic. Follow any item-specific targeting, timing, and application procedure. [SRD p. 207, Consumables](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-207.md)

<a id="srd51-item-rules-spells"></a>
### Spells from items

`srd51.item-rules.spells`

If the item lets you cast a spell, ordinarily use its lowest possible spell level and normal casting time, range, and duration. Spend none of your slots and provide no components unless the item says otherwise. The user maintains [concentration](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-concentration) where the spell requires it. If an item instead directly confers a spell effect, such as many potions, apply the granted effect and its usual duration without performing a casting. An item can override any listed spell parameter. [SRD p. 207, Spells](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-207.md)

An item can require the user's own spellcasting ability. If the user has several, they choose which applies. A user with none uses a spellcasting ability modifier of +0 for that item, and their proficiency bonus still applies. [SRD p. 207, Spells](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-207.md)

**Project interpretation.** An item that directly confers a spell's effect uses that spell's ordinary maximum duration and does not require the user to maintain concentration unless the item says otherwise. The concentration sentence concerns the user casting a spell from an item. The next sentence distinguishes items that give effects without a casting. Another reading includes concentration whenever the item inherits duration, and some item entries expressly waive it. Here the distinction is whether the user casts or receives an effect. A potion that grants the effect of Clairvoyance or Detect Thoughts therefore does not take over the drinker's concentration. An item that summons an elemental as if the user had cast Conjure Elemental uses the spell's concentration procedure. [SRD p. 207, Spells](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-207.md) · [SRD p. 233, Potion of Clairvoyance](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-233.md) · [SRD p. 234, Potion of Mind Reading](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-234.md) · [SRD p. 235, Potion of Speed](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-235.md) · [SRD p. 212, Bowl of Commanding Water Elementals](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-212.md)

<a id="srd51-item-rules-stored-spells"></a>
### Stored spells

`srd51.item-rules.stored-spells`

For the Reserve Ioun Stone or Ring of Spell Storing, touch the item while casting an eligible spell into it. Apply the incoming casting's normal costs. Instead of producing its ordinary effect, the spell is stored and uses capacity equal to the level of the slot used. If capacity is insufficient, the incoming spell is expended without effect. The item supplies capacity, eligible incoming levels, initial contents, and the required state for release. When an eligible user casts a spell from the store, use the original caster's slot level, save DC, spell attack bonus, and spellcasting ability. Otherwise treat it as cast by the current user, including concentration when applicable. The spell leaves storage and frees its capacity when cast. [SRD p. 227, Ioun Stone of Reserve](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-227.md) · [SRD p. 237, Ring of Spell Storing](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-237.md) · [SRD p. 207, Spells](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-207.md)

**Project interpretation.** If an otherwise eligible incoming spell is cast without a slot, use the level of that casting to measure capacity and preserve the level at release. These items allow any creature to cast a qualifying spell into them and state how to measure the common case using a slot. A narrower reading makes a slot mandatory even though it is not listed as an eligibility requirement. Cantrips remain outside both items' stated levels. [SRD p. 227, Ioun Stone of Reserve](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-227.md) · [SRD p. 237, Ring of Spell Storing](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-237.md)

<a id="srd51-item-rules-charges"></a>
### Charges

`srd51.item-rules.charges`

Spend the charges an activation requires. The item specifies the maximum or initial charges, timing and amount of recovery, and any result at depletion. Recovery of expended charges cannot add charges beyond that pool. Casting Identify on the item or attuning to it reveals how many charges remain. When the item recovers charges, its attuned creature learns how many it regained. [SRD p. 207, Charges](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-207.md)

<a id="srd51-item-rules-study-manual"></a>
## Ability study books

`srd51.item-rules.study-manual`

To use one of the three ability manuals or the three ability tomes, spend 48 hours studying its contents and practicing its guidance within a span of at most six days. On completion increase the specified ability score and its maximum by 2. The book loses its magic and regains it after a century. The item's entry specifies the ability. The Manual of Golems uses its own construction procedure. [SRD p. 229, Manual of Bodily Health and Manual of Gainful Exercise](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-229.md) · [SRD p. 230, Manual of Quickness of Action](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-230.md) · [SRD p. 247, Tome of Clear Thought, Tome of Leadership and Influence, and Tome of Understanding](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-247.md)

<a id="srd51-item-rules-random-resistance"></a>
## Random resistance type

`srd51.item-rules.random-resistance`

For Armor of Resistance, Potion of Resistance, or Ring of Resistance, the GM chooses the damage type or rolls d10. Read outcomes 1 through 10 in order as acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, poison, psychic, radiant, and thunder. The ring's type also fixes its gem. Use the item's own eligibility and duration. [SRD pp. 208–209, Armor of Resistance](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-208.md) · [SRD p. 209, Armor of Resistance table](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-209.md) · [SRD p. 235, Potion of Resistance](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-235.md) · [SRD p. 237, Ring of Resistance](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-237.md)

<a id="srd51-item-rules-natural-twenty-extra-damage"></a>
## Extra damage triggered by a natural 20

`srd51.item-rules.natural-twenty-extra-damage`

**Project interpretation.** If an item gives additional damage dice specifically when the attack roll's selected d20 is 20, add those dice once after the ordinary [critical damage dice](combat.md#srd51-core-critical-hits). This preserves a separately stated benefit keyed to that roll. An alternative includes these dice among all attack damage dice doubled by a critical hit. The selected reading applies to Mace of Smiting, Sword of Life Stealing, Sword of Sharpness, Vicious Weapon, and the extra-damage branch of Vorpal Sword. An ordinary rider that adds dice to any hit, such as an active Flame Tongue's fire dice, remains part of the attack's critical dice. [SRD pp. 96–97, Critical Hits](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-096.md) · [SRD p. 97, Critical Hits continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-097.md) · [SRD p. 223, Flame Tongue](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-223.md) · [SRD p. 229, Mace of Smiting](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-229.md) · [SRD p. 246, Sword of Life Stealing and Sword of Sharpness](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-246.md) · [SRD p. 248, Vicious Weapon and Vorpal Sword](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-248.md)

<a id="srd51-item-rules-extradimensional-nesting"></a>
## Extradimensional nesting

`srd51.item-rules.extradimensional-nesting`

Apply this procedure when an item with this provision is placed inside extradimensional space created by a Bag of Holding, Handy Haversack, Portable Hole, or a similar item as its entry specifies. Both items are destroyed immediately. A one-way gate opens where the item was inserted. Each creature within 10 feet is drawn through to a random place on the Astral Plane. The gate closes afterward and cannot be reopened. This particular destruction procedure is triggered by nesting the items. Use each item's own rule for other breakage, which may treat its contents differently. [SRD p. 210, Bag of Holding](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-210.md) · [SRD p. 225, Handy Haversack](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-225.md) · [SRD p. 233, Portable Hole](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-233.md)
