# Magic items from Dagger of Venom through Mirror of Life Trapping

Each entry applies the [magic-item rules](magic-items.md) and any stated item-specific overrides. The item metadata identifies which items require attunement.

## Dagger of Venom

`srd51.item.dagger-of-venom` · Weapon, dagger · rare. [SRD p. 215, Dagger of Venom](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-215.md)

Add +1 to attack and damage rolls. Use an action to coat the blade with its magical poison for 1 minute or until an attack with the dagger hits a creature. That creature makes a DC 15 Constitution save. Failure deals 2d10 poison damage and poisons it for 1 minute. The coating activation returns at the next dawn.

[SRD p. 215, Dagger of Venom](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-215.md)

## Dancing Sword

`srd51.item.dancing-sword` · Weapon, any sword · very rare · attunement. [SRD p. 215, Dancing Sword](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-215.md) · [SRD p. 216, Dancing Sword continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-216.md)

Spend a bonus action to toss the sword and speak its command word. It hovers, flies up to 30 feet, and attacks one creature you choose within 5 feet of it, using your attack roll and ability modifier for damage. On a later bonus action you can move the hovering sword up to 30 feet to a spot within 30 feet of you and have it attack a creature within 5 feet of it as part of that same bonus action.

After its fourth attack, the sword flies up to 30 feet to try to return to your hand. If you have no free hand, it drops at your feet. If it has no clear path to you, it approaches as closely as it can and drops there. It also stops hovering if you grasp it or move more than 30 feet from it.

## Decanter of Endless Water

`srd51.item.decanter-of-endless-water` · Wondrous item · uncommon. [SRD p. 216, Decanter of Endless Water](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-216.md)

The flask weighs 2 lb. Spend an action to unstopper it and speak a command word, choosing fresh or salt water. The flow stops at the start of your next turn.

## Deck of Illusions

`srd51.item.deck-of-illusions` · Wondrous item · uncommon. [SRD p. 216, Deck of Illusions](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-216.md)

A complete deck has 34 parchment cards. One found as treasure usually lacks 1d20 − 1 cards. The magic requires a random draw. Spend an action to draw randomly and throw the card onto the ground within 30 feet. It produces an illusion over the card that looks and behaves like the corresponding creature or group at proper size, but cannot harm anything. While you can see an illusion and are within 120 feet of it, use an action to move it anywhere within 30 feet of its card.

Physical interaction reveals the illusion as matter passes through. A creature can also use an action to examine it, succeeding on DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) to recognize it and see it as translucent. Moving the card itself or dispelling the illusion ends it. Then the card's image disappears and that card cannot be used again.

For each suit the following lists correspond to ranks ace, king, queen, jack, ten, nine, eight, and two, in that order. Two jokers each depict the deck's owner.

- **Hearts:** red dragon, knight with four guards, succubus or incubus, druid, cloud giant, ettin, bugbear, goblin.
- **Diamonds:** beholder, archmage with a mage apprentice, night hag, assassin, fire giant, ogre mage, gnoll, kobold.
- **Spades:** lich, priest with two acolytes, medusa, veteran, frost giant, troll, hobgoblin, goblin.
- **Clubs:** iron golem, bandit captain with three bandits, erinyes, berserker, hill giant, ogre, orc, kobold.

[SRD p. 216, Deck of Illusions](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-216.md)

<a id="srd51-item-deck-of-many-things"></a>
## Deck of Many Things

`srd51.item.deck-of-many-things` · Wondrous item · legendary. [SRD p. 216, Deck of Many Things](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-216.md) · [SRD p. 217, Deck of Many Things continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-217.md) · [SRD p. 218, Deck of Many Things continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-218.md)

The deck has 13 cards with probability 75% or 22 otherwise, usually kept in a box or pouch. Before drawing, declare the number you intend to draw. Draw at random and resolve the magic immediately as each declared draw is made. Cards drawn beyond the declared number have no effect, except where the deck grants additional draws. Each card must follow the preceding draw by no more than one hour. If you fail to draw the declared number, the remaining declared cards leave the deck automatically and take effect simultaneously. A drawn card fades and then reappears in the deck, except Fool and Jester, which stay out.

The playing-card correspondences below allow use of an adapted ordinary deck. A dagger (†) marks cards included only in the 22-card version.

- **Balance (2♠)†.** Reverse the lawful/chaotic and good/evil aspects of your alignment. True neutral and unaligned creatures are unaffected.
- **Comet (2♦)†.** If you defeat the next hostile monster or group you encounter by yourself, gain enough XP to gain one level. Otherwise there is no effect.
- **Donjon (A♠)†.** You disappear into an extradimensional sphere, imprisoned in suspended animation, leaving worn and carried possessions where you stood. Divination cannot find you, but [Wish](spells-t-z.md#srd51-spell-wish) can reveal the prison's location. You remain until found and removed. You draw no more cards.
- **Euryale (Q♠).** A curse gives −2 to your saving throws. Only a god or The Fates card's magic can remove it.
- **The Fates (A♥)†.** Erase or avoid one event as if it never occurred. You can use this benefit immediately after drawing or later before death.
- **Flames (Q♣).** A powerful devil becomes your enemy and pursues your suffering and destruction until one of you dies.
- **Fool (joker marked TM)†.** Lose 10,000 XP, with the loss capped so you keep enough XP for your current level. Discard Fool and draw again. Both draws together count as one declared draw.
- **Gem (2♥)†.** Either 25 pieces of jewelry worth 2,000 gp each or 50 gems worth 1,000 gp each appear at your feet.
- **Idiot (2♣)†.** Permanently reduce Intelligence by 1d4 + 1, to a minimum of 1. You may draw one extra card.
- **Jester (joker without TM).** Choose to gain 10,000 XP or draw two extra cards.
- **Key (Q♥).** The GM chooses a magic weapon of at least rare rarity with which you are proficient. It appears in your hands.
- **Knight (J♥).** A 4th-level fighter of your ancestry appears in a space you choose within 30 feet. You control the fighter, who serves loyally until death.
- **Moon (Q♦).** Gain the ability to cast [Wish](spells-t-z.md#srd51-spell-wish) 1d3 times.
- **Rogue (J♠).** An NPC of the GM's choice becomes hostile. You do not know who it is until the NPC or another reveals it. Only a [Wish](spells-t-z.md#srd51-spell-wish) or divine intervention can end that hostility.
- **Ruin (K♠).** Lose all owned or carried wealth other than magic items. Portable assets vanish. Businesses, buildings, and land cease to be yours with the least change to reality, and documents that prove your entitlement disappear.
- **Skull (J♣).** An [avatar of death](#srd51-creature-avatar-of-death) appears in a GM-chosen space within 10 feet, attacks you, and warns others not to help. It disappears when you die or when it reaches 0 HP. Anyone attempting to help summons their own avatar. A creature killed by an avatar cannot be restored to life.
- **Star (J♦).** Increase one ability score by 2, allowing it to exceed 20 but not 24.
- **Sun (K♦).** Gain 50,000 XP and a randomly determined wondrous item, rolled by the GM, appears in your hands.
- **Talons (A♣)†.** Destroy all magic items you wear or carry. Artifacts in your possession vanish without being destroyed.
- **Throne (K♥).** Gain Persuasion proficiency and double your proficiency bonus on checks using it. You also gain title to a small keep, which must be cleared of its current monsters before you can claim it.
- **Vizier (A♦)†.** Once within one year, ask a question during meditation. Receive a truthful mental answer and the wisdom to apply it to the problem or dilemma.
- **The Void (K♣).** Your soul is trapped in an object at a GM-chosen place guarded by one or more powerful beings. Your body is incapacitated while your soul is trapped. [Wish](spells-t-z.md#srd51-spell-wish) reveals the object's location without returning the soul. You draw no more cards.

### Avatar of Death

`srd51.creature.avatar-of-death`

## Dimensional Shackles

`srd51.item.dimensional-shackles` · Wondrous item · rare. [SRD p. 219, Dimensional Shackles](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-219.md)

Use an action to put the shackles on an incapacitated Small, Medium, or Large creature. They adjust to fit and function as manacles, with the special procedures below. The bound creature cannot use extradimensional movement, including teleportation and planar travel, but can pass through an interdimensional portal. The user and creatures they designate when applying the shackles can spend an action to remove them. Once per 30 days, the bound creature can attempt a DC 30 Strength (Athletics) check. Success frees it and destroys the shackles.

**Project interpretation.** Use the special removal and breakout procedures instead of substituting ordinary manacle escape or breaking checks. The general manacle function describes the restraint, while the entry explicitly sets the allowed removers, the bound creature's check, and its frequency.

[SRD p. 219, Dimensional Shackles](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-219.md)

## Feather Token

`srd51.item.feather-token` · Wondrous item · rare. [SRD p. 221, Feather Token](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-221.md)

The feather-shaped token has a single-use effect. The GM chooses its type or rolls d100.

- **Anchor, 01–20.** Touch the token to a boat or ship as an action. For 24 hours nothing can move the vessel. Touching the token to the vessel again ends the effect early. The token disappears when the effect ends.
- **Bird, 21–35.** Activate the token with an action by tossing it upward 5 feet. It disappears and an enormous bird takes its place. Use roc statistics except it obeys your simple commands and cannot attack. It carries up to 500 lb at 16 mph and travels at most 144 miles per day at that speed, resting one hour for every three hours flown. It carries up to 1,000 lb at half that speed. It disappears after flying its maximum distance for a day, when reduced to 0 HP, or when you dismiss it as an action.
- **Fan, 36–50.** While aboard a boat or ship, use an action to toss the token up to 10 feet into the air. The token vanishes and a floating, flapping fan appears. Its wind fills one ship's sails, adding 5 mph for 8 hours. You can dismiss it as an action.
- **Swan Boat, 51–65.** Spend an action touching water at least 60 feet across. The token vanishes and a 50-by-20-foot self-propelled vessel appears. It moves at 6 mph. While aboard, use an action to command movement or a turn up to 90 degrees. Capacity is 32 Medium-or-smaller creatures, counting a Large creature as four and Huge as nine. It disappears after 24 hours or if dismissed with an action.
- **Tree, 66–90.** Outdoors, use an action to touch it to an empty ground space. It vanishes and a nonmagical oak grows 60 feet high, with a trunk 5 feet across and branches reaching 20 feet in radius.
- **Whip, 91–100 (00).** Replace the token with a floating whip by throwing it within 10 feet as an action. Its strike uses a bonus action, targets a creature no more than 10 feet from the whip, and resolves as a +9 melee spell attack for 1d6 + 5 force on a hit. On your turn a bonus action can move the whip up to 20 feet and perform that strike from the new position. Remove it after one hour, on your incapacity or death, or when you spend an action dismissing it.

[SRD p. 221, Feather Token](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-221.md)

## Figurine of Wondrous Power

`srd51.item.figurine-of-wondrous-power` · Wondrous item · rarity varies. [SRD p. 221, Figurine of Wondrous Power](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-221.md) · [SRD p. 222, Figurine of Wondrous Power continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-222.md) · [SRD p. 223, Figurine of Wondrous Power continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-223.md)

## Iron Flask

`srd51.item.iron-flask` · Wondrous item · legendary. [SRD p. 228, Iron Flask](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-228.md)

The iron flask has a brass stopper. Spend an action speaking its command word and target a creature you can see within 60 feet. If it is native to a plane other than your current plane, it makes a DC 17 Wisdom save or becomes trapped. It has advantage if the flask trapped it before. The flask holds one creature at a time. A captive stays until released, does not age, and needs no air, food, or drink.

Unstopper the flask as an action to release its captive. The creature is friendly to you and your companions and obeys you for 1 hour. If given no orders or orders likely to kill it, it defends itself and takes no other actions. After the hour it acts by its ordinary disposition and alignment.

[Identify](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-identify) reveals occupancy but the creature's kind can only be determined by opening the flask. A newly found flask might contain a creature selected by the GM or randomly determined on a d100.

- **No captive:** 01–50.

- **Demons and devils:** Demon types 1 through 6 on 51–54, 55–58, 59–62, 63–64, 65, and 66 respectively. Greater devil on 68–69 or lesser devil on 70–73.
- **Other fiends:** Night hag on 87–90 or succubus or incubus on 97–99.
- **Elemental creatures:** Djinni on 74–75, efreeti on 76–77, any elemental on 78–83, invisible stalker on 84–86, salamander on 92–95, or xorn on 100.

**Evidence gap.** The local source specifies demon types 1 through 6 and greater and lesser devils as table outcomes, but gives no mapping from those labels to creature entries in this table. ## Mirror of Life Trapping

`srd51.item.mirror-of-life-trapping` · Wondrous item · very rare. [SRD p. 230, Mirror of Life Trapping](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-230.md) · [SRD p. 231, Mirror of Life Trapping continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-231.md)

The 4-foot mirror weighs 50 lb, shows faint images when viewed indirectly, has AC 11 and 10 HP, and is vulnerable to bludgeoning. It shatters at 0 HP. While it hangs on a vertical surface and you are within 5 feet, use an action and a command word to activate it. It remains active until the command word is used again as an action.

A creature other than the activator that sees its reflection in the active mirror from within 30 feet makes a DC 15 Charisma save. A creature that knows the mirror's nature has advantage and a construct succeeds automatically. On failure trap the creature and everything it wears and carries in one of twelve extradimensional cells. Each cell is infinite, with fog limiting visibility to 10 feet. Captives do not age or need to eat, drink, or sleep. Planar travel magic can escape the cell, otherwise it confines the captive until freed.

If all cells are occupied when the mirror captures another creature, it frees one prisoner randomly. That prisoner appears in an unoccupied space in sight of the mirror, facing away. Shattering the mirror frees all prisoners into unoccupied spaces near it. While within 5 feet, use an action and speak a prisoner's name or cell number to show its image on the mirror and communicate normally. Using a second command word in the same manner releases a chosen captive with its possessions into the nearest unoccupied space, facing away from the mirror.

An Iron Flask captive needs neither air nor food, while a breathing creature placed in a Handy Haversack begins suffocating after ten minutes.
