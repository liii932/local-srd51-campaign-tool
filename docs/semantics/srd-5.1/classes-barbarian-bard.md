# Barbarian and Bard

Class-feature levels below are levels in the named class. Character-level
[proficiency bonus](abilities.md#srd51-core-proficiency-bonus), ability modifiers,
and general [advancement](character-advancement.md#srd51-character-advancement) rules supply shared inputs.

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian"></a>

## Barbarian

`srd51.class.barbarian`

Each barbarian level grants one d12 Hit Die. At character level 1 the maximum is 12 + Constitution modifier. Each later barbarian level adds 1d12 or a fixed 7, plus Constitution modifier. Starting proficiencies are light armor, medium armor, shields, simple weapons, martial weapons, Strength saves, and Constitution saves. There are no starting tool proficiencies. Choose two skills from Animal Handling, Athletics, Intimidation, Nature, Perception, and Survival. [SRD p. 8, Barbarian Class Features](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-008.md)

Add the class equipment to the background equipment. Take one greataxe or one martial melee weapon of your choice, plus two handaxes or one simple weapon of your choice, plus an explorer's pack and four javelins. [SRD p. 8, Barbarian Equipment](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-008.md)

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian-rage"></a>

### Rage

`srd51.class.barbarian.rage`

From level 1, spend a bonus action on your turn to enter a rage. For a raging barbarian without heavy armor, give advantage on Strength checks and Strength saves, apply resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage, and add rage damage to melee weapon attacks made using Strength. Rage damage is +2 at levels 1–8, +3 at 9–15, and +4 at 16–20. While raging, a character cannot cast spells or concentrate on them, including when heavy armor prevents the three benefits. [SRD p. 8, Rage and The Barbarian](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-008.md)

A rage lasts up to 1 minute. It ends early on being knocked unconscious, if a turn ends with neither an attack on a hostile creature nor damage taken since your last turn, or if you spend a bonus action on your turn to end it. The attack need not hit. [Persistent Rage](#srd51-class-barbarian-persistent-rage) changes the early termination rules. Available uses per long rest by class level are 2 at levels 1–2, 3 at 3–5, 4 at 6–11, 5 at 12–16, and 6 at 17–19. Level 20 has unlimited uses. [SRD p. 8, Rage and The Barbarian](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-008.md) [SRD p. 9, Persistent Rage](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-009.md)

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian-unarmored-defense"></a>

### Unarmored Defense

`srd51.class.barbarian.unarmored-defense`

From level 1, a barbarian wearing no armor can calculate AC as 10 + Dexterity modifier + Constitution modifier. A shield is compatible with this calculation. [SRD p. 8, Unarmored Defense](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-008.md)

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian-reckless-attack"></a>

### Reckless Attack

`srd51.class.barbarian.reckless-attack`

At level 2, you may choose to attack recklessly as you make the first attack of your turn. This gives advantage on your Strength-based melee weapon attack rolls during that turn. All attack rolls against you gain advantage until your next turn. [SRD p. 9, Reckless Attack](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-009.md)

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian-danger-sense"></a>

### Danger Sense

`srd51.class.barbarian.danger-sense`

At level 2, roll Dexterity saves against effects you can see with advantage, such as visible traps or spells. You receive this benefit only while free of the blinded, deafened, and incapacitated conditions. [SRD p. 9, Danger Sense](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-009.md)

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian-primal-path"></a>

### Primal Path

`srd51.class.barbarian.primal-path`

At level 3 choose a path. The path grants features at levels 3, 6, 10, and 14. **Evidence gap.** The source names Path of the Totem Warrior, whose feature definitions are absent from this SRD. [Path of the Berserker](#srd51-class-barbarian-path-of-the-berserker) is the locally specified choice. [SRD p. 9, Primal Path and Path of the Berserker](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-009.md) [SRD p. 10, Berserker continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-010.md)

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian-ability-score-improvement"></a>

### Ability Score Improvement

`srd51.class.barbarian.ability-score-improvement`

At levels 4, 8, 12, 16, and 19, apply an [Ability Score Improvement](character-advancement.md#srd51-character-ability-score-improvement). [SRD p. 9, Ability Score Improvement](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-009.md)

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian-extra-attack"></a>

### Extra Attack

`srd51.class.barbarian.extra-attack`

From level 5, take up to two attacks whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. [SRD p. 9, Extra Attack](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-009.md)

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian-fast-movement"></a>

### Fast Movement

`srd51.class.barbarian.fast-movement`

From level 5, increase speed by 10 feet whenever you are wearing no heavy armor. [SRD p. 9, Fast Movement](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-009.md)

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian-feral-instinct"></a>

### Feral Instinct

`srd51.class.barbarian.feral-instinct`

From level 7, roll initiative with advantage. If surprised at combat's start and not incapacitated, you can act normally on your first turn by entering rage before doing anything else on that turn. [SRD p. 9, Feral Instinct](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-009.md)

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian-brutal-critical"></a>

### Brutal Critical

`srd51.class.barbarian.brutal-critical`

On a critical hit with a melee attack, you may roll extra weapon damage dice in addition to the usual critical damage. The number of extra dice is one at levels 9–12, two at 13–16, and three at 17 or higher. [SRD p. 9, Brutal Critical](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-009.md)

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian-relentless-rage"></a>

### Relentless Rage

`srd51.class.barbarian.relentless-rage`

From level 11, dropping to 0 hit points while raging without dying outright lets you attempt a Constitution save. Success substitutes 1 hit point for 0. DC is 10 + 5 for each prior use since the last completed short or long rest. Completion of either rest resets the DC to 10. [SRD p. 9, Relentless Rage](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-009.md)

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian-persistent-rage"></a>

### Persistent Rage

`srd51.class.barbarian.persistent-rage`

From level 15, rage continues through inactivity. It can end before its 1-minute duration only if you fall unconscious or choose to end it. [SRD p. 8, Rage](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-008.md) [SRD p. 9, Persistent Rage](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-009.md)

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian-indomitable-might"></a>

### Indomitable Might

`srd51.class.barbarian.indomitable-might`

From level 18, you may substitute your Strength score for a Strength check total that is below that score. This applies to Strength checks. [SRD p. 9, Indomitable Might](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-009.md)

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian-primal-champion"></a>

### Primal Champion

`srd51.class.barbarian.primal-champion`

At level 20, Strength and Constitution each increase by 4 and each has a new maximum of 24. [SRD p. 9, Primal Champion](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-009.md)

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian-path-of-the-berserker"></a>

### Path of the Berserker

`srd51.class.barbarian.path-of-the-berserker`

This Primal Path provides Frenzy at 3, Mindless Rage at 6, Intimidating Presence at 10, and Retaliation at 14. [SRD p. 9, Path of the Berserker](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-009.md) [SRD p. 10, Berserker continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-010.md)

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian-frenzy"></a>

#### Frenzy

`srd51.class.barbarian.frenzy`

Starting at level 3, you can choose frenzy as you begin a rage. From your following turn through the rest of that rage, you can spend a bonus action each turn on one melee weapon attack. The rage’s end adds one [exhaustion](conditions.md#srd51-condition-exhaustion) level. [SRD p. 9, Frenzy](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-009.md)

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian-mindless-rage"></a>

#### Mindless Rage

`srd51.class.barbarian.mindless-rage`

From level 6, rage prevents the berserker from being charmed or frightened. If a charm or fright effect is already active when rage starts, suspend it for the rage's duration. [SRD p. 10, Mindless Rage](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-010.md)

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian-intimidating-presence"></a>

#### Intimidating Presence

`srd51.class.barbarian.intimidating-presence`

At level 10, your action can threaten one visible creature no farther than 30 feet away. A target that sees or hears you rolls a Wisdom save with DC 8 + proficiency bonus + Charisma modifier. Failure makes the target [frightened](conditions.md#srd51-condition-frightened) of you through the end of your following turn. On a later turn you can spend another action to renew that ongoing fright through the end of the following turn. Check the target’s position when its own turn ends. If it is beyond 60 feet or outside your line of sight, its fright ends then. A successful save bars you from using the feature on that creature for the next 24 hours. [SRD p. 10, Intimidating Presence](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-010.md)

<a id="srd51-class-barbarian-retaliation"></a>

#### Retaliation

`srd51.class.barbarian.retaliation`

From level 14, when you take damage from a creature within 5 feet of you, you can spend your reaction to make a melee weapon attack against it. [SRD p. 10, Retaliation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-010.md)

<a id="srd51-class-bard"></a>

## Bard

`srd51.class.bard`

Each bard level grants one d8 Hit Die. At character level 1 the maximum is 8 + Constitution modifier. Each later bard level adds 1d8 or a fixed 5, plus Constitution modifier. Starting proficiencies are light armor, simple weapons, hand crossbows, longswords, rapiers, shortswords, three chosen musical instruments, Dexterity saves, Charisma saves, and any three chosen skills. [SRD p. 11, Bard Class Features](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-011.md)

Add one rapier, longsword, or simple weapon, one diplomat's or entertainer's pack, one lute or other chosen musical instrument, leather armor, and one dagger to the background equipment. [SRD p. 11, Bard Equipment](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-011.md)

<a id="srd51-class-bard-spellcasting"></a>

### Spellcasting

`srd51.class.bard.spellcasting`

At level 1 choose two cantrips and four 1st-level spells from the [bard spell list](spell-lists.md#srd51-spell-list-bard). Learn another chosen bard cantrip at levels 4 and 10. Each leveled spell learned must be of a spell level for which the class has slots. On gaining a bard level, you may replace one known bard spell with another bard-list spell of a level for which the class has slots. The main known-spell count includes [Magical Secrets](#srd51-class-bard-magical-secrets) choices even when one is a cantrip. The [Additional Magical Secrets](#srd51-class-bard-additional-magical-secrets) choices are outside that count. [SRD p. 11, The Bard](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-011.md) [SRD p. 12, Cantrips and Spells Known of 1st Level and Higher](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-012.md) [SRD p. 13, Magical Secrets and Additional Magical Secrets](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-013.md)

Main spells-known totals by bard level 1 through 20 are 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15, 15, 16, 18, 19, 19, 20, 22, 22, 22. Use bard level as the input to the [full-caster slot progression](character-advancement.md#srd51-character-full-caster-slots). [SRD p. 11, The Bard](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-011.md)

Spend a [slot](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-slots) at least as high as the spell's level to cast a leveled spell with slots. A long rest restores all spent slots. Charisma is the spellcasting ability. Save DC is 8 + proficiency bonus + Charisma modifier, and spell attack modifier is proficiency bonus + Charisma modifier. A known bard spell with the ritual tag may be cast as a ritual. A musical instrument can serve as a focus for bard spells. [SRD p. 12, Spell Slots, Spellcasting Ability, Ritual Casting, and Spellcasting Focus](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-012.md)

<a id="srd51-class-bard-bardic-inspiration"></a>

### Bardic Inspiration

`srd51.class.bard.bardic-inspiration`

From level 1, use a bonus action on your turn to choose another creature within 60 feet that can hear you. It gains one Bardic Inspiration die, subject to a maximum of one at a time. In the next 10 minutes it may roll that die once and add the result to its attack roll, ability check, or save. It can decide after rolling the d20 and before the GM declares success or failure. The Inspiration die is consumed on rolling. [SRD p. 12, Bardic Inspiration](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-012.md)

Your uses equal your Charisma modifier, minimum one. Recover all spent uses after a long rest. From level 5, [Font of Inspiration](#srd51-class-bard-font-of-inspiration) adds short-rest recovery. Die size is d6 at levels 1–4, d8 at 5–9, d10 at 10–14, and d12 at 15–20. [SRD p. 12, Bardic Inspiration](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-012.md) [SRD p. 13, Font of Inspiration](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-013.md)

<a id="srd51-class-bard-jack-of-all-trades"></a>

### Jack of All Trades

`srd51.class.bard.jack-of-all-trades`

From level 2, you may add half your proficiency bonus, rounded down, to any ability check that does not already include your proficiency bonus. [SRD p. 12, Jack of All Trades](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-012.md)

<a id="srd51-class-bard-song-of-rest"></a>

### Song of Rest

`srd51.class.bard.song-of-rest`

From level 2, perform during a short rest. At its end, you and friendly creatures who hear the performance and regain hit points by spending at least one Hit Die each regain extra hit points once. The extra is d6 at levels 2–8, d8 at 9–12, d10 at 13–16, and d12 at 17–20. [SRD p. 12, Song of Rest](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-012.md)

<a id="srd51-class-bard-bard-college"></a>

### Bard College

`srd51.class.bard.bard-college`

Choose a college at level 3 for benefits at levels 3, 6, and 14. [College of Lore](#srd51-class-bard-college-of-lore) is the SRD's detailed college. [SRD p. 12, Bard College](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-012.md) [SRD p. 13, College of Lore](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-013.md) [SRD p. 14, Peerless Skill](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-014.md)

<a id="srd51-class-bard-expertise"></a>

### Expertise

`srd51.class.bard.expertise`

At level 3 choose two of your current skill proficiencies. Double proficiency bonus on ability checks using either choice. At level 10, choose two additional skill proficiencies to receive the same benefit. [SRD p. 13, Expertise](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-013.md)

<a id="srd51-class-bard-ability-score-improvement"></a>

### Ability Score Improvement

`srd51.class.bard.ability-score-improvement`

At levels 4, 8, 12, 16, and 19, apply an [Ability Score Improvement](character-advancement.md#srd51-character-ability-score-improvement). [SRD p. 13, Ability Score Improvement](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-013.md)

<a id="srd51-class-bard-font-of-inspiration"></a>

### Font of Inspiration

`srd51.class.bard.font-of-inspiration`

From level 5, completing a short or long rest restores all spent Bardic Inspiration uses. [SRD p. 13, Font of Inspiration](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-013.md)

<a id="srd51-class-bard-countercharm"></a>

### Countercharm

`srd51.class.bard.countercharm`

From level 6, spend an action to begin a performance through the end of your next turn. You and friendly creatures within 30 feet of you have advantage on saves against charm or fright while able to hear you. The performance ends early if you become incapacitated, are silenced, or choose to end it. Ending it by choice costs no action. [SRD p. 13, Countercharm](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-013.md)

<a id="srd51-class-bard-magical-secrets"></a>

### Magical Secrets

`srd51.class.bard.magical-secrets`

At each of levels 10, 14, and 18, learn two spells of your choice from any class, including bard. Each choice must be a cantrip or at a level you can cast according to the bard slot progression. The choices become bard spells for you and count toward the main spells-known total. [SRD p. 13, Magical Secrets](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-013.md)

<a id="srd51-class-bard-superior-inspiration"></a>

### Superior Inspiration

`srd51.class.bard.superior-inspiration`

From level 20, regain one Bardic Inspiration use upon rolling initiative with zero uses remaining. [SRD p. 13, Superior Inspiration](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-013.md)

<a id="srd51-class-bard-college-of-lore"></a>

### College of Lore

`srd51.class.bard.college-of-lore`

This college grants Bonus Proficiencies and Cutting Words at 3, Additional Magical Secrets at 6, and Peerless Skill at 14. [SRD p. 13, College of Lore](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-013.md) [SRD p. 14, Peerless Skill](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-014.md)

<a id="srd51-class-bard-bonus-proficiencies"></a>

#### Bonus Proficiencies

`srd51.class.bard.bonus-proficiencies`

On joining at level 3, gain proficiency in three chosen skills. [SRD p. 13, Bonus Proficiencies](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-013.md)

<a id="srd51-class-bard-cutting-words"></a>

#### Cutting Words

`srd51.class.bard.cutting-words`

From level 3, when a creature you can see within 60 feet rolls an attack, an ability check, or damage, you may spend a reaction and one use of Bardic Inspiration. Roll the Inspiration die and subtract the result from its roll. You may decide after seeing its roll, up to the GM's determination of success or failure for an attack or check, or before damage is dealt for a damage roll. A creature immune to being charmed or unable to hear you is immune to Cutting Words. [SRD p. 13, Cutting Words](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-013.md)

<a id="srd51-class-bard-additional-magical-secrets"></a>

#### Additional Magical Secrets

`srd51.class.bard.additional-magical-secrets`

At level 6, learn two chosen spells from any class. Each can be a cantrip or a spell of a level you can cast according to the bard slot progression. They become bard spells for you, separate from the main number of known bard spells. [SRD p. 13, Additional Magical Secrets](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-013.md)

<a id="srd51-class-bard-peerless-skill"></a>

#### Peerless Skill

`srd51.class.bard.peerless-skill`

From level 14, when making an ability check you may spend a Bardic Inspiration use, roll its die, and add the result. You can decide after rolling the check's die and before the GM announces the outcome. [SRD p. 14, Peerless Skill](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-014.md)

## Source-checked cases

- **Ordinary.** An 11th-level barbarian enters rage for the fourth time since a long rest and adds +3 to a Strength-based melee weapon damage roll without heavy armor. Another entry requires a long rest. [SRD p. 8, Rage and The Barbarian](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-008.md)
- **Boundary.** A 15th-level berserker's frenzy persists through a turn with neither an attack nor damage taken. The end of the rage still gives one exhaustion level, and the first turn of a frenzy still has no frenzy attack. [SRD p. 9, Persistent Rage and Frenzy](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-009.md)
- **Exception.** A 6th-level Lore bard can choose a 3rd-level spell from a different class and a cantrip through Additional Magical Secrets. Both become bard spells, and the nine main known spells are accounted for separately. [SRD p. 11, The Bard](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-011.md) [SRD p. 13, Additional Magical Secrets](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-013.md)
- **Invalid.** Cutting Words cannot reduce a saving throw. An eligible roll from a creature immune to being charmed is also unaffected. [SRD p. 13, Cutting Words](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-013.md)
