# Spells E–G

Each spell's casting line gives level and school, casting time, range, components, and duration in
that order. V, S, and M mean verbal, somatic, and material. A listed price requires the named
material, and materials are consumed only where specified. A ritual tag uses the general ritual
rules. Unmodified casting and resolution use the shared
[casting procedure](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-cast-spell),
[slots](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-slots),
[components](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-components),
[range](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-range),
[targets](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-targets),
[areas](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-area),
[spell saves](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-saving-throw),
[spell attack rolls](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-attack-roll),
[duration](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-duration), and
[concentration](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-concentration) rules.
HP means hit points, AC means Armor Class, and CR means challenge rating.
Find spell names and class access lists in the [spell index](spell-index.md).

<a id="srd51-spell-eyebite"></a>

## Eyebite

`srd51.spell.eyebite`

Level 6 necromancy · 1 action · Self · V, S · Concentration up to 1 minute.

While active, the spell gives your eyes an ink-dark appearance. At casting choose one creature you can
see within 60 feet. It makes a Wisdom save and suffers one mode you choose on failure. On each of
your turns while the spell lasts, you can spend an action to choose another creature by the
same criteria. A separate available action on the casting turn can be used for this. Any creature that has
succeeded on a save against this casting is ineligible for retargeting.

- **Asleep.** The target is unconscious for the duration unless it takes damage or another creature
  spends an action shaking it awake.
- **Panicked.** The target becomes frightened, with you as the source. On its own turn it must
  Dash away by the safest and shortest available route unless it has nowhere to go. This effect ends if it reaches a place at
  least 60 feet away where it cannot see you.
- **Sickened.** The target has disadvantage on attacks and ability checks. It may make another Wisdom
  save at the end of each of its own turns, ending the effect on success.

[SRD p. 141, Eyebite](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-141.md)

## Fabricate

`srd51.spell.fabricate`

Level 4 transmutation · 10 minutes · 120 feet · V, S · Instantaneous.

Turn visible raw materials in range into a product of the same material. A sufficient supply yields an
object of Large size or smaller that fits a 10-foot cube or eight connected 5-foot cubes. A product of
metal, stone, or another mineral is limited to Medium size and one 5-foot cube. Quality follows the
quality of the inputs. Neither creatures nor magic items can be created or transmuted. Producing an
object that usually requires advanced craftsmanship, such as jewelry, glass, armor, or a weapon,
requires proficiency in the relevant artisan's tools.

[SRD p. 141, Fabricate](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-141.md)

## Find Familiar

`srd51.spell.find-familiar`

Level 1 conjuration (ritual) · 1 hour · 10 feet · V, S, M (charcoal, incense, and herbs worth 10 gp total, consumed by fire in a brass brazier) · Instantaneous.

A spirit appears in an unoccupied space in range. Choose its form from
[bat](creatures-misc-a-g.md#srd51-creature-bat), [cat](creatures-misc-a-g.md#srd51-creature-cat),
[crab](creatures-misc-a-g.md#srd51-creature-crab), [frog](creatures-misc-a-g.md#srd51-creature-frog)
(toad), [hawk](creatures-misc-h-z.md#srd51-creature-hawk),
[lizard](creatures-misc-h-z.md#srd51-creature-lizard),
[octopus](creatures-misc-h-z.md#srd51-creature-octopus),
[owl](creatures-misc-h-z.md#srd51-creature-owl), [poisonous
snake](creatures-misc-h-z.md#srd51-creature-poisonous-snake),
[quipper](creatures-misc-h-z.md#srd51-creature-quipper) (fish),
[rat](creatures-misc-h-z.md#srd51-creature-rat), [raven](creatures-misc-h-z.md#srd51-creature-raven),
[sea horse](creatures-misc-h-z.md#srd51-creature-sea-horse),
[spider](creatures-misc-h-z.md#srd51-creature-spider), or
[weasel](creatures-misc-h-z.md#srd51-creature-weasel). It uses that form's statistics except you
designate its type as celestial, fey, or fiend in place of beast. It acts independently and obeys your
commands. It rolls its own initiative and gets its own combat turns. It may take actions normally
except it cannot attack.

- **Link.** While within 100 feet of each other, you can communicate telepathically. You can use an
  action to see and hear through it, gaining its special senses and being blind and deaf through your
  own senses until the start of your next turn.
- **Deliver touch.** When you cast a spell with a range of touch and the familiar is within 100 feet,
  it can use its reaction at casting to deliver the spell as if it cast it. Use your attack modifier
  if the spell needs an attack roll.
- **Dismiss or call.** As an action dismiss it temporarily into a pocket dimension. A later action can
  call it back into an unoccupied space within 30 feet. You can instead dismiss it forever.
- **Lose or change form.** At 0 HP it disappears with no body and reappears after a new casting. You
  can have only one familiar. Recasting while you already have one changes that familiar into another
  eligible form you choose.

## Find Steed

`srd51.spell.find-steed`

Level 2 conjuration · 10 minutes · 30 feet · V, S · Instantaneous.

A loyal spirit appears in an unoccupied space in range. Choose a
[warhorse](creatures-misc-h-z.md#srd51-creature-warhorse),
[pony](creatures-misc-h-z.md#srd51-creature-pony),

telepathically. The steed disappears without remains at 0 HP or when you dismiss it as an action.

this spell at a time. As an action you may release the bond, causing it to disappear.

## Find Traps

`srd51.spell.find-traps`

Level 2 divination · 1 action · 120 feet · V, S · Instantaneous.

## Flesh to Stone

`srd51.spell.flesh-to-stone`

Level 6 transmutation · 1 action · 60 feet · V, S, M (a pinch of lime, water, and earth) · Concentration up to 1 minute.

Choose a creature you can see in range. A flesh-bodied target makes a Constitution save and is
restrained on failure. Success leaves it unaffected. While restrained by the spell it makes another
Constitution save at the end of each of its turns. Track successes and failures separately without
requiring a streak. Three successes end the spell. Three failures petrify the target for the duration.
If physical breakage damages its petrified body, comparable deformities appear if it returns to its
original form. Maintaining concentration for the full duration makes the petrification persist until
removed.

**Project interpretation.** Begin the three-outcome counters with the repeat saves. The first failure
gates entry into the restraint phase, while the counting procedure is introduced for the creature that
is already restrained. Counting the initial failure too would shorten the repeat phase by one possible
failure.

## Forcecage

`srd51.spell.forcecage`

Level 7 evocation · 1 action · 100 feet · V, S, M (ruby dust worth 1,500 gp) · 1 hour.

## Freezing Sphere

`srd51.spell.freezing-sphere`

Level 6 evocation · 1 action · 300 feet · V, S, M (a small crystal sphere) · Instantaneous.

Fire a globe at a point in range. Its explosion fills a 60-foot-radius sphere. Each creature inside
makes a Constitution save against 10d6 cold damage, taking half on success. A hit on water or a liquid
composed principally of water freezes a 30-foot square to a depth of 6 inches for 1 minute.
Water-based creatures are excluded from this freezing rule. Surface swimmers in the frozen patch are
trapped. Breaking out costs the trapped creature an action and requires a successful Strength check. The
DC is your spell save DC.

creature you give it to can throw it up to 40 feet, hurl it at a sling's normal range, or set it down

**Project interpretation.** Throwing or hurling the held globe in combat costs an action.
The source gives a later-use permission but no reaction trigger, special bonus action, or
attack roll. Treat it as deliberate use of the held object and resolve the explosion on
impact. A [sling](equipment.md#srd51-equipment-sling) supplies its normal range, without adding
a weapon damage roll. Setting the globe down uses the ordinary object-interaction rule.

## Guards and Wards

`srd51.spell.guards-and-wards`

Level 6 abjuration · 10 minutes · Touch · V, S, M (burning incense, a small measure of brimstone and oil, knotted string, a small amount of umber hulk blood, and a small silver rod worth at least 10 gp) · 24 hours.

Ward up to 2,500 square feet of floor to a height of up to 20 feet in an arrangement you choose. You

  You may disguise up to ten doors as plain wall with the illusory-object effect of [Minor
  Illusion](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-minor-illusion).
- **Stairs.** [Web](spells-t-z.md#srd51-spell-web) strands fill each stair from top to bottom. Burnt
  or torn strands grow back after 10 minutes while the ward persists.
- **Additional effect.** Choose [Dancing Lights](spells-d-e.md#srd51-spell-dancing-lights) in four
  corridors, optionally giving them a simple repeating program. Or choose [Magic
  Mouth](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-magic-mouth) in two locations, [Stinking
  Cloud](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-stinking-cloud) in two locations, continuous [Gust of
  Wind](#srd51-spell-gust-of-wind) in one room or corridor, or
  [Suggestion](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-suggestion) in an area up to 5 feet square. Stinking Cloud

against a specific effect removes just that effect. Daily castings in the structure for a year make

**Project interpretation.** The ward maintains its placed features for its own lifetime.
Those features are components of one casting, so use their referenced behaviors without
starting separate casts, timers, or concentration for the fog, door locks and disguises,
webs, lights, mouths, clouds, and gust. Preserve any specific recovery cadence given above.
Suggestion affects each entrant separately for up to its usual 8 hours without independent
concentration, ending sooner on its own triggers or when its ward component ends. Applying
each referenced spell's standalone concentration and short duration to the installed
features would prevent the multiple ongoing effects the ward expressly creates.

## Gust of Wind

`srd51.spell.gust-of-wind`

Level 2 evocation · 1 action · Self (60-foot line) · V, S, M (a legume seed) · Concentration up to 1 minute.

Project a strong wind 60 feet long and 10 feet wide from yourself in a chosen direction. A creature
starting its turn in the line makes a Strength save. Failure pushes it 15 feet away from you along the
line. Within the line, each foot of movement toward you costs 2 feet. The gust disperses gas and
vapor, extinguishes exposed flames such as candles and torches, and makes protected flames flicker
with a 50 percent chance of putting them out. On each of your turns you can spend a bonus action to

**Project interpretation.** Roll the 50 percent chance once per protected flame per
continuous exposure, including when the line first appears around it. It flickers while
exposed if it survives. A flame leaving and later entering begins another exposure. The
source gives start-of-turn timing to creatures but no periodic reroll for protected flames,
so a lantern remaining in the line does not reroll each turn.
