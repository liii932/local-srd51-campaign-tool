# Warlock and Wizard

Feature acquisition uses levels in the class that grants the feature.
[Proficiency bonus](abilities.md#srd51-core-proficiency-bonus) uses total character level.

<a id="srd51-class-warlock"></a>

## Warlock

`srd51.class.warlock`

Each warlock level grants one d8 Hit Die. At character level 1, maximum hit points are 8 + Constitution modifier. Each later warlock level adds 1d8 or a fixed 5, plus Constitution modifier. Starting proficiencies are light armor, simple weapons, Wisdom saves, and Charisma saves. There are no starting tool proficiencies. Choose two skills from Arcana, Deception, History, Intimidation, Investigation, Nature, and Religion. [SRD p. 46, Warlock Class Features](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-046.md)

In addition to background equipment, choose a light crossbow with 20 bolts or any simple weapon, a component pouch or an arcane focus, and a scholar's pack or a dungeoneer's pack. Also receive leather armor, one simple weapon of your choice, and two daggers. [SRD p. 46, Warlock Equipment](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-046.md)

<a id="srd51-class-warlock-pact-magic"></a>

### Pact Magic

`srd51.class.warlock.pact-magic`

At level 1 learn two chosen cantrips and two chosen 1st-level spells from the [warlock spell list](spell-lists.md#srd51-spell-list-warlock). Add one chosen warlock cantrip at levels 4 and 10. The leveled spell totals from warlock level 1 through 20 are 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 10, 11, 11, 12, 12, 13, 13, 14, 14, 15, 15. Choose each new leveled spell from the warlock list up to your Pact Magic slot level. On gaining a warlock level, you may replace one known warlock spell with another from the warlock list subject to the same spell level eligibility. [SRD p. 46, The Warlock and Pact Magic](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-046.md) [SRD p. 47, Cantrips continuation and Spells Known of 1st Level and Higher](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-047.md)

All Pact Magic slots have the same level. Slot count is one at warlock level 1, two at 2–10, three at 11–16, and four at 17–20. Slot level is 1st at warlock levels 1–2, 2nd at 3–4, 3rd at 5–6, 4th at 7–8, and 5th from level 9. To cast a leveled warlock spell with Pact Magic, expend one slot and cast at that slot's level. Complete a short or long rest to recover all expended Pact Magic slots. [Multiclass spellcasting](character-advancement.md#srd51-character-multiclass-spellcasting) allows additional ways to spend them. [SRD p. 46, The Warlock](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-046.md) [SRD p. 47, Spell Slots](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-047.md)

Charisma is the spellcasting ability. Save DC is 8 + proficiency bonus + Charisma modifier. Spell attack modifier is proficiency bonus + Charisma modifier. An arcane focus can serve as a focus for warlock spells. [SRD p. 47, Spellcasting Ability and Spellcasting Focus](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-047.md)

<a id="srd51-class-warlock-eldritch-invocations"></a>

### Eldritch Invocations

`srd51.class.warlock.eldritch-invocations`

At level 2 learn two chosen invocations. Gain another at each of levels 5, 7, 9, 12, 15, and 18, for a total of eight. To learn an invocation, meet its prerequisites, which can be gained at the same level. A level prerequisite always refers to warlock level. On gaining a warlock level, you may replace one known invocation with another you qualify for at that level. [SRD p. 46, The Warlock](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-046.md) [SRD p. 47, Eldritch Invocations](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-047.md) [SRD p. 48, Eldritch Invocations prerequisites](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-048.md)

An invocation that allows a spell to be cast uses that spell's normal casting rules except for the changes stated by the invocation. A use limit and a spell slot cost both apply where both are listed. [SRD p. 48, Eldritch Invocations](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-048.md) [SRD p. 49, Eldritch Invocations continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-049.md) [SRD p. 50, Eldritch Invocations continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-050.md)

<a id="srd51-class-warlock-pact-of-the-chain"></a>

#### Pact of the Chain

`srd51.class.warlock.pact-of-the-chain`

Learn [Find Familiar](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-find-familiar) outside your normal spells-known allowance and gain permission to cast it as a ritual. A casting may use a normal familiar form or an [imp](creatures-a-d.md#srd51-creature-imp), [pseudodragon](creatures-m-z.md#srd51-creature-pseudodragon), [quasit](creatures-a-d.md#srd51-creature-quasit), or [sprite](creatures-m-z.md#srd51-creature-sprite). When taking the Attack action, you can give up one of your attacks to let the familiar make one attack using its reaction. [SRD p. 47, Pact of the Chain](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-047.md)

<a id="srd51-class-warlock-pact-of-the-blade"></a>

#### Pact of the Blade

`srd51.class.warlock.pact-of-the-blade`

Use an action to create a pact weapon in your empty hand. Choose its melee weapon form on each creation unless summoning a bonded magic weapon. You have proficiency with it while wielding it. It counts as magical when overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage. It disappears if more than 5 feet from you for at least 1 minute, if you create another with the feature, if you dismiss it without an action, or if you die. [SRD p. 47, Pact of the Blade](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-047.md)

You can bond one magic weapon by holding it throughout a 1-hour ritual, which can be done during a short rest. Artifacts and sentient weapons cannot be bonded. Once bonded, you can dismiss it into an extradimensional space and it reappears when you next create your pact weapon. The bond ends when you die, bond a different weapon through the 1-hour ritual, or perform a 1-hour ritual to end the bond. If the weapon is in the extradimensional space when its bond ends, it appears at your feet. [SRD p. 47, Pact of the Blade](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-047.md) [SRD p. 48, Pact of the Blade continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-048.md)

<a id="srd51-class-warlock-pact-of-the-tome"></a>

#### Pact of the Tome

`srd51.class.warlock.pact-of-the-tome`

Your patron grants a Book of Shadows. On gaining it, choose three cantrips from any class lists, using the same or different lists. You can cast them at will while the book is on your person. They count as warlock spells for you and are outside your ordinary cantrips-known limit. If you lose the book, a 1-hour ceremony obtains a replacement from your patron and destroys the previous one. You may perform the ceremony during a short or long rest. The book turns to ash upon your death. [SRD p. 48, Pact of the Tome](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-048.md)

#### Book of Ancient Secrets

`srd51.class.warlock.book-of-ancient-secrets`

**Prerequisite.** Pact of the Tome.

Choose two 1st-level spells with the ritual tag from any class spell lists. They may come from different lists. They appear in your Book of Shadows and do not count toward your known spell total. While holding the book, you may cast these spells as rituals. You can cast them only as rituals unless you have also learned them through another source. This invocation also enables ritual casting for your other known warlock spells bearing that tag.

You may transcribe a ritual spell you find into the book if its spell level is at most ceil(warlock level ÷ 2). Each level of the spell costs 2 hours and 50 gp of rare inks to transcribe. Once recorded, use the same ritual casting rules as for the initial inscriptions. [SRD p. 49, Book of Ancient Secrets continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-049.md) [SRD p. 48, Book of Ancient Secrets](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-048.md)

`srd51.class.warlock.voice-of-the-chain-master`

While perceiving this way, you can also speak through it with your own voice even if it cannot normally speak. [SRD p. 50, Voice of the Chain Master](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-050.md)

The invocation changes reach and grants speech through the familiar but gives no alternative timing; treating it as continuous sense access would replace that timing without an explicit instruction. The separate range and reaction for delivering touch spells are unaffected. [SRD p. 50, Voice of the Chain Master](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-050.md) [SRD p. 143, Find Familiar](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-143.md)

<a id="srd51-class-warlock-the-fiend"></a>

### The Fiend

`srd51.class.warlock.the-fiend`

A fiend patron is a powerful lower-plane inhabitant. It grants an Expanded Spell List and Dark One's Blessing at level 1, Dark One's Own Luck at 6, Fiendish Resilience at 10, and Hurl Through Hell at 14. A patron's nature can inform the appearance of pact gifts and the familiar's chosen form. Imp and quasit are forms associated with the Fiend. [SRD p. 50, The Fiend](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-050.md) [SRD p. 51, The Fiend continuation and Your Pact Boon](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-051.md)

- 2nd level — [Blindness/Deafness](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-blindness-deafness) and [Scorching Ray](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-scorching-ray).
- 3rd level — [Fireball](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-fireball) and [Stinking Cloud](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-stinking-cloud).
- 4th level — [Fire Shield](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-fire-shield) and [Wall of Fire](spells-t-z.md#srd51-spell-wall-of-fire).
- 5th level — [Flame Strike](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-flame-strike) and [Hallow](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-hallow).

<a id="srd51-class-warlock-dark-ones-blessing"></a>

#### Dark One's Blessing

`srd51.class.warlock.dark-ones-blessing`

From level 1, when you reduce a hostile creature to 0 hit points, gain temporary hit points equal to Charisma modifier + warlock level, minimum 1. [SRD p. 50, Dark One's Blessing](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-050.md)

<a id="srd51-class-warlock-dark-ones-own-luck"></a>

#### Dark One's Own Luck

`srd51.class.warlock.dark-ones-own-luck`

From level 6, on one ability check or saving throw you may roll a d10 and add it to your roll. You may decide after seeing the initial roll and before its effects occur. Finish a short or long rest before using this again. [SRD p. 50, Dark One's Own Luck](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-050.md) [SRD p. 51, Dark One's Own Luck continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-051.md)

<a id="srd51-class-warlock-fiendish-resilience"></a>

#### Fiendish Resilience

`srd51.class.warlock.fiendish-resilience`

From level 10, on finishing a short or long rest you may choose a damage type. Gain resistance to that type until you choose another type using this feature. Damage from magical weapons or silver weapons bypasses this resistance. [SRD p. 51, Fiendish Resilience](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-051.md)

<a id="srd51-class-warlock-hurl-through-hell"></a>

#### Hurl Through Hell

`srd51.class.warlock.hurl-through-hell`

From level 14, when you hit a creature with an attack, you can immediately transport it through the lower planes. The target disappears until the end of your next turn. It then returns to its former space, or the nearest unoccupied space if necessary. A target other than a fiend takes 10d10 psychic damage on return. Finish a long rest before another use. [SRD p. 51, Hurl Through Hell](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-051.md)

<a id="srd51-class-wizard"></a>

## Wizard

`srd51.class.wizard`

Each wizard level grants one d6 Hit Die. At character level 1, maximum hit points are 6 + Constitution modifier. Each later wizard level adds 1d6 or a fixed 4, plus Constitution modifier. Starting proficiencies are daggers, darts, slings, quarterstaffs, light crossbows, Intelligence saves, and Wisdom saves. There are no starting armor or tool proficiencies. Choose two skills from Arcana, History, Insight, Investigation, Medicine, and Religion. [SRD p. 52, Wizard Class Features](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-052.md)

In addition to background equipment, choose a quarterstaff or dagger, a component pouch or an arcane focus, and a scholar's pack or an explorer's pack. Also receive a spellbook. [SRD p. 52, Wizard Equipment](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-052.md)

<a id="srd51-class-wizard-spellcasting"></a>

### Spellcasting

`srd51.class.wizard.spellcasting`

At level 1 choose three cantrips from the [wizard spell list](spell-lists.md#srd51-spell-list-wizard). Add one chosen wizard cantrip each at levels 4 and 10. Wizard cantrips are known directly. Other wizard spells learned through Spellcasting are recorded in the [spellbook](#srd51-class-wizard-spellbook). Use wizard level as input to the [full-caster slot progression](character-advancement.md#srd51-character-full-caster-slots). [SRD p. 52, The Wizard, Cantrips, and Spellbook](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-052.md) [SRD p. 53, Preparing and Casting Spells](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-053.md)

Choose a prepared list from your book with wizard level + Intelligence modifier spells, minimum one. Each must be of a level for which you have slots. To cast a prepared leveled spell with a slot, spend one at least as high as the spell. Casting does not remove it from your prepared list. Completing a long rest restores all expended slots and allows you to change your prepared list. A changed list requires at least 1 minute of study per spell level for each spell on that list. [SRD p. 53, Preparing and Casting Spells](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-053.md)

Intelligence is the spellcasting ability. Save DC is 8 + proficiency bonus + Intelligence modifier. Spell attack modifier is proficiency bonus + Intelligence modifier. Use an arcane focus as a focus for wizard spells. You may cast a wizard spell as a ritual if it has the ritual tag and is in your spellbook. It may be unprepared. [SRD p. 53, Spellcasting Ability, Ritual Casting, and Spellcasting Focus](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-053.md)

<a id="srd51-class-wizard-spellbook"></a>

#### Spellbook

`srd51.class.wizard.spellbook`

At level 1, the book contains six chosen 1st-level wizard spells. Each subsequent wizard level lets you add two chosen wizard spells to the book at no cost. For these choices, spell level cannot exceed the levels available in the wizard class's slot progression. The book also grows through discovered spells. [SRD p. 52, Spellbook](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-052.md) [SRD p. 53, Learning Spells of 1st Level and Higher](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-053.md)

- **Found spell.** A found wizard spell of 1st level or higher is eligible if it is of a level you can prepare. Decipher and practice it, then record it in your own notation. Spend 2 hours and 50 gp per spell level on components and inks. When done, you can prepare it under the usual rules. A spell found on a [Spell Scroll](magic-items-s-w.md#srd51-item-spell-scroll) has a further DC 10 + spell level Intelligence (Arcana) check, and the scroll is destroyed on success or failure. [SRD p. 54, Copying a Spell into the Book](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-054.md) [SRD p. 55, Copying a Spell into the Book continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-055.md) [SRD p. 242, Spell Scroll](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-242.md)
- **Copy and backup.** Copy a spell from your own book into another book for 1 hour and 10 gp per spell level because you already know the notation. [SRD p. 55, Replacing the Book](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-055.md)
- **Lost book.** Use the same 1-hour and 10-gp-per-level procedure to reconstruct the spells currently prepared. Recover other entries from a backup or by finding the spells again. [SRD p. 55, Replacing the Book](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-055.md)

<a id="srd51-class-wizard-arcane-recovery"></a>

### Arcane Recovery

`srd51.class.wizard.arcane-recovery`

From level 1, once per day when you finish a short rest, you may recover selected expended spell slots by studying your spellbook. Their combined spell levels cannot exceed ceil(wizard level ÷ 2), and each slot must be below 6th level. [SRD p. 52, The Wizard](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-052.md) [SRD p. 53, Arcane Recovery](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-053.md)

**Project interpretation.** Apply the shared [day boundary](exploration.md#srd51-core-day-boundary) for one use per in-world calendar day. Completing a long rest during that same day does not restore eligibility. [SRD p. 53, Arcane Recovery](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-053.md) [SRD p. 84, Time](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-084.md) [SRD p. 259, Limited Usage](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-259.md)

<a id="srd51-class-wizard-arcane-tradition"></a>

### Arcane Tradition

`srd51.class.wizard.arcane-tradition`

At level 2 choose an arcane tradition based on a school of magic. It grants features at 2, 6, 10, and 14. The eight schools categorize spells. The local SRD provides the subclass feature definitions for [School of Evocation](#srd51-class-wizard-school-of-evocation). [SRD p. 53, Arcane Tradition](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-053.md) [SRD p. 54, Arcane Traditions and School of Evocation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-054.md)

<a id="srd51-class-wizard-ability-score-improvement"></a>

### Ability Score Improvement

`srd51.class.wizard.ability-score-improvement`

At levels 4, 8, 12, 16, and 19, apply an [Ability Score Improvement](character-advancement.md#srd51-character-ability-score-improvement). [SRD p. 53, Ability Score Improvement](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-053.md)

<a id="srd51-class-wizard-spell-mastery"></a>

### Spell Mastery

`srd51.class.wizard.spell-mastery`

At level 18 choose one 1st-level and one 2nd-level wizard spell in your spellbook. While prepared, either may be cast at its lowest level without a slot as often as you can otherwise cast it. Casting at a higher level uses a slot as normal. After 8 hours of study, you may replace either or both choices with wizard spells of the matching levels. [SRD p. 53, Spell Mastery](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-053.md)

<a id="srd51-class-wizard-signature-spells"></a>

### Signature Spells

`srd51.class.wizard.signature-spells`

At level 20 choose two 3rd-level wizard spells in your spellbook. They are always prepared and take no place in your normal preparation allowance. Each can be cast once at 3rd level without spending a slot. A short or long rest restores those separate uses. Higher-level casting uses a slot normally. [SRD p. 54, Signature Spells](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-054.md)

<a id="srd51-class-wizard-school-of-evocation"></a>

### School of Evocation

`srd51.class.wizard.school-of-evocation`

Choosing this school at level 2 grants Evocation Savant and Sculpt Spells. Gain Potent Cantrip at 6, Empowered Evocation at 10, and Overchannel at 14. [SRD p. 54, School of Evocation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-054.md)

<a id="srd51-class-wizard-evocation-savant"></a>

#### Evocation Savant

`srd51.class.wizard.evocation-savant`

From level 2, copying an evocation spell into your spellbook takes half the otherwise required time and gold. [SRD p. 54, Evocation Savant](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-054.md)

<a id="srd51-class-wizard-sculpt-spells"></a>

#### Sculpt Spells

`srd51.class.wizard.sculpt-spells`

From level 2, when you cast an evocation spell that affects other creatures you can see, you may choose a number of those creatures equal to 1 + the spell's level. The selected creatures automatically succeed on their saves against that spell and take no damage if a successful save would ordinarily still deal half damage. [SRD p. 54, Sculpt Spells](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-054.md)

<a id="srd51-class-wizard-potent-cantrip"></a>

#### Potent Cantrip

`srd51.class.wizard.potent-cantrip`

From level 6, a creature succeeding on a saving throw against one of your cantrips takes half the cantrip's damage, if it deals any, and suffers no additional effect from it. [SRD p. 54, Potent Cantrip](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-054.md)

<a id="srd51-class-wizard-empowered-evocation"></a>

#### Empowered Evocation

`srd51.class.wizard.empowered-evocation`

From level 10, you may add your Intelligence modifier to one damage roll of a wizard evocation spell you cast. [SRD p. 54, Empowered Evocation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-054.md)

<a id="srd51-class-wizard-overchannel"></a>

#### Overchannel

`srd51.class.wizard.overchannel`

From level 14, when casting a damaging wizard spell of 1st through 5th level, you may deal its maximum damage. The first use has no adverse effect. For the second use between long rests, take 2d12 necrotic damage per spell level immediately after casting. Each subsequent use before the next long rest increases that damage by another d12 per spell level. This damage ignores resistance and immunity. [SRD p. 54, Overchannel](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-054.md)

## Source-checked cases

- **Ordinary.** A level 5 warlock has two 3rd-level Pact Magic slots. Spending one to cast a known 1st-level spell casts it at 3rd level. A completed short rest restores the spent slot. [SRD p. 46, The Warlock](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-046.md) [SRD p. 47, Spell Slots](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-047.md)
- **Boundary.** A level 9 Pact of the Tome warlock with Book of Ancient Secrets can transcribe a 5th-level ritual spell in 10 hours for 250 gp. A 6th-level ritual is above the cap. [SRD p. 48, Book of Ancient Secrets](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-048.md) [SRD p. 49, Book of Ancient Secrets continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-049.md)
- **Exception.** A wizard can ritual-cast a ritual-tagged wizard spell in the spellbook without preparing it. Spell Mastery instead requires the chosen spell to be prepared for slotless casting. [SRD p. 53, Ritual Casting and Spell Mastery](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-053.md)
- **Interpretation check.** After using Arcane Recovery, a wizard who finishes a long rest on the same calendar day and later finishes a short rest still has no Arcane Recovery use. By contrast, a druid’s [Natural Recovery](classes-cleric-druid.md#srd51-class-druid-natural-recovery) becomes available after the long rest. [SRD p. 21, Natural Recovery](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-021.md) [SRD p. 53, Arcane Recovery](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-053.md)
- **Invalid.** A sentient magic weapon cannot be bonded as a Pact of the Blade weapon. A wizard cannot apply Overchannel to a damaging cantrip because its permitted spell levels start at 1st. [SRD p. 47, Pact of the Blade](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-047.md) [SRD p. 54, Overchannel](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-054.md)
