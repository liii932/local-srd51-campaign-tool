# Creatures from Drider through Manticore

Use the [stat-block rules](creatures.md#srd51-creature-rules-stat-block) for the profiles. The first listed damage number is the source's average, and its parenthesized dice can be rolled instead. Bonuses on weapon and spell attacks are attack-roll modifiers. Apply the common rules for [attacks](creatures.md#srd51-creature-rules-attack), [innate casting](creatures.md#srd51-creature-rules-innate-spellcasting), [spellcasting](creatures.md#srd51-creature-rules-spellcasting), [recharge and per-day uses](creatures.md#srd51-creature-rules-limited-usage), [grapples](creatures.md#srd51-creature-rules-grapple), and [legendary actions](creatures.md#srd51-creature-rules-legendary-actions). Multiattack and legendary options retain each component's listed targeting and effect. Profile-specific limits take precedence. [SRD pp. 254–260, Monster Statistics](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-254.md) · [SRD p. 259, Actions, Limited Usage, and Grapple Rules for Monsters](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-259.md) · [SRD p. 260, Legendary Actions](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-260.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-dryad-speak-with-beasts-and-plants"></a> **Trait — Speak with Beasts and Plants.** `srd51.creature.dryad.speak-with-beasts-and-plants` Communicate with beasts and plants as with a shared language. [SRD p. 305, Dryad, Speak with Beasts and Plants](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-305.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-dryad-tree-stride"></a> **Trait — Tree Stride.** `srd51.creature.dryad.tree-stride` Once on its turn, spend 10 feet of movement to enter a reachable living tree and emerge into an empty space within 5 feet of another living tree within 60 feet of the first. Both trees must be Large or bigger. [SRD p. 305, Dryad, Tree Stride](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-305.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-dryad-club"></a> **Action — Club.** `srd51.creature.dryad.club` Melee weapon, reach 5 feet, one target. Normally +2 to hit and 2 (1d4) bludgeoning on hit. With [Shillelagh](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-shillelagh), use +6 to hit and deal 8 (1d8 + 4) bludgeoning. [SRD p. 305, Dryad, Club](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-305.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-dryad-fey-charm"></a> **Action — Fey Charm.** `srd51.creature.dryad.fey-charm` Choose one humanoid or beast the dryad can see within 30 feet. If it can see the dryad, it makes a DC 14 Wisdom save or is magically charmed. It treats the dryad as a trusted friend to heed and protect and interprets its actions and requests favorably. The dryad does not control it. The target can repeat the save after harm by the dryad or an ally. End the effect on success, after 24 hours, when the dryad dies or changes planes relative to the target, or if the dryad spends a bonus action ending it. A successful save gives immunity to this dryad's Fey Charm for 24 hours. Up to one humanoid and three beasts can be charmed by this dryad at once. **Project interpretation.** An existing charm is never replaced automatically to make room. If the target category is full, the dryad cannot add a charm in that category until one ends, which it can arrange with its separate bonus action. This preserves both the stated cap and the explicit ending procedure. [SRD p. 305, Dryad, Fey Charm](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-305.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-air-elemental-whirlwind"></a> **Action — Whirlwind (Recharge 4–6).** `srd51.creature.air-elemental.whirlwind` Each creature in the elemental's space makes a DC 13 Strength save. Failure deals 15 (3d8 + 2) bludgeoning and hurls the target up to 20 feet away in a random direction, knocking it prone. A thrown target that strikes an object takes a further 3 (1d6) bludgeoning per 10 feet thrown. When the throw is directed at another creature, resolve a DC 13 Dexterity save for that creature. Failure inflicts the same impact damage on that creature and knocks it prone. Success on the initial Strength save halves the initial damage and avoids displacement and prone. **Project interpretation.** The PDF says “flung up 20 feet away,” with no “to.” Read this as a maximum displacement of 20 feet in the randomly determined direction, following the comparable random throw wording of Fling. A fixed 20-foot displacement is another reading. [SRD p. 306, Air Elemental, Whirlwind (Recharge 4–6)](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-306.md)

## Half-Dragon Template

`srd51.creature-rules.half-dragon-template`

Apply only to a beast, humanoid, giant, or monstrosity. Keep the base creature's statistics except for the changes below. Add blindsight to 10 feet, darkvision to 60 feet, and spoken Draconic without losing existing languages. Gain damage resistance keyed to the dragon half's color. Black and copper grant acid, blue and bronze lightning, brass, gold, and red fire, green poison, and silver and white cold. ## Lycanthropy

`srd51.creature-rules.lycanthropy`

The specific bite or tusk attack can curse a humanoid target that fails its listed Constitution save. Each named profile specifies the monster's own forms, form-restricted attacks, and exceptions to its statistics. See [werebear](#srd51-creature-werebear), [wereboar](#srd51-creature-wereboar), [wererat](#srd51-creature-wererat), [weretiger](#srd51-creature-weretiger), and [werewolf](#srd51-creature-werewolf). The curse is separate from the triggering attack's damage. [SRD p. 327, Werebear and Wereboar](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-327.md) · [SRD p. 328, Wererat and Weretiger](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-328.md) · [SRD p. 329, Werewolf](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-329.md)

**Derived conclusion.** [Remove Curse](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-remove-curse) ends an acquired lycanthropy curse on the touched creature, because the attack imposes a curse and the spell ends all curses affecting a creature. These entries provide no exception. [SRD p. 174, Remove Curse](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-174.md) · [SRD p. 175, Remove Curse continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-175.md) · [SRD pp. 327–329, Lycanthrope curse attacks](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-327.md)

**Evidence gap.** The local Lycanthropes section supplies five monster stat blocks and their curse attacks. It supplies no player-character conversion, inherited form or cure exception, rules to transfer ability scores or proficiencies, alignment and character-control transitions, or involuntary transformation schedule. The monster stat blocks' form statistics therefore do not by themselves specify what changes on a player character who receives the curse. ## Acceptance cases

- **Normal.** A werebear in hybrid form can use Multiattack to choose the two-Claw sequence or the two-Greataxe sequence. Its Bite is available in that form as a separate action and curses only an eligible humanoid failing the save. [SRD p. 327, Werebear](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-327.md)
- **Boundary.** A gorgon that moves exactly 20 feet straight toward its target and hits it with Gore can trigger the charge's DC 16 Strength save. A 19-foot approach cannot. [SRD p. 318, Gorgon, Trampling Charge](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-318.md)
- **Exception.** A Medium creature grappled by Whelm is unable to breathe unless it can breathe water. Whelm can still restrain a water-breathing captive and deal its ongoing damage. [SRD p. 307, Water Elemental, Whelm](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-307.md)
- **Invalid.** A kraken on dry ground cannot choose the underwater-only Ink Cloud even with all three legendary actions. A lich with only one legendary action remaining cannot select Frightening Gaze. [SRD p. 325, Kraken, Ink Cloud](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-325.md) · [SRD p. 326, Lich, Frightening Gaze](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-326.md)
