# Interpretations and evidence gaps

The [conventions](conventions.md) distinguish source requirements, derived
conclusions, project interpretations, and choices reserved for the DM or player.
An interpretation's primary definition contains its operational result, reasoning,
and precise source citations. The links below index every locally labeled
**Project interpretation** without establishing competing definitions. Several
decisions under the same primary share one link. Dependents apply the linked
primary within their own stated scope.

These working readings resolve ambiguity for this specification. They are not
additional SRD requirements. Source-defined choices remain choices; narrative
latitude and facts that the DM supplies for a particular world are not missing
rules. No later edition or external commentary supplies unmarked rules here.

supply. Their affected behavior and limits remain explicit in the primary
entries. An implementer must retain the documented boundary rather than invent
an omitted feature set, stat block, or table.

## Source defects with working resolutions

Two locally labeled evidence gaps have enough surrounding evidence for a stated
operational reading. They remain source defects even though this specification
chooses a usable result. Other textual ambiguities, including Ice Walk and
Teleportation Circle, are recorded with their linked interpretations above.

| Defect | Working resolution |
|---|---|
| [p. 45, Draconic Presence](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-045.md) requires a Wisdom save without specifying its DC. | [Draconic Presence](classes-rogue-sorcerer.md#srd51-class-sorcerer-draconic-presence) uses the sorcerer's spell save DC as an explicitly reasoned project interpretation. |
| [p. 115, Animal Friendship](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-115.md) has a broken upcasting sentence in the PDF itself. | [Animal Friendship](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-animal-friendship) permits one additional beast per slot level above 1, with the surviving text and rationale documented locally. |

Source limits are separate from application support. They neither imply that an
unimplemented rule is absent from the SRD nor prevent completing the unaffected
semantics.
