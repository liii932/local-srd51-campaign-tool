# Class spell lists

The memberships below are the base lists for learning and preparing spells in
SRD 5.1. Each class or feature determines the spell levels, choices, and
casting permissions that apply. A spell can belong to several lists, and the
associated class still governs that character's casting. Spells added or granted
by a [domain](classes-cleric-druid.md#srd51-class-cleric-divine-domain),
[circle](classes-cleric-druid.md#srd51-class-druid-circle-spells),
[oath](classes-paladin-ranger.md#srd51-class-paladin-sacred-oath),
[patron](classes-warlock-wizard.md#srd51-class-warlock-expanded-spell-list),
or another feature follow that feature's rules. See the
[spell index](spell-index.md) for the individual definitions by name. [SRD p. 58, Spells Known and Prepared](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-058.md) [SRD p. 105, Spell Lists](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-105.md)

## Source-checked cases

- **Ordinary.** A level 1 bard can choose Vicious Mockery as a cantrip. [SRD p. 12, Bard Cantrips](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-012.md) [SRD p. 105, Bard Spells](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-105.md)
- **Boundary.** Revivify is a 3rd-level paladin spell. A single-class paladin first receives a 3rd-level slot at level 9 and may then prepare it if the preparation limit allows. [SRD p. 30, The Paladin](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-030.md) [SRD p. 31, Preparing and Casting Spells](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-031.md) [SRD p. 108, Paladin Spells](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-108.md)
- **Exception.** Hallow is absent from the base warlock list, but a Fiend warlock may choose it as a known spell once eligible for 5th-level choices. Being on the expanded list does not make it automatically known. [SRD p. 47, Spells Known of 1st Level and Higher](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-047.md) [SRD p. 50, Fiend Expanded Spells](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-050.md) [SRD p. 111, Warlock Spells](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-111.md)
- **Invalid.** A single-class wizard cannot choose Eldritch Blast as one of its starting wizard cantrips. It is on the warlock list. [SRD p. 52, Wizard Cantrips](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-052.md) [SRD p. 110, Warlock Spells](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-110.md) [SRD p. 111, Wizard Spells](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-111.md)
