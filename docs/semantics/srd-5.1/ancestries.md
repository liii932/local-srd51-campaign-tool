# Ancestries

<a id="srd51-ancestry-traits"></a>

## Reading ancestry traits

`srd51.ancestry.traits`

An ancestry supplies its listed ability-score increases, size, speed, and other grants. A subrace adds its own traits to the parent ancestry. Each granted language permits speaking, reading, and writing it. Ancestry age figures describe typical development and lifespan. Players choose their character's age and alignment, using those figures and ancestry tendencies as characterization. Ancestry descriptions generally place Medium people around 4–8 feet tall and Small people around 2–4 feet tall. Use the assigned category, which affects rules such as wielding heavy weapons. Walking speed applies through the [travel](exploration.md#srd51-core-travel-pace) and [combat movement](combat.md#srd51-core-turn) rules. [SRD p. 3, Racial Traits and Subraces](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-003.md)

## Dwarf

`srd51.ancestry.dwarf`

Constitution +2, Medium, base walking speed 25 feet. Heavy armor cannot reduce this speed. Typical stature is 4–5 feet and about 150 pounds. Dwarves mature as quickly as humans, are considered young until 50, and average about 350 years. Typical alignment tends toward lawful and good. Common and Dwarvish are granted. [SRD p. 3, Dwarf Traits](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-003.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-dwarf-darkvision"></a>

### Darkvision

`srd51.ancestry.dwarf.darkvision`

Range 60 feet. See [racial darkvision](exploration.md#srd51-core-racial-darkvision). [SRD p. 3, Darkvision](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-003.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-dwarf-dwarven-resilience"></a>

### Dwarven Resilience

`srd51.ancestry.dwarf.dwarven-resilience`

Gain advantage on saving throws against poison and resistance to poison damage. [SRD p. 3, Dwarven Resilience](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-003.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-dwarf-dwarven-combat-training"></a>

### Dwarven Combat Training

`srd51.ancestry.dwarf.dwarven-combat-training`

Gain proficiency with battleaxe, handaxe, light hammer, and warhammer. [SRD p. 3, Dwarven Combat Training](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-003.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-dwarf-tool-proficiency"></a>

### Tool Proficiency

`srd51.ancestry.dwarf.tool-proficiency`

Choose one proficiency from smith's tools, brewer's supplies, and mason's tools. [SRD p. 3, Tool Proficiency](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-003.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-dwarf-stonecunning"></a>

### Stonecunning

`srd51.ancestry.dwarf.stonecunning`

For Intelligence (History) checks about the origin of stonework, count as proficient in History and apply twice your proficiency bonus in place of the normal proficiency contribution. Other History checks follow normal proficiency rules. [SRD p. 3, Stonecunning](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-003.md)

## Elf

`srd51.ancestry.elf`

Dexterity +2, Medium, base walking speed 30 feet. Elves are slender and range from under 5 feet to over 6 feet tall. They physically mature at about the human rate, usually take adult status and an adult name around 100, and can reach 750. Typical alignment tends toward chaos and good. Common and Elvish are granted. [SRD p. 4, Elf Traits](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-004.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-elf-darkvision"></a>

### Darkvision

`srd51.ancestry.elf.darkvision`

Range 60 feet. See [racial darkvision](exploration.md#srd51-core-racial-darkvision). [SRD p. 4, Darkvision](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-004.md)

### Fey Ancestry

`srd51.ancestry.elf.fey-ancestry`

Gain advantage on saves against being charmed. Magic cannot put you to sleep. [SRD p. 4, Fey Ancestry](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-004.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-elf-trance"></a>

### Trance

`srd51.ancestry.elf.trance`

An elf has no need to sleep. It can meditate while semiconscious for 4 hours daily, receiving the benefit of 8 hours of human sleep after resting this way. Dreamlike experiences can occur as practiced mental exercises. Consult [long rest](exploration.md#srd51-core-long-rest) for the separate rest rules. [SRD p. 4, Trance](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-004.md)

## Halfling

`srd51.ancestry.halfling`

Dexterity +2, Small, base walking speed 25 feet. Halflings average about 3 feet and 40 pounds. They reach adulthood at 20 and usually live to about 150. Typical alignment is lawful good. Common and Halfling are granted. [SRD p. 4, Halfling Traits](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-004.md) [SRD p. 5, Halfling Traits continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-005.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-halfling-lucky"></a>

### Lucky

`srd51.ancestry.halfling.lucky`

On a d20 result of 1 for an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, the halfling may reroll the die and must use the new result if it does. The [advantage/disadvantage rule](abilities.md#srd51-core-advantage-disadvantage) limits a reroll to one of the dice. [SRD p. 4, Lucky](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-004.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-halfling-brave"></a>

### Brave

`srd51.ancestry.halfling.brave`

Gain advantage on saves against being frightened. [SRD p. 4, Brave](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-004.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-halfling-halfling-nimbleness"></a>

### Halfling Nimbleness

`srd51.ancestry.halfling.halfling-nimbleness`

A creature's space can be passed through if that creature is larger than the halfling. See the general [turn and movement](combat.md#srd51-core-turn) constraints on moving through and ending movement in occupied spaces. [SRD p. 5, Halfling Nimbleness](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-005.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-lightfoot"></a>

### Lightfoot

`srd51.ancestry.lightfoot`

Add Charisma +1 to the [halfling traits](#srd51-ancestry-halfling). [SRD p. 5, Lightfoot](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-005.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-lightfoot-naturally-stealthy"></a>

#### Naturally Stealthy

`srd51.ancestry.lightfoot.naturally-stealthy`

You may attempt to hide when your only obscuration is a creature at least one size larger than you. Resolve the attempt under [hiding](abilities.md#srd51-core-hiding). [SRD p. 5, Naturally Stealthy](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-005.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-human"></a>

## Human

`srd51.ancestry.human`

Increase each of the six ability scores by 1. Medium, base walking speed 30 feet. Human height ranges from barely 5 feet to well over 6 feet, with a wide range of builds. Humans reach adulthood in their late teens and live less than 100 years. There is no typical alignment. Grant Common and one additional language of the player's choice. [SRD p. 5, Human Traits](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-005.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-dragonborn"></a>

## Dragonborn

`srd51.ancestry.dragonborn`

Strength +2, Charisma +1, Medium, base walking speed 30 feet. Dragonborn usually stand well over 6 feet tall and average nearly 250 pounds. They walk within hours of hatching, develop to about the size and maturity of a ten-year-old human by age 3, reach adulthood at 15, and live around 80 years. Their alignment tends toward extremes of good and evil, with good more common. Common and Draconic are granted. [SRD p. 5, Dragonborn Traits](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-005.md) [SRD p. 6, Dragonborn Languages](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-006.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-dragonborn-draconic-ancestry"></a>

### Draconic Ancestry

`srd51.ancestry.dragonborn.draconic-ancestry`

Choose one dragon type. Its damage type governs both [Breath Weapon](#srd51-ancestry-dragonborn-breath-weapon) and [Damage Resistance](#srd51-ancestry-dragonborn-damage-resistance). The shape and save come from the same choice. [SRD p. 5, Draconic Ancestry](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-005.md) [SRD p. 6, Damage Resistance](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-006.md)

| Breath area and save | Dragon choices and damage types |
| --- | --- |
| 5-foot-wide, 30-foot-long line, Dexterity | Black or Copper → acid. Blue or Bronze → lightning. Brass → fire. |
| 15-foot cone, Dexterity | Gold or Red → fire. |
| 15-foot cone, Constitution | Green → poison. Silver or White → cold. |

<a id="srd51-ancestry-dragonborn-breath-weapon"></a>

### Breath Weapon

`srd51.ancestry.dragonborn.breath-weapon`

Use an action to exhale into the area fixed by Draconic Ancestry. Every creature in it rolls the ancestry's save against DC 8 + the dragonborn's Constitution modifier + proficiency bonus. Base damage is 2d6, rising to 3d6 at character level 6, 4d6 at 11, and 5d6 at 16. Failed save takes full damage of the ancestry's type. Successful save takes half. After using the breath, finish a short or long rest before using it again. [SRD p. 5, Breath Weapon](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-005.md) [SRD p. 6, Breath Weapon continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-006.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-dragonborn-damage-resistance"></a>

### Damage Resistance

`srd51.ancestry.dragonborn.damage-resistance`

You have resistance to the damage type of your chosen Draconic Ancestry. [SRD p. 6, Damage Resistance](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-006.md)

## Gnome

`srd51.ancestry.gnome`

Intelligence +2, Small, base walking speed 25 feet. Gnomes usually stand 3–4 feet tall and average about 40 pounds. They mature at the human rate, usually settle into adult life around 40, and can live from 350 to nearly 500 years. Typical alignment is good, with both lawful and chaotic dispositions. Common and Gnomish are granted. Gnomish uses the Dwarvish script. [SRD p. 6, Gnome Traits](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-006.md)

#### Artificer's Lore

`srd51.ancestry.rock-gnome.artificers-lore`

For Intelligence (History) checks about magic items, alchemical objects, or technological devices, you may use twice your proficiency bonus in place of any normal proficiency contribution. [SRD p. 6, Artificer's Lore](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-006.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-rock-gnome-tinker"></a>

#### Tinker

`srd51.ancestry.rock-gnome.tinker`

You are proficient with tinker’s tools. To build one device, pick a design, use those tools for an hour, and spend 10 gp worth of materials. The finished clockwork is Tiny, with 1 HP and AC 5. Your limit is three working devices. Each works for 24 hours unless you keep it going with an hour of repair. You can also end its operation by dismantling it as an action. [SRD p. 6, Tinker](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-006.md)

**Project interpretation.** Allow reclamation of construction materials after either automatic expiry or dismantling. The phrase “at that time” follows both routes to cessation and could also modify only the closer dismantling clause. This reading applies it to the shared cessation event. [SRD p. 6, Tinker](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-006.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-rock-gnome-tinker-clockwork-toy"></a>

##### Clockwork Toy

`srd51.ancestry.rock-gnome.tinker.clockwork-toy`

Represent an animal, monster, or person. When placed on the ground, the toy travels 5 feet along the ground in a random direction on each of your turns and makes noises fitting what it represents. [SRD p. 6, Clockwork Toy](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-006.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-rock-gnome-tinker-fire-starter"></a>

##### Fire Starter

`srd51.ancestry.rock-gnome.tinker.fire-starter`

Use your action to produce a small flame suitable for lighting a candle, torch, or campfire. [SRD p. 6, Fire Starter](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-006.md)

<a id="srd51-ancestry-rock-gnome-tinker-music-box"></a>

##### Music Box

`srd51.ancestry.rock-gnome.tinker.music-box`

Opening it plays one song at moderate volume. The playback ends when the song finishes or someone closes the box. [SRD p. 6, Music Box](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-006.md)

## Half-Orc

`srd51.ancestry.half-orc`

Strength +2, Constitution +1, Medium, base walking speed 30 feet. Half-orcs are generally bulkier than humans and range from 5 feet to well over 6 feet tall. They reach adulthood around 14 and seldom live past 75. Chaos is a tendency, and those raised among orcs who choose to remain with them are usually evil. Grant Common and Orc. ## Tiefling

`srd51.ancestry.tiefling`

Intelligence +1, Charisma +2, Medium, base walking speed 30 feet. Tieflings have a similar range of stature and builds to humans. They mature at the human rate and live a few years longer. Independence often inclines them toward chaos. The source describes evil as a common outcome without treating it as innate. Common and Infernal are granted. ### Infernal Legacy

`srd51.ancestry.tiefling.infernal-legacy`

Know the [Thaumaturgy](spells-t-z.md#srd51-spell-thaumaturgy) cantrip. At character level 3 gain one slotless casting of [Hellish Rebuke](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-hellish-rebuke) as a 2nd-level spell per long rest. At character level 5 gain a separate slotless casting of [Darkness](spells-d-e.md#srd51-spell-darkness) per long rest. Charisma is the spellcasting ability for all three. Apply each spell's normal timing and components. [SRD p. 7, Infernal Legacy](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-007.md)

## Source-checked cases

- **Ordinary.** A 6th-level Green dragonborn breathes a 15-foot cone for 3d6 poison damage. Every creature in the cone saves using Constitution. A creature that succeeds takes half. [SRD p. 5, Draconic Ancestry and Breath Weapon](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-005.md) [SRD p. 6, Breath Weapon continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-006.md)
- **Boundary.** A Small halfling can move through a Medium creature's space. Nimbleness supplies no permission for a space occupied by another Small creature. [SRD p. 5, Halfling Nimbleness](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-005.md)
- **Exception.** A half-orc asleep with positive hit points takes damage that would reduce them to 0 without killing them outright. An available Relentless Endurance use can make the result 1 hit point instead. The feature specifies no action or reaction to spend. [SRD p. 7, Relentless Endurance](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-007.md)
- **Invalid.** A rock gnome with three active devices cannot activate a fourth without first freeing capacity. [SRD p. 6, Tinker](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-006.md)
