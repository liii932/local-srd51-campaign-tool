# Coins, armor, and weapons

<a id="srd51-equipment-coins"></a>
## Coins

`srd51.equipment.coins`

Gold pieces (gp) are the standard unit for quoting prices. Use a common copper-piece value to convert denominations in either direction: a copper piece is 1 cp, a silver piece is 10 cp, an electrum piece is 50 cp, a gold piece is 100 cp, and a platinum piece is 1,000 cp. Gold is consequently worth ten silver or two electrum, and platinum is worth ten gold. Fifty standard coins weigh about 1 pound, irrespective of denomination. Silver is common among ordinary people and copper among laborers and the poor. Electrum and platinum are unusual and can arouse suspicion. Large transactions can be quoted in gp and settled using bars, credit, or goods instead of coins. [SRD p. 62, Equipment and Standard Exchange Rates](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-062.md)

The source gives 1 gp per day as an example of an ordinary skilled artisan's earnings and 1 sp for half a day of labor. These examples describe local purchasing power. The separate [services schedule](adventuring-gear.md#srd51-equipment-services) gives prices for hiring. [SRD p. 62, Equipment](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-062.md) · [SRD p. 74, Services](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-074.md)

<a id="srd51-equipment-selling-treasure"></a>
## Selling treasure

`srd51.equipment.selling-treasure`

Sales require a buyer, often found in a settlement. Undamaged weapons, armor, and other ordinary equipment generally sell for half their listed cost. Gear scavenged from monsters is seldom in salable condition. Gems, jewelry, art, and trade goods generally retain their full value and can be bartered directly. Especially valuable objects may require a buyer in a large town or larger community. Potions and scrolls are relatively easy to find buyers for. Other magic items may be affordable only to very wealthy buyers, and magic items and spells are usually unavailable for purchase except for a few common magic items. Treat this as market guidance, with no fixed price formula for magic. [SRD p. 62, Selling Treasure](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-062.md)

<a id="srd51-equipment-armor"></a>
## Armor and shields

`srd51.equipment.armor`

An armor suit supplies the base for the wearer's AC. Light armor adds the entire Dexterity modifier. Medium armor adds the Dexterity modifier capped at +2, including a negative modifier in full. Heavy armor uses the listed AC without a Dexterity adjustment. A wielded shield occupies one hand and adds +2 to AC. The wearer can benefit from one shield at a time. [SRD p. 62, Armor and Armor Class](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-062.md) · [SRD p. 63, Shields and the Light, Medium, and Heavy Armor categories](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-063.md)

Any creature that can put on the equipment may wear armor or strap on a shield. If it wears armor it lacks proficiency with, it has disadvantage on all Strength- or Dexterity-based checks, saves, and attacks and cannot [cast spells](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-casting-in-armor). Armor with a Strength requirement reduces speed by 10 feet when the wearer's Strength score is lower. Where a suit penalizes Stealth, apply disadvantage to Dexterity (Stealth) checks. The [encumbrance variant](abilities.md#srd51-core-encumbrance) replaces use of the Strength column. [SRD p. 62, Armor Proficiency](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-062.md) · [SRD p. 63, Heavy Armor and Stealth](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-063.md) · [SRD p. 80, Variant: Encumbrance](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-080.md)

**Project interpretation.** Apply the nonproficiency penalties to an unproficient creature wielding a shield. The Armor Proficiency passage introduces shields along with armor and the Armor table includes shield as its own category. A narrower reading of “wear armor” would exclude a wielded shield. The combined reading makes the named shield proficiency matter under the same equipment rule. [SRD p. 62, Armor Proficiency](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-062.md) · [SRD pp. 63–64, Shields and Armor](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-063.md) · [SRD p. 64, Armor continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-064.md)

<a id="srd51-equipment-donning-and-doffing-armor"></a>
### Donning and doffing

`srd51.equipment.donning-and-doffing-armor`

Finish donning the suit to gain its AC. Light armor takes 1 minute to put on and 1 minute to remove. Medium takes 5 minutes to put on and 1 minute to remove. Heavy takes 10 minutes to put on and 5 minutes to remove. A shield takes 1 action in either direction. Help halves the time to remove armor. The source gives no separate fractional-action procedure for a helped shield removal. [SRD p. 64, Getting Into and Out of Armor and Donning and Doffing Armor](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-064.md)

**Project interpretation.** With help, halve suit doffing times, yielding 30 seconds for light or medium armor and 2½ minutes for heavy armor. Doffing a shield still takes one action, since the source gives no half-action procedure. Another reading would convert the shield's action to elapsed time, but the table distinguishes actions from timed suit removal. [SRD p. 64, Getting Into and Out of Armor and Donning and Doffing Armor](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-064.md)

### Armor construction

Use the physical construction when a rule cares about material, including the [druid's armor restriction](classes-cleric-druid.md), [Heat Metal](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-heat-metal), and [Shocking Grasp](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-shocking-grasp). The source describes the following constructions. [SRD p. 63, Shields, Light Armor, Medium Armor, and Heavy Armor](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-063.md)

- **Padded:** quilted cloth with batting.
- **Leather:** stiffened leather at the chest and shoulders and more flexible material elsewhere.
- **Studded leather:** tough flexible leather with closely spaced rivets or spikes. The source gives no material for the reinforcement.
- **Hide:** thick furs and pelts.
- **Chain shirt:** interlocking metal rings between layers of clothing or leather.
- **Scale mail:** leather covered by overlapping metal scales, with gauntlets.
- **Breastplate:** a fitted metal chest piece with flexible leather.
- **Half plate:** shaped metal plates, with simple greaves strapped to the legs with leather.
- **Ring mail:** leather with heavy sewn-on rings. The source does not name the ring material.
- **Chain mail:** interlocking metal rings over quilted padding, with gauntlets.
- **Splint:** narrow metal strips riveted to leather over cloth padding, with chain mail at the joints.
- **Plate:** interlocking metal plates with gauntlets, heavy leather boots, a visored helmet, and padding. Buckles and straps distribute the load.
- **Shield:** wood or metal, depending on the item.

**Project interpretation.** Resolve whether a particular piece of equipment is made of metal from its actual construction. Where the description specifies metal as part of its protective structure, that is evidence of metal armor. For rings or studs of unspecified material, and for minor fittings on an otherwise nonmetal suit, the source does not decide the material or give a universal mixed-material threshold. The GM adjudicates the actual item rather than assigning a material from the armor name alone. [SRD p. 63, Shields and armor construction](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-063.md)

**Acceptance cases.** A proficient character with Dexterity modifier −2 has AC 11 in a chain shirt or 13 with a wielded shield. The same character in chain mail has AC 16 without the shield. Strength 13 avoids the chain mail speed penalty while Strength 12 incurs the 10-foot reduction. A second shield never grants another +2. [SRD pp. 62–64, Armor](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-062.md) · [SRD p. 63, Heavy Armor, Shields, and armor categories](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-063.md) · [SRD p. 64, Armor](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-064.md)

<a id="srd51-equipment-weapons"></a>
## Weapons

`srd51.equipment.weapons`

Every weapon belongs to one proficiency category, simple or martial, and one attack category, melee or ranged. A character can gain proficiency by weapon or category from ancestry, class, or a feat. Weapon proficiency adds the character's proficiency bonus to attack rolls with that weapon. Resolve melee and ranged attacks through their [combat rules](combat.md#srd51-core-attack). [SRD p. 64, Weapons and Weapon Proficiency](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-064.md)

<a id="srd51-equipment-ammunition"></a>
### Ammunition

`srd51.equipment.ammunition`

A ranged attack with the weapon needs one piece of its ammunition and expends that piece whether the attack hits or misses. Drawing it from its container is part of the attack. A one-handed ammunition weapon needs a free hand to load. After the battle, spending one minute searching recovers half the expended ammunition. Since a piece is discrete, recover at most the greatest whole count within half. Using an ammunition weapon in melee invokes the improvised weapon rule. An unloaded sling deals no damage used that way. [SRD p. 64, Ammunition](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-064.md)

<a id="srd51-equipment-finesse"></a>
### Finesse

`srd51.equipment.finesse`

Choose either Strength or Dexterity for a finesse weapon attack and use the same modifier for its attack and damage rolls. [SRD p. 64, Finesse](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-064.md)

<a id="srd51-equipment-heavy"></a>
### Heavy

`srd51.equipment.heavy`

Small creatures have disadvantage on attacks with heavy weapons. The local text specifies Small creatures for this predicate. [SRD p. 65, Heavy](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-065.md)

<a id="srd51-equipment-light"></a>
### Light

`srd51.equipment.light`

A light melee weapon can meet the weapon requirement of [two-weapon fighting](combat.md#srd51-core-two-weapon-fighting). Light by itself supplies no bonus action. [SRD p. 65, Light](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-065.md) · [SRD p. 95, Two-Weapon Fighting](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-095.md)

<a id="srd51-equipment-loading"></a>
### Loading

`srd51.equipment.loading`

When using an action, bonus action, or reaction to fire a loading weapon, you can fire only one piece of ammunition from it during that use, even if that use grants several attacks. [SRD p. 65, Loading](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-065.md)

<a id="srd51-equipment-weapon-range"></a>
### Range

`srd51.equipment.weapon-range`

An ammunition or thrown range pair gives normal and long range in feet, respectively. Beyond normal range through long range, attack with disadvantage. Beyond long range, the target is ineligible. [SRD p. 65, Range](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-065.md)

<a id="srd51-equipment-reach"></a>
### Reach

`srd51.equipment.reach`

Add 5 feet to your reach when attacking with the weapon and when determining the range of opportunity attacks with it. [SRD p. 65, Reach](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-065.md)

<a id="srd51-equipment-special-weapon"></a>
### Special

`srd51.equipment.special-weapon`

A Special property directs you to the particular weapon's rule. For a lance or a net, apply the rules below alongside other listed properties. [SRD p. 65, Special and Special Weapons](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-065.md)

<a id="srd51-equipment-thrown"></a>
### Thrown

`srd51.equipment.thrown`

Throw the weapon to make a ranged attack within its thrown range. For a thrown melee weapon, use the ability modifier its melee attack would use for both attack and damage. For example, a thrown handaxe uses Strength and a thrown dagger can use either Strength or Dexterity because of finesse. [SRD p. 65, Thrown](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-065.md)

<a id="srd51-equipment-two-handed"></a>
### Two-handed

`srd51.equipment.two-handed`

Have two hands available to hold the weapon when you attack with it. [SRD p. 65, Two-Handed](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-065.md)

<a id="srd51-equipment-versatile"></a>
### Versatile

`srd51.equipment.versatile`

Wield the weapon in one or two hands. Use the separate damage value only when using two hands for a melee attack. [SRD p. 65, Versatile](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-065.md)

<a id="srd51-equipment-improvised-weapons"></a>
### Improvised weapons

`srd51.equipment.improvised-weapons`

An object you can wield in one or two hands can serve as an improvised weapon. If it resembles an existing weapon, the GM can let a creature proficient with that weapon use the object as that weapon and add proficiency. Otherwise the damage die is 1d4 and the GM chooses a suitable type. A ranged weapon used in melee or a melee weapon thrown without the Thrown property also uses 1d4. An improvised thrown weapon has range 20/60 feet. An ammunition weapon in melee follows the ammunition rule's exception for an unloaded sling. [SRD pp. 64–65, Ammunition and Improvised Weapons](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-064.md) · [SRD p. 65, Improvised Weapons](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-065.md)

<a id="srd51-equipment-silvered-weapons"></a>
### Silvered weapons

`srd51.equipment.silvered-weapons`

Pay 100 gp to silver one weapon or ten pieces of ammunition. The cost covers the silver and work. Silvering preserves ordinary effectiveness and can bypass a monster's resistance or immunity when that creature's rule specifies susceptibility to silvered weapons. [SRD p. 65, Silvered Weapons](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-065.md)

### Special use of a lance

`srd51.equipment.lance-use`

Attacking a target within 5 feet with a lance imposes disadvantage. Wield the lance with two hands while unmounted. A mounted wielder may use one hand. [SRD p. 65, Special Weapons, Lance](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-065.md)

<a id="srd51-equipment-net-use"></a>
### Special use of a net

`srd51.equipment.net-use`

An attack with a net permits only one attack in the action, bonus action, or reaction used, even when that use would normally grant more. A hit restrains a creature that has form and is Large or smaller. A formless or Huge-or-larger creature is unaffected. To free yourself or a creature you can reach, spend an action and succeed on a DC 10 Strength check. Another route is to deal 5 slashing damage to the net against its AC of 10, destroying the net and freeing its captive without harming the captive. [SRD p. 65, Special Weapons, Net](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-065.md)

**Acceptance cases.** A proficient archer can fire a shortbow at 80 feet without the range disadvantage, at 81 feet with it, and cannot target something at 321 feet. A combatant with two weapon attacks in one action cannot fire the same loading light crossbow twice during that action. An attack with a net does not restrain a Huge creature even on a hit. [SRD p. 65, Range, Loading and Special Weapons, Net](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-065.md) · [SRD p. 66, Weapons](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-066.md)
