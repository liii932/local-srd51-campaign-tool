# Creature statistics and behavior

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-stat-block"></a>

## Reading a stat block

`srd51.creature-rules.stat-block`

A creature's stat block supplies the statistics, traits, and choices needed to run it. Use the listed values and any applicable general rules. The GM may modify a creature, borrow traits, or apply a variant or template. A modification can change the creature's challenge rating. [SRD p. 254, Monsters and Modifying Creatures](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-254.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-size"></a>

## Size

`srd51.creature-rules.size`

The combat space is the area the creature controls rather than its exact bodily dimensions. Tiny controls a square 2½ feet on a side. Small and Medium each control 5 feet, Large 10 feet, Huge 15 feet, and Gargantuan at least 20 feet on a side. Use the [combat space and movement rules](combat.md#srd51-core-creature-size) for occupation, squeezing, and nearby creatures. [SRD p. 254, Size and Size Categories](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-254.md) [SRD p. 92, Creature Size](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-092.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-type"></a>

## Types and tags

`srd51.creature-rules.type`

Type describes a creature's fundamental nature. The types listed below carry no general action, defense, or other mechanical package on their own. A spell, item, class feature, or other rule can use type as a target or effect predicate. Tags in parentheses further categorize the creature and likewise have significance when a rule refers to them. A dragon-slaying item keyed to the dragon type can affect a wyvern even though it is not a true dragon. An effect keyed to the demon tag applies to creatures with that tag. [SRD p. 254, Type](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-254.md) [SRD p. 255, Type continuation and Tags](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-255.md)

| Type | Classification described by the source |
| --- | --- |
| Aberration | Alien creatures, often with magic arising from the mind. |
| Beast | Nonhumanoid creatures of the natural ecology, including ordinary, giant, and prehistoric animals. |
| Celestial | Creatures native to the Upper Planes, typically good. |
| Construct | Made creatures, ranging from programmed to independently sentient. |
| Dragon | True dragons and their distant relatives, such as wyverns and pseudodragons. |
| Elemental | Creatures of the elemental planes, including animate elements, element-infused bodies, and genies. |
| Fey | Magic creatures closely associated with nature and, in some worlds, the Feywild or certain Outer Planes. |
| Fiend | Lower Plane creatures such as demons, devils, and their kin, typically evil. |
| Giant | True giants and related creatures such as trolls and ogres. |
| Humanoid | The diverse peoples of the world, generally bipedal with language and culture. |
| Monstrosity | Unnatural creatures that may result from magical experiments, curses, or other origins. |
| Ooze | Gelatinous creatures, usually with no fixed form. |
| Plant | Vegetable or fungal creatures, distinct from ordinary plants. |
| Undead | Once-living creatures animated by necromancy or a curse, including corporeal and incorporeal forms. |

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-alignment"></a>

## Creature alignment

`srd51.creature-rules.alignment`

A listed alignment is the default and helps the GM decide how the creature behaves. The GM can change it for an individual. “Any” leaves the choice open, while an entry such as “any chaotic alignment” limits the default choices to the stated axis. An unaligned creature lacks alignment. See [alignment](backgrounds-and-personality.md#srd51-character-alignment) for the nine possibilities. [SRD p. 255, Alignment](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-255.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-armor-class"></a>

## Armor Class

`srd51.creature-rules.armor-class`

Use a creature's listed AC. If it wears armor or carries a shield, AC accounts for that equipment and Dexterity as applicable. Otherwise AC derives from Dexterity and any natural armor. The entry identifies natural armor or relevant equipment when present and can give distinct values for different forms. [SRD p. 255, Armor Class](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-255.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-hit-points"></a>

## Hit points and Hit Dice

`srd51.creature-rules.hit-points`

A creature entry presents hit points as an average and as a dice expression. Its Hit Die follows size, with Tiny d4, Small d6, Medium d8, Large d10, Huge d12, and Gargantuan d20. Mean hit points per die before modifiers are respectively 2½, 3½, 4½, 5½, 6½, and 10½. The dice expression adds Constitution modifier × number of dice. A Medium creature with Constitution 12 and two Hit Dice has 2d8 + 2, averaging 11. A monster usually dies or is destroyed when it reaches 0 hit points. See [monster death](combat.md#srd51-core-monsters-and-death) for the GM's discretion. [SRD p. 255, Hit Points](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-255.md) [SRD p. 256, Hit Dice by Size and Constitution](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-256.md) [SRD p. 98, Monsters and Death](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-098.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-speed"></a>

## Creature speed and movement modes

`srd51.creature-rules.speed`

The unlabeled speed is walking speed. A creature without ground locomotion has a walking speed of 0. The listed speeds determine [movement on its turn](combat.md#srd51-core-turn), including switching modes under the movement rules. [SRD p. 256, Speed](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-256.md)

- **Burrow.** Use the burrowing speed through sand, earth, mud, or ice. Solid rock requires a feature that permits it. [SRD p. 256, Burrow](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-256.md)
- **Climb.** Use the climbing speed for vertical movement without the normal extra movement cost of climbing. [SRD p. 256, Climb](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-256.md)
- **Fly.** Use the flying speed to fly. A listed hover capability supplies the [flying movement exception](combat.md#srd51-core-flying-movement) to falling. It stops hovering when it dies. [SRD p. 256, Fly](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-256.md) [SRD p. 92, Flying Movement](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-092.md)
- **Swim.** Use the swimming speed without the normal extra movement cost of swimming. [SRD p. 256, Swim](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-256.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-ability-scores"></a>

## Ability scores and proficiency bonus

`srd51.creature-rules.ability-scores`

Each monster has scores and modifiers for Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma. For a monster's proficiency contribution, use its challenge rating (CR). The following bonus thresholds apply. [SRD p. 256, Ability Scores and Proficiency Bonus by Challenge Rating](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-256.md)

| CR | Proficiency bonus |
| --- | --- |
| 0, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, and 1–4 | +2 |
| 5–8 | +3 |
| 9–12 | +4 |
| 13–16 | +5 |
| 17–20 | +6 |
| 21–24 | +7 |
| 25–28 | +8 |
| 29–30 | +9 |

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-saving-throws"></a>

## Saving throws

`srd51.creature-rules.saving-throws`

A listed save bonus already includes the ability modifier and the monster's CR-based proficiency bonus. Use that listed bonus for that save. If no special bonus applies, use the governing [saving throw rule](abilities.md#srd51-core-saving-throw) with the relevant ability modifier. A missing saving throw entry means the block grants no special save proficiencies. [SRD p. 256, Saving Throws](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-256.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-skills"></a>

## Skill bonuses

`srd51.creature-rules.skills`

A listed skill bonus accounts for the governing ability modifier and proficiency bonus and may also include another adjustment, such as doubling proficiency for expertise. The printed bonus is the total for that skill. For other checks, use the normal [ability check](abilities.md#srd51-core-ability-check) and [skill proficiency](abilities.md#srd51-core-skill-proficiency) rules. [SRD p. 256, Skills](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-256.md) [SRD p. 257, Skills continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-257.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-defenses"></a>

## Damage defenses and condition immunity

`srd51.creature-rules.defenses`

A block can list vulnerabilities, resistances, or immunities to damage types, with qualifiers that limit which sources qualify. For a defense against nonmagical attacks, an attack delivered by a spell, magic item, or another magical source counts as magical. Apply [damage defenses](combat.md#srd51-core-damage-resistance-vulnerability) and [condition immunity](conditions.md#srd51-core-condition-lifecycle) according to the listed scope. [SRD p. 257, Vulnerabilities, Resistances, and Immunities](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-257.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-senses"></a>

## Senses

`srd51.creature-rules.senses`

Use the listed passive Wisdom (Perception) score and the stated range of each special sense. Apply the [Blindsight](exploration.md#srd51-core-blindsight), [Darkvision](exploration.md#srd51-core-darkvision), and [Truesight](exploration.md#srd51-core-truesight) definitions. A creature marked naturally blind or blind beyond its blindsight radius can perceive only within that radius. [SRD p. 257, Senses, Blindsight, Darkvision, and Truesight](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-257.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-tremorsense"></a>

### Tremorsense

`srd51.creature-rules.tremorsense`

Detect vibrations and pinpoint their source within the stated radius when the creature and source are in contact with the same ground or substance. Flying and incorporeal creatures cannot be detected this way. [SRD p. 257, Tremorsense](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-257.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-languages"></a>

## Languages

`srd51.creature-rules.languages`

A language listed for a creature is one it can speak unless its entry restricts it to understanding. A dash means the creature understands and speaks no languages. [SRD p. 257, Languages](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-257.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-telepathy"></a>

### Telepathy

`srd51.creature-rules.telepathy`

Telepathy is magical mental communication within a listed range. A contact must be able to understand at least one language, which need not be shared, and need not be visible. A contact without telepathy can receive and answer but cannot initiate or end the conversation. The telepathic creature can initiate or terminate contact without an action. A contact ends if either creature moves beyond range, if the telepathic creature contacts another creature, or if the telepathic creature becomes incapacitated. Incapacitation also prevents initiating a contact. Creatures in an antimagic field or another location where magic cannot function cannot send or receive these messages. [SRD p. 257, Telepathy](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-257.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-challenge"></a>

## Challenge rating

`srd51.creature-rules.challenge`

CR gauges the threat a monster poses. An appropriately equipped and rested party of four should generally be able to defeat one monster with CR equal to the party's level without deaths. A CR below 1 is weaker than a 1st-level character, CR 0 usually matters only in large numbers, and CR 21 and above can challenge even a 20th-level party. CR is a threat guideline and can change with a creature's modifications. [SRD p. 254, Modifying Creatures](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-254.md) [SRD p. 258, Challenge](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-258.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-experience-points"></a>

### Experience points

`srd51.creature-rules.experience-points`

A monster's XP usually goes to those who defeat it. The GM may award XP for otherwise neutralizing its threat. A creature summoned by magic is worth its listed XP unless a specific rule says otherwise. At CR 0, award 10 XP for a creature with an effective attack and 0 for one without. Other XP values follow these sequences, in the order of the stated CRs. [SRD p. 258, Experience Points and Experience Points by Challenge Rating](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-258.md)

| CR sequence | XP in the same order |
| --- | --- |
| 1/8, 1/4, 1/2 | 25, 50, 100 |
| 1–5 | 200, 450, 700, 1,100, 1,800 |
| 6–10 | 2,300, 2,900, 3,900, 5,000, 5,900 |
| 11–15 | 7,200, 8,400, 10,000, 11,500, 13,000 |
| 16–20 | 15,000, 18,000, 20,000, 22,000, 25,000 |
| 21–25 | 33,000, 41,000, 50,000, 62,000, 75,000 |
| 26–30 | 90,000, 105,000, 120,000, 135,000, 155,000 |

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-special-traits"></a>

## Special traits

`srd51.creature-rules.special-traits`

Traits are the provisions between the challenge entry and the action or reaction entries. They can modify ordinary rules or grant new abilities. Their stated timings, restrictions, and costs govern their use. [SRD p. 258, Special Traits](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-258.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-innate-spellcasting"></a>

### Innate Spellcasting

`srd51.creature-rules.innate-spellcasting`

Cast the listed innate spells with their stated restrictions and use limits. Unless the trait says otherwise, cast a leveled spell at its lowest level. When a cantrip depends on level and no caster level is given, use the monster's challenge rating. A spell restricted to self affects only the monster. Its innate spell selection cannot be swapped for other spells. The trait may omit an attack bonus when none of the listed spells requires an attack roll. [SRD p. 258, Innate Spellcasting](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-258.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-spellcasting"></a>

### Spellcasting

`srd51.creature-rules.spellcasting`

Use the stat block's spellcaster level, spell slots, and known or prepared spells. Spellcaster level also supplies scaling for any cantrips in the trait. The spell list may include class-feature grants, such as a cleric domain's spells. The creature qualifies as a member of the associated class when using or attuning to a magic item that requires that class or its spell list. Spend an eligible slot and apply normal [higher-level casting](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-casting-at-a-higher-level) if appropriate. The GM may replace a spell with another of the same level from the same class list, potentially changing the threat presented. [SRD p. 258, Spellcasting](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-258.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-psionics"></a>

### Psionics

`srd51.creature-rules.psionics`

A Spellcasting or Innate Spellcasting trait with the psionics tag uses the power of the mind. The tag itself adds no general mechanical rule, though effects can refer to it. Such spellcasting typically has no components. Use the creature's stated component rules. [SRD p. 259, Psionics](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-259.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-actions"></a>

## Monster actions

`srd51.creature-rules.actions`

When a monster takes an action, it can use an action option from its stat block or take an action generally available to creatures, such as [Dash](combat.md#srd51-core-dash) or [Hide](combat.md#srd51-core-hide). If a creature's stat block gives special uses for its reaction, those are available alongside applicable [general reaction rules](combat.md#srd51-core-reaction). [SRD p. 259, Actions and Reactions](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-259.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-attack"></a>

### Monster attacks

`srd51.creature-rules.attack`

A melee or ranged attack can be a weapon attack or spell attack. A weapon can be manufactured or natural. An entry that targets one creature requires a creature. An entry that specifies one target can target an object as well. After a hit, apply the listed damage and other effects, using either listed average damage or the listed dice expression. Apply a miss effect only if the action supplies one. [SRD p. 259, Melee and Ranged Attacks](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-259.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-multiattack"></a>

### Multiattack

`srd51.creature-rules.multiattack`

A creature with Multiattack can choose that action on its turn, following the specified combination and counts. It cannot use Multiattack for an opportunity attack, which is one melee attack. [SRD p. 259, Multiattack](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-259.md) [SRD p. 95, Opportunity Attacks](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-095.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-ammunition"></a>

### Ammunition

`srd51.creature-rules.ammunition`

Assume a monster has enough ammunition for its attacks. For tracking inventory, assume 2d4 pieces for a thrown weapon attack and 2d10 pieces for a projectile weapon attack such as a bow or crossbow. [SRD p. 259, Ammunition](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-259.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-reactions"></a>

### Monster reactions

`srd51.creature-rules.reactions`

Use the trigger and behavior of each reaction in the stat block. A block with no Reactions section provides no special reaction. [SRD p. 259, Reactions](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-259.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-limited-usage"></a>

## Use limits and recharge

`srd51.creature-rules.limited-usage`

- **X/day.** The creature has X uses of the ability and replenishes expended uses on finishing a long rest.
- **Recharge X–Y.** The ability is usable once, after which roll a d6 at the start of each of the creature's turns in subsequent combat rounds while the use is spent. Restore the use on any result in the inclusive specified range. A completed short or long rest also restores it.
- **Recharge after a short or long rest.** The creature has one use until it finishes either kind of rest. [SRD p. 259, Limited Usage](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-259.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-grapple"></a>

## Grapples imposed by monster attacks

`srd51.creature-rules.grapple`

When a monster's attack imposes a grapple on a hit, apply the stated result without a further check unless the attack specifically requires one. To escape, the victim may use an action to make either a Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check. Use the monster's listed escape DC. If absent, use 10 + the monster's Strength (Athletics) modifier. Also apply the attack's individual restrictions and the [grappled condition](conditions.md#srd51-condition-grappled). [SRD p. 259, Grapple Rules for Monsters](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-259.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-equipment"></a>

## Creature equipment and proficiency

`srd51.creature-rules.equipment`

Assume a creature is proficient with the armor, weapons, and tools it normally uses. When the GM swaps equipment, the GM determines whether it has proficiency with each replacement. Assume appropriate clothing for creatures that customarily wear it and any material components a spellcasting creature needs for the spells in its stat block. The GM may add equipment and decides what gear can be recovered and used after a monster is slain. [SRD p. 257, Armor, Weapon, and Tool Proficiencies](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-257.md) [SRD p. 259, Equipment](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-259.md) [SRD p. 260, Equipment continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-260.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-legendary-creatures"></a>

## Legendary creatures

`srd51.creature-rules.legendary-creatures`

A legendary creature can have special actions outside its turn and environmental effects. A creature assuming a legendary creature's form does not gain that form's legendary actions, lair actions, or regional effects. [SRD p. 260, Legendary Creatures](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-260.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-legendary-actions"></a>

### Legendary actions

`srd51.creature-rules.legendary-actions`

A legendary creature's entry gives its budget and each option's cost. It may choose one option at the end of another creature's turn, spending that option's cost. It can decline. Recover spent legendary actions at the start of the legendary creature's turn. Legendary actions are unavailable while the creature is incapacitated or otherwise unable to take actions. A surprised legendary creature cannot use them before its first turn of the combat has ended. [SRD p. 260, Legendary Actions](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-260.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-lair-actions"></a>

### Lair actions

`srd51.creature-rules.lair-actions`

A lair section applies to a legendary creature that spends much of its time in its lair. If the creature has lair actions there, it can choose one at initiative count 20, after all creatures tied at 20. It cannot do so while incapacitated or otherwise unable to act. A surprised creature cannot use a lair action until after its first turn of combat. Apply any option restrictions in the lair's entry. [SRD p. 260, A Legendary Creature's Lair and Lair Actions](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-260.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-regional-effects"></a>

### Regional effects

`srd51.creature-rules.regional-effects`

A legendary creature's presence can affect the surrounding region as its entry describes. When the creature dies, follow that entry's rules for the effects ending immediately or fading over time. [SRD p. 260, Regional Effects](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-260.md)

## Shared named creature traits

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-amphibious"></a>

### Amphibious

`srd51.creature-rules.amphibious`

The creature can breathe air and water. [SRD p. 261, Aboleth, Amphibious](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-261.md) [SRD p. 280, Ancient Black Dragon, Amphibious](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-280.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-magic-resistance"></a>

### Magic Resistance

`srd51.creature-rules.magic-resistance`

For this creature's saves, a spell or other magical effect qualifies the roll for advantage. [SRD p. 262, Deva, Magic Resistance](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-262.md) [SRD p. 270, Balor, Magic Resistance](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-270.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-magic-weapons"></a>

### Magic Weapons

`srd51.creature-rules.magic-weapons`

The creature’s weapon attacks are magical. A profile can add a separate damage rider or a specific restriction. [SRD p. 269, Couatl, Magic Weapons](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-269.md) [SRD p. 270, Balor, Magic Weapons](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-270.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-false-appearance"></a>

### False Appearance

`srd51.creature-rules.false-appearance`

While the creature remains motionless and meets any other stated requirement, it is indistinguishable from the appearance specified in its entry. [SRD p. 264, Animated Objects, False Appearance](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-264.md) [SRD p. 268, Cloaker, False Appearance](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-268.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-keen-senses"></a>

### Keen Senses

`srd51.creature-rules.keen-senses`

Grant advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks relying on a sense named by the trait, such as hearing, sight, or smell. Each profile specifies the senses it enhances. [SRD p. 401, Scout, Keen Hearing and Sight](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-401.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-pack-tactics"></a>

### Pack Tactics

`srd51.creature-rules.pack-tactics`

Check the target's surroundings when attacking a creature. If an ally of the attacker is within 5 feet of that target and lacks the incapacitated condition, apply advantage to the attack roll. [SRD p. 402, Thug, Pack Tactics](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-402.md) [SRD p. 403, Tribal Warrior, Pack Tactics](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-403.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-siege-monster"></a>

### Siege Monster

`srd51.creature-rules.siege-monster`

The creature deals twice its damage to objects and structures. [SRD p. 306, Earth Elemental, Siege Monster](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-306.md) [SRD p. 324, Kraken, Siege Monster](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-324.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-legendary-resistance"></a>

### Legendary Resistance

`srd51.creature-rules.legendary-resistance`

After failing a saving throw, the creature can spend one of its uses to succeed instead. Use the profile’s stated daily capacity and the [X/day rule](#srd51-creature-rules-limited-usage). [SRD p. 280, Ancient Black Dragon, Legendary Resistance](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-280.md) [SRD p. 259, Limited Usage](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-259.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-devils-sight"></a>

### Devil’s Sight

`srd51.creature-rules.devils-sight`

A creature with this stat-block trait can use its darkvision through magical darkness. The profile’s darkvision range still applies. [SRD p. 274, Barbed Devil, Devil’s Sight](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-274.md) [SRD p. 277, Imp, Devil’s Sight](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-277.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-brute"></a>

### Brute

`srd51.creature-rules.brute`

On a hit using a weapon in the [melee weapon category](equipment.md#srd51-equipment-weapons), add one die of that weapon’s damage. Use the stat block’s listed damage for each mode of its profiled attacks. Those entries incorporate the trait where applicable. A [thrown](equipment.md#srd51-equipment-thrown) melee weapon remains in that category when used for a ranged attack. [SRD p. 266, Bugbear, Brute](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-266.md) [SRD p. 399, Gladiator, Brute](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-399.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-parry"></a>

### Parry

`srd51.creature-rules.parry`

When a melee attack would hit the creature, it can spend its reaction to increase its AC by the listed bonus against that attack. It must be able to see the attacker and wield a melee weapon. [SRD p. 272, Marilith, Parry](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-272.md) [SRD p. 276, Erinyes, Parry](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-276.md) [SRD p. 397, Bandit Captain, Parry](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-397.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-damage-transfer"></a>

### Damage Transfer

`srd51.creature-rules.damage-transfer`

While the profile’s attachment or grapple persists, divide damage dealt to the monster between the monster and the linked creature. **Project interpretation.** Apply the monster’s [damage adjustments](combat.md#srd51-core-damage-resistance-vulnerability) first. Split each remaining typed amount, rounding the monster’s half down and transferring the remainder with its original damage type. Apply the linked creature’s own damage adjustments to its share. Then each recipient applies its [temporary hit points](combat.md#srd51-core-temporary-hit-points) and current hit points to the damage it takes. The cloaker explicitly rounds down its own half. Using the remainder for the other half preserves every odd point at the split. A split before the first recipient’s damage adjustments could transfer damage that never reached the monster, and splitting after temporary HP would treat those points as a reduction in damage dealt instead of a buffer. Apply each creature’s damage adjustments only once. [SRD p. 264, Rug of Smothering, Damage Transfer](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-264.md) [SRD p. 268, Cloaker, Damage Transfer](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-268.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-infernal-wound"></a>

### Infernal wound

`srd51.creature-rules.infernal-wound`

When the named attack hits a creature other than an undead or construct, the creature must succeed on the attack's Constitution save or suffer an infernal wound. At the start of each of its turns, the creature loses the listed wound hit points. Each later hit against that wounded target with the same attack increases the amount of hit point loss by the amount in the profile. A creature can use an action and succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom (Medicine) check to stanch the wound. The wound also closes when the target receives magical healing. [SRD p. 274, Bearded Devil, Glaive](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-274.md) [SRD p. 275, Glaive continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-275.md) [SRD p. 276, Horned Devil, Tail](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-276.md)

**Project interpretation.** Reduce current hit points directly. The primary periodic effect says the target loses hit points and specifies no damage type, although the stacking clause calls the wound's increase damage. Treating that shorthand as ordinary damage would instead let temporary hit points absorb it and would require deciding its type. Direct loss bypasses temporary hit points, damage resistance and immunity, and damage-triggered concentration checks. If it brings the target to 0, use the ordinary 0-hit-point state and monster death guidance. It does not inflict damage overflow, instant death from massive damage, or failed death saves for damage while at 0. [SRD p. 274, Bearded Devil, Glaive](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-274.md) [SRD p. 276, Horned Devil, Tail](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-276.md) [SRD p. 97, Dropping to 0 Hit Points](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-097.md) [SRD p. 98, Instant Death, Damage at 0 Hit Points, and Temporary Hit Points](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-098.md) [SRD p. 102, Concentration](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-102.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-damage-absorption"></a>

### Damage Absorption

`srd51.creature-rules.damage-absorption`

When the creature would take damage of the type named by its feature, replace that typed amount with healing equal to the amount. Apply any unrelated damage normally. **Project interpretation.** Compute the amount before applying the profile’s matching damage immunity or temporary HP. Matching immunity would otherwise zero the same damage the trait uses to calculate healing. Apply the normal HP maximum to the healing and leave temporary HP intact for the damage absorbed. [SRD p. 316, Clay Golem, Acid Absorption and Flesh Golem, Lightning Absorption](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-316.md) [SRD p. 317, Iron Golem, Fire Absorption](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-317.md) [SRD p. 345, Shambling Mound, Lightning Absorption](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-345.md)

## Source-checked cases

- **Ordinary.** A CR 9 creature has +4 proficiency bonus and is worth 5,000 XP. A printed +7 skill bonus already includes proficiency where relevant. [SRD p. 256, Proficiency Bonus by Challenge Rating and Skills](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-256.md) [SRD p. 258, Experience Points by Challenge Rating](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-258.md)
- **Boundary.** A used Recharge 5–6 action returns at the start of a subsequent turn on a d6 result of 5 or 6. A result of 4 leaves it spent. A finished short or long rest also restores it. [SRD p. 259, Limited Usage](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-259.md)
- **Exception.** An ordinary creature that magically assumes the form of a legendary creature cannot take its legendary or lair actions or produce its regional effects. [SRD p. 260, Legendary Creatures](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-260.md)
- **Invalid.** A monster cannot take its Multiattack action as an opportunity attack. An incorporeal creature is also outside the detection capabilities of tremorsense. [SRD p. 257, Tremorsense](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-257.md) [SRD p. 259, Multiattack](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-259.md)
- **Transfer.** A rug grappling a creature takes a 9-point damage amount with no damage adjustments of its own. Its share is 4 and the grappled creature’s share is 5 of the same type. If the creature resists that type and has 1 temporary HP, it takes 2 after resistance and loses 1 temporary HP and 1 current HP. [SRD p. 264, Rug of Smothering, Damage Transfer](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-264.md) [SRD p. 97, Damage Resistance and Vulnerability](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-097.md) [SRD p. 98, Temporary Hit Points](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-098.md)
- **Absorption.** If an iron golem would take 12 fire damage and has room to heal 7 HP, it regains 7 HP and takes no fire damage. [SRD p. 317, Iron Golem, Fire Absorption](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-317.md)
