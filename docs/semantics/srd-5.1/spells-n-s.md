# Spells N–S

Each spell's casting line gives level and school, casting time, range, components, and duration in
that order. V, S, and M mean verbal, somatic, and material. A listed price requires the named
material, and materials are consumed only where specified. A ritual tag uses the general ritual
rules. Unmodified casting and resolution use the shared
[casting procedure](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-cast-spell),
[slots](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-slots),
[components](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-components),
[range](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-range),
[targets](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-targets),
[areas](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-area),
[spell saves](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-saving-throw),
[spell attack rolls](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-attack-roll),
[duration](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-duration), and
[concentration](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-concentration) rules.
HP means hit points, AC means Armor Class, and CR means challenge rating.
Find spell names and class access lists in the [spell index](spell-index.md).

## Nondetection

`srd51.spell.nondetection`

Level 3 abjuration · 1 action · Touch · V, S, M (diamond dust worth 25 gp sprinkled over the target, consumed) · 8 hours.

Touch a willing creature, a place, or an object. A place or object can measure at most 10 feet in
every dimension. During the ward the subject cannot be targeted by divination magic or perceived

Level 2 abjuration · 1 action · Self · V, S, M (ashes from a burned mistletoe leaf and a spruce sprig) · Concentration up to 1 hour.

<a id="srd51-spell-planar-ally"></a>

## Planar Ally

`srd51.spell.planar-ally`

Level 6 conjuration · 10 minutes · 60 feet · V, S · Instantaneous.

Petition a known entity of cosmic power. It sends a loyal celestial, elemental, or fiend into an
unoccupied space in range. At casting, you may request a specific creature by name, but the DM can
send a different one. The creature is not compelled to help. You must be able to communicate with it
to bargain. It can accept or refuse service and may ask for money, treasure, a donation or sacrifice,
a quest, or other payment.

Use 100 gp per minute, 1,000 gp per hour, or 10,000 gp per day for a task of up to 10 days as
bargaining guides. The DM can adjust the price. Work in keeping with the creature's ethos can cost
half or be free. Harmless work typically costs half. Particularly hazardous work may cost more, and it
rarely accepts a seemingly suicidal task.

Failure to agree on payment makes it return home immediately. After completing its task or reaching
its agreed service deadline, it reports back if suitable and possible and then goes to its home plane.
If enlisted as a member of the group, it receives a full share of the experience awarded.

[SRD p. 168, Planar Ally](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-168.md)

<a id="srd51-spell-planar-binding"></a>

## Planar Binding

`srd51.spell.planar-binding`

Level 5 abjuration · 1 hour · 60 feet · V, S, M (a jewel worth at least 1,000 gp, consumed) · 24 hours.

Select a celestial, elemental, fey, or fiend. It must remain in range throughout casting, for example
in a reversed [Magic Circle](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-magic-circle). At completion it makes a
Charisma save and is bound on failure. If another spell summoned or created it, extend
that spell to last at least through the binding. **Project interpretation.** An already later
expiry stays in place because the rule extends the duration. Any original concentration
requirement continues. Unlike a Spell Glyph in [Glyph of
Warding](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-glyph-of-warding), this rule says nothing about removing that
requirement.

The creature follows your instructions to the best of its ability and observes their literal terms. A
hostile one tries to bend their wording to serve its own interests. If it finishes the task before
expiry and you are on its plane, it travels to report to you. If you are elsewhere, it returns to
where it was bound and waits until expiry. Slots 6, 7, 8, and 9 extend the duration to 10 days, 30
days, 180 days, and a year and a day respectively.

[SRD p. 168, Planar Binding](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-168.md) · [SRD p. 160, Magic Circle](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-160.md) · [SRD p. 149, Glyph of Warding](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-149.md) · [SRD p. 102, Concentration](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-102.md)

## Polymorph

`srd51.spell.polymorph`

Level 4 transmutation · 1 action · 60 feet · V, S, M (a caterpillar cocoon) · Concentration up to 1 hour.

Choose one creature you can see in range. Shapechangers and creatures at 0 HP are unaffected. An
unwilling target can avoid transformation on a successful Wisdom save. Choose a beast form whose CR is
at most the target's CR, using target level if it has no CR.

Replace all game statistics including mental scores with the form's statistics. The target keeps its
alignment and personality and adopts the beast's HP. Its form constrains actions and it cannot speak,
cast spells, or perform other actions needing hands or speech. Its equipment merges into the form and
gives no benefit or ability to wield, use, or activate it.

The target reverts at 0 HP, death, or spell end. Restore the HP it had before transforming and apply
any excess damage from reducing the form to 0. Reaching 0 in beast form does not cause unconsciousness
if the damage carried into the original form leaves it above 0.

[SRD p. 169, Polymorph](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-169.md)

## Prismatic Wall

`srd51.spell.prismatic-wall`

Level 9 abjuration · 1 action · 60 feet · V, S · 10 minutes.

Choose between an opaque vertical wall and a sphere. The wall can measure up to 30 feet in height,
90 feet in length, and 1 inch in thickness, and its center must be a visible point in range. A sphere
can be at most 30 feet across and can be centered on any point in range. The effect stays in place. Intersecting a creature's occupied space makes the cast fail, wasting both slot and
action.

The wall sheds bright light for 100 feet and dim light for 100 more. You and creatures you designate
at casting may pass through it and stay near it harmlessly. Another creature that sees the wall and
moves within 20 feet of it or starts its turn there makes a Constitution save. Failure blinds it for 1
minute.

Reaching into or passing through the wall encounters layers one at a time. Make a Dexterity save per
layer encountered. Destruction also progresses layer by layer, in red-to-violet order. A destroyed
layer stays gone until expiry. [Antimagic Field](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-antimagic-field) has no
effect on the wall. A rod of cancellation destroys the whole wall. **Evidence gap.** The local SRD
refers to this rod here and gives no item definition for it.

- **Red.** 10d6 fire damage, halved on success. Blocks nonmagical ranged attacks. At least 25 cold
  damage destroys this layer.
- **Orange.** 10d6 acid damage, halved on success. Blocks magical ranged attacks. Strong wind destroys
  this layer.
- **Yellow.** 10d6 lightning damage, halved on success. At least 60 force damage destroys this layer.
- **Green.** 10d6 poison damage, halved on success. [Passwall](#srd51-spell-passwall), or an equal- or
  higher-level spell that opens a portal on a solid surface, destroys this layer.
- **Blue.** 10d6 cold damage, halved on success. At least 25 fire damage destroys this layer.
- **Indigo.** Apply the [indigo rider](#srd51-spell-prismatic-riders) on failure. This layer blocks
  spell casting through the wall. Bright light from [Daylight](spells-d-e.md#srd51-spell-daylight) or
  a similar spell of at least that level destroys this layer.
- **Violet.** Apply the [violet rider](#srd51-spell-prismatic-riders) on failure. [Dispel
  Magic](spells-d-e.md#srd51-spell-dispel-magic) or similar magic of at least that level that ends
  spells and magical effects destroys this layer.

**Project interpretation.** Evaluate damage thresholds on a qualifying damage event. The layer rules
give destruction thresholds and supply no pool of layer hit points. Accumulating small hits would
require an additional persistent damage state.

<a id="srd51-spell-prismatic-riders"></a>

### Prismatic riders

`srd51.spell.prismatic-riders`

- **Indigo.** Restrain the creature. It makes a Constitution save at the end of each of its turns.
  Count successes and failures separately without requiring a streak. Three successes end the effect.
  Three failures petrify the creature permanently.
- **Violet.** Blind the creature until it makes a Wisdom save at the start of your next turn. Success
  ends the blindness. Failure also ends the blindness and transports it to a different plane chosen by
  the DM. A typical destination for an extraplanar creature is home, while the Astral or Ethereal
  Plane is typical for a creature on its home plane.

**Project interpretation.** Indigo's success ends that creature's indigo effect. The source says the
spell ends, but Spray can affect multiple creatures and Wall has a separate ordered layer-destruction
rule. Ending the entire casting would undo independent riders or collapse the whole wall when one
creature saved.

[SRD p. 170, Prismatic Spray](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-170.md) · [SRD p. 171, Prismatic
Spray continuation and Prismatic Wall](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-171.md).

## Programmed Illusion

`srd51.spell.programmed-illusion`

Level 6 illusion · 1 action · 120 feet · V, S, M (a bit of fleece and jade dust worth at least 25 gp) · Until dispelled.

Place an imperceptible illusion in range depicting a creature, object, or other visible phenomenon
fitting a 30-foot cube. Set a script of behavior and sounds lasting up to 5 minutes and a trigger

detailed.

When triggered, the illusion appears and performs its script. It disappears at the end of the
performance, waits dormant for 10 minutes, and can then trigger again. Things pass through it,

## Project Image

<a id="srd51-spell-shapechange"></a>

## Shapechange

`srd51.spell.shapechange`

Level 9 transmutation · 1 action · Self · V, S, M (a jade circlet worth at least 1,500 gp worn on your head before casting) · Concentration up to 1 hour.

Choose a creature kind you have seen with CR no greater than your level. Constructs and undead are
ineligible. Become an average specimen with no class levels or Spellcasting trait. Its statistics
replace yours except as specified below.

- **Retained statistics and abilities.** Keep alignment and Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores.
  Keep your skill and save proficiencies and gain the form's. For any shared proficiency, use the
  greater listed bonus. Keep features from your class, race, and other sources when your form can
  physically use them. Use special senses only if the form has them and speak only if the form
  normally can. You cannot use the form's legendary or lair actions.
- **Hit points.** Adopt the form's HP and Hit Dice. On reversion, restore the HP you had before
  transformation. If dropping to 0 in the form causes reversion, carry excess damage into your normal
  form. You avoid unconsciousness from that transition if the normal form remains above 0 HP.
- **Equipment.** For each item choose to drop, merge, or wear it. Worn gear functions normally. The DM
  decides wearability by shape and size. Equipment retains its size and shape. Anything the form
  cannot wear must drop or merge, and merged gear provides no effect.
- **Changing again.** Spend an action during the spell to assume a different eligible form under the
  same rules. If the new form would give more HP than you currently have, keep your current HP
  instead.

**Project interpretation.** Revert immediately when the form reaches 0 HP, applying its
stated excess-damage rule, or when the spell ends. The source expressly anticipates reversion
from 0 HP and gives no procedure for persisting in a 0 HP form. Concentration ending through
expiry, interruption, or death also ends the transformation. A creature already killed by
an effect that bypassed 0 HP returns to its normal form dead.

Level 3 necromancy · 1 action · 10 feet · V, S, M (burning incense) · 10 minutes.

Choose a corpse in range that still has a mouth and is not undead. The spell fails if that corpse was
targeted by it within the last 10 days. The body's animating spirit answers up to five questions
before expiry. Its soul does not return. The corpse knows only what the creature knew while alive,
including its languages. It learns nothing new, knows nothing of events since death, and cannot
speculate about the future.

Expect short, cryptic, or repeated answers. The corpse is free to lie if it knows you as an enemy
or if you treat it with hostility.

[SRD p. 181, Speak with Dead](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-181.md)

## Speak with Plants

`srd51.spell.speak-with-plants`

Level 3 transmutation · 1 action · Self (30-foot radius) · V, S · 10 minutes.

Give plants within 30 feet limited awareness and movement, letting them communicate with you and
follow simple commands. Ask about creatures that passed, weather, and other events in the area over
the previous day. For the duration you can make difficult terrain caused by plant growth ordinary, or

Plants can move their branches, tendrils, and stalks, but cannot uproot and relocate themselves. The
DM may allow other tasks. You can speak to a plant creature inside as if sharing a language but gain
no magical control over it. This spell can make plants from

## Storm of Vengeance

`srd51.spell.storm-of-vengeance`

Level 9 conjuration · 1 action · Sight · V, S · Concentration up to 1 minute.

Center a storm with a 360-foot radius on a point you can see. At appearance each creature under it and
at most 5,000 feet below it makes a Constitution save. Failure deals 2d6 thunder damage and deafens it
for 5 minutes. The following effects occur on your turn in the stated rounds while you maintain
concentration.

- **Round 2.** All creatures and objects below take 1d6 acid damage.
- **Round 3.** Aim six lightning bolts at distinct creatures or objects below. A struck creature makes
  a Dexterity save against 10d6 lightning damage, taking half on success.
- **Round 4.** Each creature below takes 2d6 bludgeoning damage from hail.
- **Rounds 5–10.** Each creature below takes 1d6 cold damage per round. Freezing rain and wind make
  the area difficult terrain and heavily obscure it. Ranged weapon attacks there are impossible. The
  wind and rain are a severe distraction for concentration. Gusts of 20 to 50 miles per hour
  automatically disperse fog, mist, and similar phenomena, including magical ones.

**Project interpretation.** Use the stated 5,000-foot depth throughout the storm. The opening clause
supplies the meaning of being under the cloud, and the later effects reuse that area. On round 3,
allow any unused bolts to go unassigned if there are fewer than six eligible targets. A selected
object takes the stated 10d6 lightning damage without the creature's save. The source expressly
permits object targets but describes the damage through the creature's save. Leaving objects
unaffected would give that selection no consequence.

[SRD p. 183, Storm of Vengeance](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-183.md) · [SRD p. 102, Concentration](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-102.md)

<a id="srd51-spell-symbol"></a>

## Symbol

`srd51.spell.symbol`

Level 7 abjuration · 1 minute · Touch · V, S, M (mercury, phosphorus, powdered diamond, and powdered opal worth at least 1,000 gp total, consumed) · Until dispelled or triggered.

Inscribe a nearly invisible glyph on a surface within a diameter of 10 feet or conceal it inside a
closable object. Moving a containing object more than 10 feet from the casting location breaks the
glyph and ends the spell without activation. Finding it requires a successful Intelligence
(Investigation) check against your spell save DC.

Define its trigger and choose the effect at casting. Surface triggers can include touching or stepping
on it, removing its cover, approaching, or manipulating the inscribed object. Container triggers can
include opening, approaching, seeing, or reading. You can refine the trigger by circumstances,
physical traits, or physical kind. You can exempt creatures from triggering it, such as those who
speak a password.

Once activated, it emits dim light in a 60-foot-radius sphere for 10 minutes and then ends. Apply the
selected effect to each creature inside on activation, on its first entry in a turn, or on ending its
turn inside. Trigger exemptions govern activation. They do not themselves confer immunity to the
effect of an activated glyph.

- **Death.** Constitution save against 10d10 necrotic damage, halved on success.
- **Discord.** Constitution save. On failure, spend 1 minute bickering, unable to communicate
  meaningfully and at disadvantage on attacks and ability checks.
- **Fear.** Failure on Wisdom causes 1 minute of fright. The target drops what it is holding. On
  its own turns it must travel away from the glyph if able, with 30 feet as the minimum movement.
- **Hopelessness.** Failure on Charisma prevents attacking for 1 minute and bars choosing a
  creature as the target of any harmful spell, ability, or magical effect during that time.
- **Insanity.** Intelligence save. On failure, spend 1 minute unable to take actions, understand
  speech, or read, speaking only gibberish. The DM controls the target's erratic movement.
- **Pain.** Constitution save. On failure, incapacitated for 1 minute.
- **Sleep.** Wisdom save. On failure, unconscious for 10 minutes unless damaged or shaken or slapped
  awake by someone spending an action.
- **Stunning.** Wisdom save. On failure, stunned for 1 minute.

[SRD p. 184, Symbol](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-184.md) · [SRD p. 185, Symbol continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-185.md)
