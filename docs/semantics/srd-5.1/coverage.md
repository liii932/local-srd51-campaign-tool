# Source coverage and review

The source is the repository's 403-page [SRD 5.1 reference](../../references/srd-5.1/README.md).
Page numbers here are PDF page numbers. Entries that cross page boundaries stay
with their rule family; a range ending on a page does not discard its continuation.
The page-based source index supplies discovery hints, not proof that every rule
or table cell has been interpreted.

## Exclusions and catalogue reconciliation

Pages 1 and 2 contain licensing and the blank-page record. Page
[23, Druids and the Gods](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-023.md), contains
characterization and examples of religious traditions without additional game
procedures. Its disposition is also recorded in the
[Druid source map](classes-cleric-druid.md#source-coverage). The other 400 pages
from 3 through 403 are cited by substantive definitions. Family source maps
identify further narrative-only omissions within mixed pages; descriptions that
change eligibility, physical interaction, sensing, or other behavior are retained.

| Inventory | Reconciled destinations |
|---|---|
| Spells | [319 named spells](spell-index.md), plus every class spell-list membership and level in [spell lists](spell-lists.md). |
| Magic items | [240 primary item entries](magic-item-index.md), retaining named variants and the Orb of Dragonkind. |
| Creatures | [317 primary profiles and 2 embedded profiles](creature-index.md): Avatar of Death and Giant Fly. Shared traits, templates, variants, and individual restrictions have their own definitions. |
| Character classes | All 12 classes and their locally defined subclasses, features, progression, and spellcasting dependencies. Named but unavailable subclasses are recorded as source gaps. |
| Deities | All 67 entries, including alignment, suggested domains, and symbols, in [cosmology](cosmology.md). |

## Acceptance record

Detailed specification drafting was delegated to three authors using
`gpt-6.1-sol` with `xhigh` reasoning. Assignments divided shared rules, mundane
equipment and hazards; character acquisition and features; spells, magic items,
and creatures into owned files and dependency-aware batches. The main agent
owned conventions, the inventory and indexes, inspected the actual drafts and
relevant source passages, returned substantive defects for correction, reviewed
the revisions, and completed integration.

The source review covered the entire 403-page reference, including adjacent
pages where entries continued. Local PDF inspection resolved ambiguous
extraction, column order, tables, and footnotes. Review checked values,
restrictions, optionality, normal and exceptional outcomes, recovery, and the
lifecycle of ongoing effects. Numerical and categorical source facts are
retained; explanatory prose and table organization are independently composed.

The early integration case used [Ready](combat.md#srd51-core-ready),
[concentration](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-concentration), and
[incapacitation](conditions.md#srd51-condition-incapacitated). A held spell pays
its casting costs, requires concentration before release, and loses that held
energy if concentration ends. This exercised a triggered action, paid resources,
a shared ongoing obligation, and automatic interruption across documents. Each
family also includes ordinary, boundary, exceptional, and invalid-use examples
checked against the cited source.

Documentation validation completed on 2026-11-07 checks:

- 48 Markdown documents, 3,540 unique semantic definitions, and their unique
  explicit anchors.
- Relative file links and anchor fragments, including links to the local source.
- Forward and reverse spell, magic-item, and creature catalogue reconciliation.
- Disposition of all 403 pages, distinguishing substantive citations from
  narrative and licensing exclusions.
- Every labeled interpretation's index destination and all 12 evidence-gap
  records: 10 missing-material records and 2 source defects with working readings.
- Shared-rule references, exceptions, and examples through source review, plus
  phrase-match inspection for copied passages. Technical terms, names, numerical
  expressions, components, and categorical facts are retained as necessary;
  these are not treated as independently expressive source prose.

Link counts and phrase matching are editorial aids, not proof of semantic truth.
Acceptance rests on the source review as well as those structural checks.
Semantic completion and source review are complete for the available local
source. The [gap register](interpretations.md) identifies the exact remaining
source limits. Runtime support remains a separate implementation assessment.
