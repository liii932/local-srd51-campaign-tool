# Backgrounds and personality

<a id="srd51-character-alignment"></a>

## Alignment

`srd51.character.alignment`

Alignment describes typical moral and social attitudes. One axis is good, neutral, or evil, and the other is lawful, neutral, or chaotic. Their combinations produce nine alignments. Individuals can depart from the behavior typical of their alignment. [SRD p. 58, Alignment](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-058.md) [SRD p. 59, Alignment continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-059.md)

| Morality | Lawful | Neutral on order | Chaotic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Good | **Lawful good (LG).** Does the right thing in keeping with social expectations. | **Neutral good (NG).** Tries to help people according to their needs. | **Chaotic good (CG).** Follows conscience despite social expectations. |
| Neutral | **Lawful neutral (LN).** Follows law, tradition, or a personal code. | **Neutral (N).** Avoids taking moral sides and acts as circumstances warrant. | **Chaotic neutral (CN).** Values personal freedom above other commitments and follows personal whims. |
| Evil | **Lawful evil (LE).** Pursues self-interest methodically within the limits of a code, loyalty, or order. | **Neutral evil (NE).** Does what it can get away with, without compassion or qualms. | **Chaotic evil (CE).** Acts with arbitrary violence driven by greed, hatred, or bloodlust. |

Thinking creatures such as humans, dwarves, and elves can choose moral and ethical paths. Ancestries may have strong inborn tendencies without determining each individual's choice. Alignment can be essential to celestials and fiends. For example, ceasing to be lawful evil would end a devil's nature as a devil. Most creatures incapable of rational moral choice are unaligned. [SRD p. 59, Alignment in the Multiverse](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-059.md)

## Languages

`srd51.character.languages`

Record the languages granted by ancestry, background, or another rule. For an open language choice, choose a standard language or one common in the campaign. An exotic or secret language, such as [Thieves' Cant](classes-rogue-sorcerer.md#srd51-class-rogue-thieves-cant) or [Druidic](classes-cleric-druid.md#srd51-class-druid-druidic), may be chosen with the GM's permission. Typical speakers are descriptive. A shared script does not grant understanding of another language. Dialects within one language family are mutually communicable. Primordial includes Auran, Aquan, Ignan, and Terran, associated with the four elemental planes. [SRD p. 59, Languages, Standard Languages, and Exotic Languages](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-059.md)

<a id="srd51-character-inspiration"></a>

## Inspiration

`srd51.character.inspiration`

The GM may award inspiration for roleplaying true to the character's personality traits, ideal, bond, or flaw, including accepting a drawback those characteristics create. The GM tells the players how it can be earned. Each character either has inspiration or lacks it, with at most one held. [SRD p. 59, Inspiration](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-059.md) [SRD p. 60, Gaining Inspiration](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-060.md)

When making an attack roll, saving throw, or ability check, you may spend held inspiration to gain [advantage](abilities.md#srd51-core-advantage-disadvantage) on that roll. You may also give up your inspiration to give it to another player's character in recognition of that player's contribution to play, such as good roleplaying or clever thinking. That character's capacity remains one. [SRD p. 60, Using Inspiration and Gaining Inspiration](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-060.md)

<a id="srd51-background-selection"></a>

## Choosing a background

`srd51.background.selection`

A background describes prior life and how the character became an adventurer. It supplies two skill proficiencies, often one or more tool proficiencies, sometimes additional languages, an equipment package, and a feature where described. If two sources would grant the same skill or tool proficiency, the player may choose a different proficiency of that same kind instead. The suggested personality material can be chosen, rolled randomly, or used as a starting point for original characteristics. [SRD p. 60, Backgrounds, Proficiencies, Languages, Equipment, and Suggested Characteristics](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-060.md)

<a id="srd51-background-customization"></a>

### Customizing a Background

`srd51.background.customization`

Replace one background feature with another, choose any two skills, and choose a combined total of two tool proficiencies or languages from the sample backgrounds. If no existing feature fits, work with the GM to create one. Choose two personality traits, one ideal, one bond, and one flaw. [SRD p. 60, Customizing a Background](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-060.md)

Use the background equipment package or the optional approach of spending starting coin on gear. Choosing the coin approach also forgoes the class equipment package. **Evidence gap.** The background section points to Equipment for this approach. The local equipment section supplies item prices and money denominations, but provides no class starting coin allocations for that choice. [SRD p. 60, Equipment and Customizing a Background](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-060.md) [SRD p. 62, Equipment and Coinage](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-062.md)

<a id="srd51-background-acolyte"></a>

## Acolyte

`srd51.background.acolyte`

Choose a god, pantheon, or other quasi-divine being from the pantheon material or as specified by the GM. Work with the GM to describe your service and religious community. Your background can involve conducting sacred rites and sacrifices regardless of class. [SRD p. 60, Acolyte](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-060.md) [SRD p. 61, Acolyte continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-061.md)

Gain proficiency in Insight and Religion and choose two languages. The package gives you a holy symbol, a prayer book or prayer wheel, five sticks of incense, vestments, common clothes, and a pouch with 15 gp. [SRD p. 61, Acolyte proficiencies, languages, and equipment](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-061.md)

<a id="srd51-background-acolyte-shelter-of-the-faithful"></a>

### Shelter of the Faithful

`srd51.background.acolyte.shelter-of-the-faithful`

You are respected by followers of your faith and can conduct your deity's religious ceremonies. You and your adventuring companions can expect free healing and care at an established presence of the faith, such as a temple or shrine. Supply material components that any spells require. Fellow worshippers support you at a modest lifestyle. This lifestyle benefit applies only to you. [SRD p. 61, Shelter of the Faithful](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-061.md)

If you have ties and a residence at a particular temple of your deity or pantheon, you can request assistance from its priests when nearby. You must remain in good standing, and the assistance requested must be safe for them. Your temple may be a former place of service where you remain welcome or a new religious home. [SRD p. 61, Shelter of the Faithful](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-061.md)

<a id="srd51-background-acolyte-suggested-characteristics"></a>

### Suggested Characteristics

`srd51.background.acolyte.suggested-characteristics`

Use these suggestions as choices, die results, or inspiration for your own characteristics. The position in each list is the result of the indicated die. Alignment labels on ideals are retained as guidance. [SRD p. 60, Suggested Characteristics](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-060.md) [SRD p. 61, Acolyte Suggested Characteristics](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-061.md)

**Personality trait (d8).**

1. Constantly point to a hero of the faith and their deeds as an example.
2. Find common ground even among bitter enemies and work toward peace.
3. Look for divine omens in everything that happens.
4. Remain optimistic through every setback.
5. Apply sacred quotations or proverbs, sometimes incorrectly, to almost every situation.
6. Treat other faiths and their worship with respect, or with intolerance and condemnation.
7. Prefer the fine food, drink, and company of the temple elite and resent rough living.
8. Have little experience with people outside the temple after spending so long inside.

**Ideal (d6).**

1. **Tradition (Lawful).** Preserve old forms of worship and sacrifice.
2. **Charity (Good).** Aid people in need whatever it costs personally.
3. **Change (Chaotic).** Help realize the changes the gods are making in the world.
4. **Power (Lawful).** Rise to the top of the religious hierarchy.
5. **Faith (Lawful).** Trust the deity for guidance and expect hard work to yield good results.
6. **Aspiration (Any).** Earn the deity's favor by measuring one's deeds against its teachings.

**Bond (d6).**

1. Recover a long-lost relic of the faith, even at the cost of one's life.
2. Take revenge on the corrupt temple hierarchy that condemned one as a heretic.
3. Repay the priest who took one in after the death of one's parents.
4. Devote all one's work to the common people.
5. Protect the temple one served at any cost.
6. Save a sacred text that enemies condemn as heretical and seek to destroy.

**Flaw (d6).**

1. Judge others harshly and oneself more harshly still.
2. Place undue trust in temple authorities.
3. Blindly trust people who claim to worship the same deity.
4. Think inflexibly.
5. Distrust strangers and assume the worst of them.
6. Pursue a chosen goal so obsessively that it crowds out everything else.

## Source-checked cases

- **Ordinary.** An acolyte chooses two standard languages and gains Insight and Religion. If already proficient in Insight from another source, the duplicated skill can be replaced with a different skill. [SRD p. 59, Languages](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-059.md) [SRD p. 60, Proficiencies](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-060.md) [SRD p. 61, Acolyte grants](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-061.md)
- **Boundary.** A character can hold one inspiration. Spending it on a saving throw gives advantage subject to the general advantage/disadvantage rules and leaves the character without inspiration. [SRD p. 60, Gaining Inspiration and Using Inspiration](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-060.md) [SRD p. 77, Advantage and Disadvantage continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-077.md)
- **Exception.** An acolyte's companions can expect free temple care and healing, subject to the needed material components, but only the acolyte receives support at a modest lifestyle. [SRD p. 61, Shelter of the Faithful](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-061.md)
- **Invalid.** Learning Goblin does not grant Dwarvish even though both use Dwarvish script. A character also cannot take the class equipment package in addition to the optional starting coin approach. [SRD p. 59, Standard Languages](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-059.md) [SRD p. 60, Customizing a Background](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-060.md)
