# Miscellaneous creatures from Hawk through Worg

These profiles use [shared creature rules](creatures.md) for attacks, recharge, senses, and normal damage resolution. Melee and ranged weapon labels specify attack type. A range pair gives normal then long range. Where an attack targets one creature, it requires a creature. Apply each action's own limits in addition to its range. Multiattack and other profiled attacks are creature actions unless a feature grants their use at another time.

<a id="srd51-creature-rules-swarm"></a>

## Swarm

`srd51.creature-rules.swarm`

A swarm and another creature can occupy each other's space. The swarm can move through an opening large enough for one Tiny constituent of the kind listed. A swarm cannot regain HP or gain temporary HP. Each swarm's attack also lists a target position, damage above half maximum HP, and damage at or below half. Where the attack lists a separate saving throw and poison amount, use that amount independently of the lower physical-damage branch. [SRD p. 389, Swarm of Bats, Swarm and Bites](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-389.md) [SRD p. 390, Swarms of Insects, Poisonous Snakes, Quippers and Rats](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-390.md) [SRD p. 391, Swarm of Ravens, Swarm and Beaks](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-391.md)

## Worked cases

- **Ordinary.** A Phase Spider spends its bonus action to shift to the Ethereal Plane. It can then use its movement on that plane. Returning to the Material Plane uses another bonus action on a later turn. [SRD p. 386, Phase Spider, Ethereal Jaunt and description](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-386.md)
- **Boundary.** A swarm of insects at 11 of its default 22 HP uses the lower 5 (2d4) damage branch of Bites. At 12 HP it uses 10 (4d4). [SRD p. 389, Swarm of Insects, profile](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-389.md) [SRD p. 390, Swarm of Insects, Bites](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-390.md)
- **Exception.** If the Swarm of Centipedes' piercing damage reduces a creature to 0 HP, the variant still stabilizes, poisons, and paralyzes that creature as specified. A later heal changes current HP and leaves the poison in place for its remaining duration. [SRD p. 390, Swarm of Insects, Bites](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-390.md) [SRD p. 391, Variant Insect Swarms, Swarm of Centipedes](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-391.md)
- **Invalid.** A target 5 feet from a swarm of ravens but outside the swarm's space fails Beaks' targeting requirement, even though the printed reach is 5 feet. A heal also cannot restore a swarm's HP. [SRD p. 391, Swarm of Ravens, Swarm and Beaks](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-391.md)
- **Terrain.** A winter wolf has advantage to hide on snowy terrain. Cold Breath instead calls for a DC 12 Dexterity save from each creature in its 15-foot cone, taking half damage on a success. [SRD p. 393, Winter Wolf, Snow Camouflage and Cold Breath](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-393.md)
