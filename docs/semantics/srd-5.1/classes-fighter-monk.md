# Fighter and Monk

Feature acquisition uses levels in the class that grants the feature.
[Proficiency bonus](abilities.md#srd51-core-proficiency-bonus) uses total character level.

<a id="srd51-class-fighter"></a>

## Fighter

`srd51.class.fighter`

Each fighter level grants one d10 Hit Die. At character level 1, maximum hit points are 10 + Constitution modifier. Each later fighter level adds 1d10 or a fixed 6, plus Constitution modifier. Starting proficiencies are all armor, shields, simple weapons, martial weapons, Strength saves, and Constitution saves. There are no starting tool proficiencies. Choose two skills from Acrobatics, Animal Handling, Athletics, History, Insight, Intimidation, Perception, and Survival. [SRD p. 24, Fighter Class Features](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-024.md)

In addition to background equipment, choose one bundle from each group. [SRD p. 24, Fighter Equipment](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-024.md)

- Chain mail, or leather armor with a longbow and 20 arrows.
- A martial weapon and shield, or two martial weapons.
- A light crossbow and 20 bolts, or two handaxes.
- A dungeoneer's pack or an explorer's pack.

<a id="srd51-class-fighter-fighting-style"></a>

### Fighting Style

`srd51.class.fighter.fighting-style`

At level 1 choose one of these styles. A style cannot be selected more than once, including through later choices. Other classes grant access only to the styles listed in their own features. [SRD p. 24, Fighting Style](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-024.md) [SRD p. 31, Paladin Fighting Style](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-031.md) [SRD p. 36, Ranger Fighting Style](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-036.md)

<a id="srd51-class-fighter-archery"></a>

#### Archery

`srd51.class.fighter.archery`

Add +2 to attack rolls made with ranged weapons. [SRD p. 24, Archery](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-024.md)

<a id="srd51-class-fighter-defense"></a>

#### Defense

`srd51.class.fighter.defense`

Add +1 to AC while wearing armor. [SRD p. 24, Defense](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-024.md)

<a id="srd51-class-fighter-dueling"></a>

#### Dueling

`srd51.class.fighter.dueling`

Add +2 to damage rolls with a melee weapon held in one hand while you wield no other weapon. [SRD p. 24, Dueling](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-024.md)

<a id="srd51-class-fighter-great-weapon-fighting"></a>

#### Great Weapon Fighting

`srd51.class.fighter.great-weapon-fighting`

When a damage die comes up 1 or 2 on an attack you make with a melee weapon held in two hands, you may reroll it and must keep the new result, even another 1 or 2. The weapon must have the two-handed or versatile property. [SRD p. 24, Great Weapon Fighting](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-024.md)

<a id="srd51-class-fighter-protection"></a>

#### Protection

`srd51.class.fighter.protection`

While wielding a shield, spend your reaction when a creature you see attacks a target other than you within 5 feet of you. The attack roll has disadvantage. [SRD p. 24, Protection](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-024.md)

<a id="srd51-class-fighter-two-weapon-fighting"></a>

#### Two-Weapon Fighting

`srd51.class.fighter.two-weapon-fighting`

When using two-weapon fighting, you may add the ability modifier to damage from the second attack. [SRD p. 24, Two-Weapon Fighting](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-024.md)

<a id="srd51-class-fighter-second-wind"></a>

### Second Wind

`srd51.class.fighter.second-wind`

From level 1, use a bonus action on your turn to regain 1d10 + fighter level hit points. Finish a short or long rest before using it again. [SRD p. 24, Second Wind](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-024.md)

<a id="srd51-class-fighter-action-surge"></a>

### Action Surge

`srd51.class.fighter.action-surge`

From level 2, spend a use to take one additional action on your turn. The extra action is in addition to your normal action and any available bonus action. A short or long rest restores expended uses. You have one use at levels 2–16 and two at level 17 and later, with at most one use on the same turn. [SRD p. 25, Action Surge](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-025.md)

<a id="srd51-class-fighter-martial-archetype"></a>

### Martial Archetype

`srd51.class.fighter.martial-archetype`

Choose an archetype at level 3. It grants features at levels 3, 7, 10, 15, and 18. The SRD details [Champion](#srd51-class-fighter-champion). [SRD p. 25, Martial Archetype and Champion](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-025.md)

<a id="srd51-class-fighter-ability-score-improvement"></a>

### Ability Score Improvement

`srd51.class.fighter.ability-score-improvement`

At levels 4, 6, 8, 12, 14, 16, and 19, apply an [Ability Score Improvement](character-advancement.md#srd51-character-ability-score-improvement). [SRD p. 25, Ability Score Improvement](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-025.md)

<a id="srd51-class-fighter-extra-attack"></a>

### Extra Attack

`srd51.class.fighter.extra-attack`

When you take the Attack action on your turn, you may make up to two attacks at levels 5–10, up to three at 11–19, and up to four at 20. [SRD p. 25, Extra Attack](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-025.md)

<a id="srd51-class-fighter-indomitable"></a>

### Indomitable

`srd51.class.fighter.indomitable`

From level 9, when you fail a saving throw you may expend a use to reroll it. The new result replaces the old. You have one use at levels 9–12, two at 13–16, and three at 17–20. Recover spent uses after a long rest. [SRD p. 25, Indomitable](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-025.md)

<a id="srd51-class-fighter-champion"></a>

### Champion

`srd51.class.fighter.champion`

Champion gives Improved Critical at 3, Remarkable Athlete at 7, Additional Fighting Style at 10, Superior Critical at 15, and Survivor at 18. [SRD p. 25, Champion](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-025.md)

<a id="srd51-class-fighter-improved-critical"></a>

#### Improved Critical

`srd51.class.fighter.improved-critical`

Starting at level 3, your weapon attacks score a critical hit on a die result of 19 or 20. [SRD p. 25, Improved Critical](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-025.md)

<a id="srd51-class-fighter-remarkable-athlete"></a>

#### Remarkable Athlete

`srd51.class.fighter.remarkable-athlete`

From level 7, you may add half your proficiency bonus, rounded up, to any Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution check that does not already use your proficiency bonus. A running long jump also adds feet equal to your Strength modifier to its possible distance. [SRD p. 25, Remarkable Athlete](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-025.md)

<a id="srd51-class-fighter-additional-fighting-style"></a>

#### Additional Fighting Style

`srd51.class.fighter.additional-fighting-style`

At level 10 choose another option from [Fighting Style](#srd51-class-fighter-fighting-style). The duplicate-choice restriction still applies. [SRD p. 24, Fighting Style](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-024.md) [SRD p. 25, Additional Fighting Style](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-025.md)

<a id="srd51-class-fighter-superior-critical"></a>

#### Superior Critical

`srd51.class.fighter.superior-critical`

From level 15, your weapon attacks score a critical hit on a die result of 18, 19, or 20. [SRD p. 25, Superior Critical](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-025.md)

<a id="srd51-class-fighter-survivor"></a>

#### Survivor

`srd51.class.fighter.survivor`

From level 18, check current hit points at the start of each of your turns. If positive and at or below half your maximum, regain 5 + Constitution modifier hit points. At 0 the feature grants no healing. [SRD p. 25, Survivor](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-025.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk"></a>

## Monk

`srd51.class.monk`

Each monk level grants one d8 Hit Die. At character level 1, maximum hit points are 8 + Constitution modifier. Each later monk level adds 1d8 or a fixed 5, plus Constitution modifier. There are no starting armor proficiencies. Gain proficiency with simple weapons, shortswords, Strength saves, Dexterity saves, and either one chosen type of artisan's tools or one chosen musical instrument. Choose two skills from Acrobatics, Athletics, History, Insight, Religion, and Stealth. [SRD p. 26, Monk Class Features](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-026.md)

In addition to background equipment, choose a shortsword or one simple weapon and a dungeoneer's pack or explorer's pack. Also receive ten darts. [SRD p. 26, Monk Equipment](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-026.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-unarmored-defense"></a>

### Unarmored Defense

`srd51.class.monk.unarmored-defense`

From level 1, if you wear no armor and wield no shield, calculate AC as 10 + Dexterity modifier + Wisdom modifier. [SRD p. 26, Unarmored Defense](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-026.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-martial-arts"></a>

### Martial Arts

`srd51.class.monk.martial-arts`

From level 1, the benefits below require you to wear no armor, wield no shield, and be unarmed or wield only monk weapons. Monk weapons are shortswords and simple melee weapons without the two-handed or heavy property. A monastery's distinctive shape or name for such a weapon uses the underlying weapon's statistics. [SRD p. 26, Martial Arts](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-026.md) [SRD p. 27, Martial Arts continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-027.md)

- Choose Dexterity in place of Strength on attack and damage rolls for unarmed strikes and monk weapons.
- You may use the Martial Arts die in place of normal damage for those attacks. The die is d4 at levels 1–4, d6 at 5–10, d8 at 11–16, and d10 from 17 onward.
- After taking the Attack action on your turn using an unarmed strike or monk weapon, you may make one unarmed strike as a bonus action. [SRD p. 26, The Monk and Martial Arts](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-026.md) [SRD p. 27, Martial Arts continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-027.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-ki"></a>

### Ki

`srd51.class.monk.ki`

From level 2, maximum ki points equal monk level. Spent points return at the end of a short or long rest if at least 30 minutes of that rest were spent meditating. For a ki effect that calls for a save, DC is 8 + proficiency bonus + Wisdom modifier. Level 2 grants Flurry of Blows, Patient Defense, and Step of the Wind, with later features adding other ways to spend ki. [SRD p. 26, The Monk](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-026.md) [SRD p. 27, Ki](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-027.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-flurry-of-blows"></a>

#### Flurry of Blows

`srd51.class.monk.flurry-of-blows`

Complete an Attack action on your turn. You may immediately follow it with a bonus action that costs 1 ki point and delivers two unarmed strikes. [SRD p. 27, Flurry of Blows](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-027.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-patient-defense"></a>

#### Patient Defense

`srd51.class.monk.patient-defense`

For 1 ki point, you can use your bonus action to Dodge. [SRD p. 27, Patient Defense](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-027.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-step-of-the-wind"></a>

#### Step of the Wind

`srd51.class.monk.step-of-the-wind`

On your turn, spend 1 ki point to use a bonus action for Disengage or Dash. Your jump distance also doubles for that turn. [SRD p. 27, Step of the Wind](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-027.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-unarmored-movement"></a>

### Unarmored Movement

`srd51.class.monk.unarmored-movement`

When wearing no armor and wielding no shield, the speed bonus by monk level is +10 feet at 2–5, +15 at 6–9, +20 at 10–13, +25 at 14–17, and +30 at 18–20. From level 9, you can travel along a vertical surface or over a liquid while moving on your turn. You stay up for the move itself. [SRD p. 26, The Monk](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-026.md) [SRD p. 27, Unarmored Movement](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-027.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-monastic-tradition"></a>

### Monastic Tradition

`srd51.class.monk.monastic-tradition`

Choose a tradition at level 3. It grants features at levels 3, 6, 11, and 17. [Way of the Open Hand](#srd51-class-monk-way-of-the-open-hand) is the tradition detailed here. [SRD p. 27, Monastic Tradition](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-027.md) [SRD p. 28, Monastic Traditions and Way of the Open Hand](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-028.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-deflect-missiles"></a>

### Deflect Missiles

`srd51.class.monk.deflect-missiles`

Starting at level 3, a hit from a ranged weapon attack lets you react. Subtract monk level + Dexterity modifier + 1d10 from its damage. If the remaining damage is zero, you may use a free hand to catch a missile that fits in it. A catch lets you pay 1 ki point to make a ranged attack with the caught missile before the reaction is over. Use proficiency with this attack and treat the missile as a monk weapon for it. Its normal range is 20 feet and its long range is 60 feet. [SRD p. 27, Deflect Missiles](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-027.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-ability-score-improvement"></a>

### Ability Score Improvement

`srd51.class.monk.ability-score-improvement`

At levels 4, 8, 12, 16, and 19, apply an [Ability Score Improvement](character-advancement.md#srd51-character-ability-score-improvement). [SRD p. 27, Ability Score Improvement](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-027.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-slow-fall"></a>

### Slow Fall

`srd51.class.monk.slow-fall`

From level 4, when you fall, you may spend a reaction to reduce any falling damage you take by 5 × monk level. [SRD p. 27, Slow Fall](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-027.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-extra-attack"></a>

### Extra Attack

`srd51.class.monk.extra-attack`

From level 5, take up to two attacks whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. [SRD p. 27, Extra Attack](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-027.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-stunning-strike"></a>

### Stunning Strike

`srd51.class.monk.stunning-strike`

From level 5, when your melee weapon attack hits another creature, you may spend 1 ki point. It makes a Constitution save against your ki save DC. On failure, it is [stunned](conditions.md#srd51-condition-stunned) through the end of your next turn. [SRD p. 27, Ki](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-027.md) [SRD p. 28, Stunning Strike](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-028.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-ki-empowered-strikes"></a>

### Ki-Empowered Strikes

`srd51.class.monk.ki-empowered-strikes`

From level 6, treat your unarmed strikes as magical for overcoming resistance or immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage. [SRD p. 28, Ki-Empowered Strikes](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-028.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-evasion"></a>

### Evasion

`srd51.class.monk.evasion`

From level 7, when an effect would deal half damage on a successful Dexterity save, you take no damage on success and half on failure. [SRD p. 28, Evasion](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-028.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-stillness-of-mind"></a>

### Stillness of Mind

`srd51.class.monk.stillness-of-mind`

From level 7, spend an action to end one effect on yourself that is making you charmed or frightened. [SRD p. 28, Stillness of Mind](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-028.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-purity-of-body"></a>

### Purity of Body

`srd51.class.monk.purity-of-body`

From level 10, you are immune to poison and disease. [SRD p. 28, Purity of Body](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-028.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-tongue-of-the-sun-and-moon"></a>

### Tongue of the Sun and Moon

`srd51.class.monk.tongue-of-the-sun-and-moon`

From level 13, understand all spoken languages. Any creature that can understand a language can understand your speech. [SRD p. 28, Tongue of the Sun and Moon](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-028.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-diamond-soul"></a>

### Diamond Soul

`srd51.class.monk.diamond-soul`

From level 14, gain proficiency in all saves. When you fail a save, you may spend 1 ki point to reroll and must take the second result. [SRD p. 28, Diamond Soul](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-028.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-timeless-body"></a>

### Timeless Body

`srd51.class.monk.timeless-body`

From level 15, you suffer no frailty from aging and cannot be aged by magic. Natural death from old age remains possible. You no longer require food or water. [SRD p. 28, Timeless Body](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-028.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-empty-body"></a>

### Empty Body

`srd51.class.monk.empty-body`

At level 18, gain two new ways to use ki. For 4 points and an action, gain a minute of [invisibility](conditions.md#srd51-condition-invisible) with resistance to damage of every type other than force for that interval. For 8 points, cast [Astral Projection](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-astral-projection) on yourself alone. This casting waives the material components. [SRD p. 28, Empty Body](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-028.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-perfect-self"></a>

### Perfect Self

`srd51.class.monk.perfect-self`

From level 20, regain 4 ki points whenever you roll initiative while you have zero ki points. [SRD p. 28, Perfect Self](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-028.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-way-of-the-open-hand"></a>

### Way of the Open Hand

`srd51.class.monk.way-of-the-open-hand`

This tradition grants Open Hand Technique at 3, Wholeness of Body at 6, Tranquility at 11, and Quivering Palm at 17. [SRD p. 28, Way of the Open Hand](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-028.md) [SRD p. 29, Way of the Open Hand continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-029.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-open-hand-technique"></a>

#### Open Hand Technique

`srd51.class.monk.open-hand-technique`

From level 3, on each hit against a creature with an attack granted by Flurry of Blows, you may impose one of these effects on that target. [SRD p. 27, Ki](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-027.md) [SRD p. 28, Open Hand Technique](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-028.md)

- The target falls prone if it fails a Dexterity save.
- The target makes a Strength save. On failure, you may push it away from you by up to 15 feet.
- The target cannot take reactions until the end of your next turn. This option requires no save.

<a id="srd51-class-monk-wholeness-of-body"></a>

#### Wholeness of Body

`srd51.class.monk.wholeness-of-body`

From level 6, spend an action to regain 3 × monk level hit points. Finish a long rest before another use. [SRD p. 28, Wholeness of Body](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-028.md) [SRD p. 29, Wholeness of Body continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-029.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-tranquility"></a>

#### Tranquility

`srd51.class.monk.tranquility`

From level 11, at the end of a long rest gain the effects of [Sanctuary](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-sanctuary) with a save DC of 8 + Wisdom modifier + proficiency bonus. The effect lasts until the start of your next long rest unless ended early under [Sanctuary](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-sanctuary)'s normal rules. [SRD p. 29, Tranquility](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-029.md)

<a id="srd51-class-monk-quivering-palm"></a>

#### Quivering Palm

`srd51.class.monk.quivering-palm`

From level 17, when your unarmed strike hits a creature, you may spend 3 ki points to start imperceptible vibrations in it. They last for a number of days equal to monk level and have no harmful effect while waiting. You may have only one creature affected at a time. You may end the vibrations harmlessly without an action. [SRD p. 29, Quivering Palm](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-029.md)

While you and the affected creature are on the same plane, you may spend your action to end the vibrations harmfully. It makes a Constitution save against your ki save DC. On failure it is reduced to 0 hit points. On success it takes 10d10 necrotic damage. [SRD p. 27, Ki](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-027.md) [SRD p. 29, Quivering Palm](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-029.md)

## Source-checked cases

- **Ordinary.** A level 11 fighter takes the Attack action for up to three attacks. Spending Action Surge to take that action again on the same turn allows up to three more. [SRD p. 25, Extra Attack and Action Surge](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-025.md)
- **Boundary.** A Champion with maximum 80 hit points and Constitution modifier +3 heals 8 at the start of a turn when at 40 hit points. At 41 or 0, Survivor does not heal them. [SRD p. 25, Survivor](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-025.md)
- **Exception.** A monk who reduces a ranged weapon hit to 0 damage and catches a one-hand-sized missile may spend 1 ki to return it with proficiency as part of the same reaction. At 25 feet this is a long-range attack. [SRD p. 27, Deflect Missiles](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-027.md)
- **Invalid.** A short rest that includes only 20 minutes of meditation does not restore a monk's expended ki. [SRD p. 27, Ki](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-027.md)
