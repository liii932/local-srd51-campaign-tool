# Magic items M–R

Each item states category, rarity, and any attunement requirement. Apply the shared
[wearing and wielding](magic-items.md#srd51-item-rules-wearing-and-wielding),
[attunement](magic-items.md#srd51-item-rules-attunement),
[activation](magic-items.md#srd51-item-rules-activation),
[consumable](magic-items.md#srd51-item-rules-consumables),
[item spell](magic-items.md#srd51-item-rules-spells), and
[charge](magic-items.md#srd51-item-rules-charges) rules except where an entry specifies a
different procedure. HP means hit points and AC means Armor Class. Find other named items
in the [magic-item index](magic-item-index.md).

## Necklace of Fireballs

`srd51.item.necklace-of-fireballs`

Wondrous item · Rare · No attunement.

The necklace has 1d6 + 3 beads. As an action detach and throw one bead up to 60 feet. At its endpoint
it bursts as a 3rd-level [Fireball](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-fireball) with save DC 15. Throw
multiple beads or the whole necklace in the same action to increase the Fireball's level by one per
additional bead. **Project interpretation.** Apply the spell's damage progression for every bead
thrown, even when the calculated effect level exceeds 9. This item defines a bead count and an
uncapped increment. It creates no spell slot, and capping the burst would discard some increments.

[SRD p. 231, Necklace of Fireballs](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-231.md) · [SRD p. 144, Fireball](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-144.md)

## Necklace of Prayer Beads

`srd51.item.necklace-of-prayer-beads`

Wondrous item · Rare · Attunement by a cleric, druid, or paladin.

The necklace holds 1d4 + 2 magic beads of aquamarine, black pearl, or topaz, among many nonmagical beads. A magic bead removed from the necklace
becomes nonmagical. The DM picks or rolls each bead type and duplicates are possible. Wear the
necklace and use a bonus action to cast the selected bead's spell, using your spell save DC when
needed. Each bead replenishes its use at the next dawn.

The d20 assignment is Blessing 1–6 ([Bless](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-bless)), Curing 7–12 (choose
2nd-level [Cure Wounds](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-cure-wounds) or [Lesser
Restoration](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-lesser-restoration)), Favor 13–16 ([Greater
Restoration](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-greater-restoration)), Smiting 17–18 ([Branding
Smite](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-branding-smite)), Summons 19 ([Planar
Ally](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-planar-ally)), and Wind Walking 20 ([Wind
Walk](spells-t-z.md#srd51-spell-wind-walk)).

<a id="srd51-item-nine-lives-stealer"></a>

## Nine Lives Stealer

`srd51.item.nine-lives-stealer`

Weapon (any sword) · Very rare · Attunement.

Gain +2 to attack and damage rolls with the sword. It has 1d8 + 1 charges. While a charge remains, a
critical hit on a creature below 100 HP makes that creature pass a DC 15 Constitution save or die
immediately. Constructs and undead are immune to this effect. Spend one charge only if the effect
kills the creature. When all charges are gone the instant-death property is lost.

**Project interpretation.** Check the surviving target's current HP after ordinary critical-hit
damage. The save is a consequence of the hit, so this follows the hit resolution before
applying the instant-death rider. Checking before damage would exempt a creature the strike
just brought below 100 HP. A target already slain by the hit does not consume a charge.

[SRD p. 231, Nine Lives Stealer](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-231.md)

<a id="srd51-item-oathbow"></a>

## Oathbow

`srd51.item.oathbow`

Weapon (longbow) · Very rare · Attunement.

Nocking an arrow makes the bow whisper “Swift defeat to my enemies” in Elvish. As part of a ranged
attack with it, you can speak the command phrase “Swift death to you who have wronged me” to make the
attack's target your sworn enemy. Only one can be marked at a time. The designation lasts through the
enemy's death or dawn seven days later. If it dies, you can choose another after the next dawn.

Against that sworn enemy, the bow grants advantage on ranged attack rolls, ignores cover short of
total cover, and removes long-range disadvantage. A hit adds 3d6 piercing damage. While the
designation is active and the enemy lives, other weapons give you disadvantage on attack rolls.

[SRD p. 231, Oathbow](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-231.md)

<a id="srd51-item-pipes-of-the-sewers"></a>

## Pipes of the Sewers

`srd51.item.pipes-of-the-sewers`

Wondrous item · Uncommon · Attunement.

Wind instrument proficiency is required for use. While attuned,
[rats](creatures-misc-h-z.md#srd51-creature-rat) and [giant
rats](creatures-misc-a-g.md#srd51-creature-giant-rat) are indifferent to you and do not attack unless
threatened or harmed. The pipes have 3 charges and regain 1d3 spent charges each dawn.

Play as an action. A bonus action can spend one to three charges to call one [swarm of
rats](creatures-misc-h-z.md#srd51-creature-swarm-of-rats) per charge. The DM determines whether enough
rats are within half a mile. A charge spent without enough rats is wasted. A called swarm takes the
shortest available route toward the music and remains uncontrolled.

When a swarm not already under another creature's control comes within 30 feet while you play, you may
contest its Wisdom check with your Charisma check. A win makes it friendly to you and your companions
and obedient to commands while you keep spending an action to play each round. Without orders it only
defends itself. On a loss it behaves normally and resists the pipes for 24 hours. A controlled swarm
starting its turn unable to hear the pipes is freed, behaves normally, and cannot be swayed by them
for 24 hours.

[SRD p. 232, Pipes of the Sewers](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-232.md) · [SRD p. 233, Pipes of the Sewers continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-233.md) · [SRD p. 387, Rat](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-387.md) · [SRD p. 378, Giant Rat](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-378.md) · [SRD p. 390, Swarm of Rats](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-390.md)

<a id="srd51-item-portable-hole"></a>

## Portable Hole

`srd51.item.portable-hole`

Wondrous item · Rare · No attunement.

The cloth unfolds to a circle 6 feet across. As an action unfold it against a solid surface, opening a
10-foot-deep cylindrical extradimensional space on another plane. It cannot form a passage through the
surface. A creature inside an open hole can climb out.

As an action fold the cloth by its edges to close the opening. The contents remain inside and do not
substantially change the folded hole's weight. A creature inside the closed hole can spend an action
on a DC 10 Strength check. On success it forces its way out and appears within 5 feet of the hole or
its carrier. A breathing creature inside the closed space begins suffocating after 10 minutes.

Putting it inside the extradimensional space of a [Bag of
Holding](magic-items-a-c.md#srd51-item-bag-of-holding), [Handy
Haversack](magic-items-d-m.md#srd51-item-handy-haversack), or similar item triggers the shared
[nesting procedure](magic-items.md#srd51-item-rules-extradimensional-nesting). It destroys both items
and pulls creatures within 10 feet through a one-way, short-lived gate to a random location on the
Astral Plane.

[SRD p. 233, Portable Hole](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-233.md) · [SRD p. 210, Bag of Holding](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-210.md) · [SRD p. 224, Handy Haversack](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-224.md) · [SRD p. 86, Suffocating](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-086.md) · [SRD p. 225, Handy Haversack continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-225.md) · [SRD p. 364, Astral Plane](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-364.md)

## Rod of Rulership

`srd51.item.rod-of-rulership`

Rod · Rare · Attunement.

Spend an action presenting it and commanding obedience from creatures you choose and can see within
120 feet. Each makes a DC 15 Wisdom save. On failure it is charmed by you for 8 hours and treats you
as a trusted leader. The charm ends for a creature if you or your companions harm it, or if it
receives an order contrary to its nature. The rod can be used again at dawn.

[SRD p. 240, Rod of Rulership](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-240.md) · [SRD p. 241, Rod of Rulership continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-241.md)

<a id="srd51-item-rod-of-security"></a>

## Rod of Security

`srd51.item.rod-of-security`

Rod · Very rare · No attunement.

Hold the rod and activate it as an action. You immediately enter an extraplanar paradise of your design.
Select as many as 199 companions to arrive with you, each visible to you and willing to go. Food and water are sufficient for visitors.
Other interactable things there only exist in that space and disappear if taken outside.

A visitor does not age there though time passes normally. Each hour it regains HP using the
[Hit Die healing calculation](exploration.md#srd51-core-short-rest). **Project interpretation.** The
rod simulates spending a die, so the visitor spends none and can heal even if all are expended.
With [multiple Hit Die sizes](character-advancement.md#srd51-character-hit-point-advancement), the
visitor chooses one of its sizes for each hour's roll. Calculate the shared stay limit by dividing 200 days by the visitor count and discarding any
fraction of a day. When the limit expires or you end the stay as an action, each visitor returns to its original
place or the closest unoccupied space. The rod cannot be used again until ten days pass.

[SRD p. 241, Rod of Security](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-241.md) · [SRD p. 57, Hit Points and Hit Dice](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-057.md) · [SRD p. 87, Short Rest](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-087.md)

<a id="srd51-item-rope-of-climbing"></a>

## Rope of Climbing

`srd51.item.rope-of-climbing`

Wondrous item · Uncommon · No attunement.

This silk rope is 60 feet long, weighs 3 pounds, and holds up to 3,000 pounds. Hold one end and speak
its command word as an action to animate it. As a bonus action command the far end toward a chosen
place. It moves 10 feet on that turn and each of your turns thereafter until it arrives, extends its
full length, or you tell it to stop. You can also tell it to fasten securely, unfasten, tie or untie
its own knots, or coil itself.

Knots appear at 1-foot intervals, reducing length to 50 feet and granting advantage on checks to climb
it. The rope has AC 20 and 20 HP and heals 1 HP per 5 minutes while at 1 HP or more. At 0 HP it is
destroyed.

[SRD p. 241, Rope of Climbing](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-241.md)

<a id="srd51-item-rope-of-entanglement"></a>

## Rope of Entanglement

`srd51.item.rope-of-entanglement`

Wondrous item · Rare · No attunement.

The rope is 30 feet long and weighs 3 pounds. Hold one end and speak its command word as an action to
lash at a creature you can see within 20 feet. A failed DC 15 Dexterity save restrains it. You can
release it with a second command word as a bonus action. The restrained creature can spend an action
to attempt a DC 15 Strength or Dexterity check of its choice, ending restraint on success.

The rope has AC 20 and 20 HP. It regains 1 HP each 5 minutes while at 1 HP or more. At 0 HP it is
destroyed.

[SRD p. 241, Rope of Entanglement](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-241.md)

## Examples

- A Periapt of Wound Closure wearer who spends a Hit Die on a short rest, rolls 4, and has a Constitution modifier of +2 restores 12 HP, within the ordinary HP maximum. [SRD p. 232, Periapt of Wound Closure](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-232.md) · [SRD p. 87, Short Rest](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-087.md)
- Spending three charges of Ring of the Ram on one creature makes one attack at +7. On a hit it deals 6d10 force damage and pushes 15 feet. [SRD p. 238, Ring of the Ram](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-238.md)
- For a Large creature, apply two vials of Oil of Etherealness over 10 minutes to gain Etherealness for 1 hour. A single vial is insufficient. [SRD p. 231, Oil of Etherealness](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-231.md) · [SRD p. 232, Oil of Etherealness continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-232.md)
- If a Nine Lives Stealer critical hit leaves a creature at exactly 100 HP, it does not trigger the Constitution save. An eligible creature that passes the save costs no charge. [SRD p. 231, Nine Lives Stealer](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-231.md)
- A creature escapes a closed Portable Hole by using its action and succeeding on the DC 10 Strength check. It then appears within 5 feet of the hole or its carrier. [SRD p. 233, Portable Hole](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-233.md)
- A Potion of Poison drinker who fails the initial save takes the first 3d6 at drinking and 3d6 at its next turn start. A subsequent successful save reduces the following turn-start damage to 2d6. [SRD p. 234, Potion of Poison](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-234.md)
- A Ring of Spell Storing with four levels stored cannot accept a level-2 casting. That casting is expended without effect. Casting a stored spell frees its own slot levels of space. [SRD p. 237, Ring of Spell Storing](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-237.md)
- A Ring of Shooting Stars with three spheres created uses 2d6 for each failed save against Ball Lightning, even after only one of those spheres remains. [SRD p. 237, Ring of Shooting Stars and Ball Lightning table](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-237.md)
- A Rod of Absorption that already absorbed 48 lifetime levels cannot cancel a level-3 spell, even after its holder spent every stored level. [SRD p. 239, Rod of Absorption](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-239.md)
