# Creatures from Aboleth through Doppelganger

Each entry specifies one stat block. The first damage number in an attack is the
listed average and the parenthesized expression is its alternative roll.
Modifiers on weapon and spell attacks are attack-roll bonuses. Where no special
save, skill, defense, or sense is listed, use the
[general creature rules](creatures.md#srd51-creature-rules-stat-block). Each
Multiattack entry describes its own action, and recharge labels follow the
[use limit rules](creatures.md#srd51-creature-rules-limited-usage). Use the
[monster grapple rules](creatures.md#srd51-creature-rules-grapple) for the
stated escape DCs. An action in a Multiattack or legendary option retains its
attack details and the restrictions stated by that option. [SRD p. 259, Actions, Limited Usage, and Grapple Rules for Monsters](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-259.md) [SRD p. 260, Legendary Actions](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-260.md)

## Aboleth

`srd51.creature.aboleth`

**Profile.** Large aberration · lawful evil · CR 10 (5,900 XP) · 135 average HP (18d10 + 36) · AC 17 (natural armor) · movement walk 10 ft., swim 40 ft.

**Abilities.** Str 21 (+5) · Dex 9 (−1) · Con 15 (+2) · Int 18 (+4) · Wis 15 (+2) · Cha 18 (+4). Save bonuses Con +6, Int +8, Wis +6. Skill bonuses History +12, Perception +10.

**Awareness and defenses.** Passive Perception 20, darkvision 120 ft. Languages Deep Speech, telepathy 120 ft. [SRD p. 261, Aboleth](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-261.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-aboleth-amphibious"></a> **Trait — Amphibious.** `srd51.creature.aboleth.amphibious` Apply [Amphibious](creatures.md#srd51-creature-rules-amphibious). [SRD p. 261, Aboleth, Amphibious](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-261.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-aboleth-mucous-cloud"></a> **Trait — Mucous Cloud.** `srd51.creature.aboleth.mucous-cloud` While the aboleth is underwater, a creature that touches it or hits it with a melee attack from within 5 feet makes a DC 14 Constitution save. Failure causes a disease lasting 1d4 hours. While diseased, the creature can breathe only underwater. [SRD p. 261, Aboleth, Mucous Cloud](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-261.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-aboleth-probing-telepathy"></a> **Trait — Probing Telepathy.** `srd51.creature.aboleth.probing-telepathy` When a creature communicates with the aboleth telepathically, the aboleth learns its greatest desires if it can see the creature. [SRD p. 261, Aboleth, Probing Telepathy](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-261.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-aboleth-multiattack"></a> **Action — Multiattack.** `srd51.creature.aboleth.multiattack` Make three Tentacle attacks. [SRD p. 261, Aboleth, Multiattack](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-261.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-aboleth-tentacle"></a> **Action — Tentacle.** `srd51.creature.aboleth.tentacle` Melee weapon, +9, reach 10 feet, one target. A hit deals 12 (2d6 + 5) bludgeoning. A creature hit also makes a DC 14 Constitution save or contracts a disease. For the first minute it has no effect and any disease-curing magic can remove it. After 1 minute, the skin becomes translucent and slimy, the creature can regain hit points only while underwater, and only [Heal](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-heal) or another disease-curing spell of 6th level or higher can remove it. Outside a body of water the creature takes 6 (1d12) acid damage at each 10-minute interval unless its skin is moistened before the interval elapses. [SRD p. 261, Aboleth, Tentacle](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-261.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-aboleth-tail"></a> **Action — Tail.** `srd51.creature.aboleth.tail` Melee weapon, +9, reach 10 feet, one target. A hit deals 15 (3d6 + 5) bludgeoning. [SRD p. 261, Aboleth, Tail](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-261.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-aboleth-enslave"></a> **Action — Enslave (3/Day).** `srd51.creature.aboleth.enslave` Choose a visible creature within 30 feet. On a failed DC 14 Wisdom save it is magically charmed, controlled by the aboleth, unable to take reactions, and able to exchange telepathic messages with the aboleth at any distance. End the effect on the aboleth's death or when the pair are on different planes. After taking damage, the target may repeat the save. When at least 1 mile away, the target may also repeat the save at most once every 24 hours. A successful repeat ends the effect. [SRD p. 261, Aboleth, Enslave](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-261.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-aboleth-legendary-actions"></a> **Legendary budget — Legendary Actions.** `srd51.creature.aboleth.legendary-actions` [Legendary action rules](creatures.md#srd51-creature-rules-legendary-actions) apply. Three legendary actions per budget. Detect and Tail Swipe cost one each and Psychic Drain costs two. [SRD p. 261, Aboleth, Legendary Actions](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-261.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-aboleth-detect"></a> **Legendary option — Detect.** `srd51.creature.aboleth.detect` Make a Wisdom (Perception) check. [SRD p. 261, Aboleth, Detect](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-261.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-aboleth-tail-swipe"></a> **Legendary option — Tail Swipe.** `srd51.creature.aboleth.tail-swipe` Make one Tail attack. [SRD p. 261, Aboleth, Tail Swipe](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-261.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-aboleth-psychic-drain"></a> **Legendary option — Psychic Drain (Costs 2 Actions).** `srd51.creature.aboleth.psychic-drain` Select one creature under this aboleth’s charm and deal 10 (3d6) psychic damage to it. Heal the aboleth for the amount of damage the target actually takes. [SRD p. 261, Aboleth, Psychic Drain](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-261.md)
