# Pantheons and planes

<a id="srd51-cosmology-pantheons"></a>

## Fantasy-historical pantheons

`srd51.cosmology.pantheons`

The SRD adapts the Celtic, Greek, Egyptian, and Norse pantheons for fantasy
campaigns. The listed alignment, sphere, symbol, and suggested domains describe
each deity in this treatment. They can inform a character's faith or affiliation.
A suggested domain supplies a deity relationship for
[Divine Domain selection](classes-cleric-druid.md#srd51-class-cleric-divine-domain).
Features and spell grants come from the domain rules, including the
[Life Domain](classes-cleric-druid.md#srd51-class-cleric-life-domain). An
[Acolyte](backgrounds-and-personality.md#srd51-background-acolyte) can choose a
deity, pantheon, or another quasi-divine being from this material or as
specified by the GM. [SRD p. 360, Fantasy-Historical Pantheons](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-360.md) [SRD p. 361, Deities and Suggested Domains](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-361.md) [SRD p. 16, Divine Domain](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-016.md) [SRD p. 61, Acolyte](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-061.md)

## The planes of existence

`srd51.cosmology.planes`

The planes are distinct dimensions of reality. The Material Plane contains the
campaign worlds, where elemental and philosophical influences mingle in mortal
life and ordinary matter. It is the usual starting plane. Beyond it are planes
shaped more directly by elemental forces, thought, spiritual principles, or
the powers of their inhabitants. Spells and items may interact with a plane
through their stated permissions, including drawing on its power, summoning
inhabitants, communicating, or traveling. [SRD p. 363, The Planes of Existence, The Material Plane, and Beyond the Material](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-363.md)

<a id="srd51-cosmology-planar-travel"></a>

### Planar travel

`srd51.cosmology.planar-travel`

Planar travel beyond the Material Plane uses a spell or a portal. Apply each
spell's own selection and destination limits. [Plane Shift](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-plane-shift) and
[Gate](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-gate) can carry adventurers directly to other planes, with
different destination precision. [Etherealness](spells-d-e.md#srd51-spell-etherealness) permits entry
to the Border Ethereal from a plane it borders, such as an Elemental Plane.
The spell returns a traveler to the plane from which it was cast. Reaching a
different plane through the Ethereal requires a further applicable route, such
as a portal. [SRD p. 140, Etherealness](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-140.md) [SRD p. 141, Etherealness continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-141.md) [Astral Projection](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-astral-projection) permits entry to the
Astral Plane and indirect travel to the Outer Planes. [SRD p. 363, Planar Travel, Spells](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-363.md)

A portal is a stationary connection between specific locations on two planes.
A doorway, window-like opening, or fog-bound passage can transfer a creature
when it steps through. Sites such as stone circles, towers, ships, and towns
can exist on multiple planes at once or shift between them. A vortex typically
joins an Elemental Plane to a corresponding Material Plane environment, such
as a volcanic heart for Fire or ocean depths for Water. The GM chooses the
portal's form and the locations it connects. [SRD p. 363, Planar Travel, Portals](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-363.md)

<a id="srd51-cosmology-transitive-planes"></a>

### Transitive planes

`srd51.cosmology.transitive-planes`

The Ethereal and Astral Planes are largely featureless routes that enable travel
between other planes. Their particular connections and access modes follow
below. [SRD p. 363, Transitive Planes](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-363.md) [SRD p. 364, Transitive Planes continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-364.md)

<a id="srd51-cosmology-ethereal-plane"></a>

#### Ethereal Plane

`srd51.cosmology.ethereal-plane`

The Border Ethereal overlaps the Material and Inner Planes. Each location on
those planes has a corresponding Border Ethereal location. Some creatures can
see into this border, and [See Invisibility](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-see-invisibility) and
[True Seeing](spells-t-z.md#srd51-spell-true-seeing) can grant this sight. Particular magical effects
also extend from the Material Plane to the Border Ethereal, including the
force effects in [Forcecage](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-forcecage) and [Wall of Force](spells-t-z.md#srd51-spell-wall-of-force).
The Deep Ethereal lies beyond the border, with dense mist and colored fog.
[SRD p. 363, Transitive Planes, Ethereal Plane](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-363.md) [SRD p. 364, Ethereal Plane continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-364.md)

<a id="srd51-cosmology-astral-plane"></a>

#### Astral Plane

`srd51.cosmology.astral-plane`

Visitors can travel through this plane as disembodied souls on the way to
divine or demonic realms, applying the rules of the travel method. The plane
is mostly a silvery open domain with lights, occasional solid matter, and
whirlpools of color. [SRD p. 364, Transitive Planes, Astral Plane](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-364.md)

<a id="srd51-cosmology-inner-planes"></a>

### Inner Planes

`srd51.cosmology.inner-planes`

The Elemental Planes of Air, Earth, Fire, and Water surround the Material Plane
and its echoes. Their innermost regions, closest conceptually to the Material
Plane, contain mixtures of the elements and can resemble its worlds. Farther
out, each becomes hostile and dominated by its pure element, with vast expanses
of fire, water, earth, or air. References to a particular Elemental Plane often
mean its more familiar border region. At the far reaches, the pure elements
mingle into the turbulent Elemental Chaos. This section describes geography
and hazards without assigning a universal damage or travel rate. [SRD p. 364, Inner Planes](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-364.md)

<a id="srd51-cosmology-outer-planes"></a>

### Outer Planes

`srd51.cosmology.outer-planes`

These planes reflect spiritual thought, purpose, and alignment and are homes of
deities. Mortal visitors can perceive a region that looks familiar, with further
spiritual regions beyond ordinary senses. Powerful inhabitants can alter the
landscape or remake a region altogether. Distance and travel time are therefore
variable, from a journey across the Nine Hells in a day with the powers'
cooperation to weeks on one layer. The GM resolves the route and conditions
according to the plane and its inhabitants. [SRD p. 364, Outer Planes](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-364.md)

Sixteen well-known Outer Planes correspond to the eight non-neutral alignments
and shades between them. Planes with an element of good are the Upper Planes,
where celestials such as angels and pegasi dwell. Planes with an element of evil
are the Lower Planes, home to fiends such as demons and devils. Alignment is
fundamental to an Outer Plane. A mismatched visitor feels dissonance, as an evil
visitor does in neutral good Elysium. This passage gives no numeric penalty for
that experience. [SRD p. 364, Outer Planes](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-364.md)

<a id="srd51-cosmology-demiplanes"></a>

### Demiplanes

`srd51.cosmology.demiplanes`

A demiplane is a small extradimensional space with its own rules. It can
arise naturally from a separated fold of reality, through a spell such as
[Demiplane](spells-d-e.md#srd51-spell-demiplane), or through a powerful deity or other force. Entry is
possible at a single point where that demiplane touches another plane. The
proper tuning fork frequency for reaching it with [Plane Shift](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-plane-shift)
is difficult to acquire. [Gate](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-gate) is a more reliable means for a
caster who knows of the demiplane and meets the spell's requirements.
[SRD p. 364, Demiplanes](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-364.md) [SRD p. 365, Demiplanes continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-365.md)

## Source-checked cases

- **Ordinary.** A cleric choosing a deity associated with healing can look to Diancecht, listed as lawful good with Life as a suggested domain and crossed oak and mistletoe branches as the symbol. [SRD p. 360, Celtic Deities, Diancecht](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-360.md) [SRD p. 16, Divine Domain](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-016.md)
- **Boundary.** An Ethereal traveler in the border region has corresponding locations on the Material and Inner Planes. The overlap does not make the Astral Plane part of the Border Ethereal. [SRD p. 363, Transitive Planes](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-363.md) [SRD p. 364, Ethereal and Astral Planes](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-364.md)
- **Exception.** Wall of Force extends from the Material Plane into the Border Ethereal under its own rule. This is a specific force effect rather than a blanket interplanar reach rule. [SRD p. 364, Ethereal Plane continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-364.md) [SRD p. 190, Wall of Force](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-190.md)
- **Invalid.** A deity's suggested domains are not granted automatically as cleric subclasses or as spell access. A demiplane's existence likewise does not supply a Plane Shift focus tuned to it. [SRD p. 16, Divine Domain](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-016.md) [SRD p. 361, Suggested Domains](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-361.md) [SRD p. 365, Demiplanes continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-365.md)
