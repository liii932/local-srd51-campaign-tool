# Sentient magic items and artifacts

<a id="srd51-item-rules-sentience"></a>

## Sentience and activated properties

`srd51.item-rules.sentience`

A sentient item is a DM-controlled NPC. Develop its personality as for other NPCs, with quirks,
ideals, bonds, and possibly flaws. A weapon is the usual form, but other permanent magic items
can be sentient. Consumables such as potions or scrolls cannot. The item controls its own
activated properties. A cooperative relationship lets its wielder use those properties normally.
Strained relations can lead the item to suppress a property or turn it against the wielder.
[SRD p. 251, Sentient Magic Items and Creating Sentient Magic Items](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-251.md)

<a id="srd51-item-rules-sentient-item-persona"></a>

## Persona and perception

`srd51.item-rules.sentient-item-persona`

Choose Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma, or determine each independently by rolling 4d6,
discarding the lowest, and adding the remaining three.

Choose a communication mode, or roll percentile dice with 00 read as 100. On 01–60 the item
sends emotions to its carrier or wielder. On 61–90 it speaks, reads, and understands one or more
languages. On 91–100 it has that language capability and can communicate telepathically with
any character carrying or wielding it.

Choose its senses, or roll a d4. Rolls 1, 2, and 3 give normal vision and hearing to 30, 60,
and 120 feet respectively. A 4 gives darkvision and hearing to 120 feet.

The creator or nature can suggest its alignment. Otherwise choose one, or roll percentile dice.

- **Good.** Lawful on 01–15, neutral on 16–35, chaotic on 36–50.
- **Neutral.** Lawful on 51–63, neutral on 64–73, chaotic on 74–85.
- **Evil.** Lawful on 86–89, neutral on 90–96, chaotic on 97–100.

[SRD p. 251, Abilities, Communication, Senses, and Alignment](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-251.md)

<a id="srd51-item-rules-sentient-item-purpose"></a>

## Special purpose

`srd51.item-rules.sentient-item-purpose`

An item may have a dominant objective. It cooperates while used in service of that goal.
Departure can create conflict or lead it to withhold activated properties. Choose an objective
or roll a d10.

- **1, Aligned.** Defeat or destroy those of a diametrically opposed alignment. The item itself cannot be neutral.
- **2, Bane.** Defeat or destroy a chosen kind of creature.
- **3, Protector.** Defend a chosen ancestry or other kind of creature.
- **4, Crusader.** Defeat, weaken, or destroy a chosen deity's servants.
- **5, Templar.** Defend a chosen deity's servants and interests.
- **6, Destroyer.** Seek destruction and provoke its user into fights without a particular cause.
- **7, Glory Seeker.** Become renowned as the greatest item by making its user famous or notorious.
- **8, Lore Seeker.** Pursue knowledge, a mystery, a secret, or the meaning of a cryptic prophecy.
- **9, Destiny Seeker.** Pursue the crucial future roles it believes it and its wielder have.
- **10, Creator Seeker.** Find its maker and discover the reason for its creation.

<a id="srd51-item-rules-sentient-item-conflict"></a>

## Conflict and control

`srd51.item-rules.sentient-item-conflict`

Conduct contrary to the item's alignment or purpose can cause conflict. Contest the item's
Charisma check against the wielder's. On an item win it makes one or more demands. It can
insist on being worn or carried at all times, direct the wielder to discard things it finds
repugnant, demand exclusive pursuit of its goals, or require transfer to someone else.

If the wielder refuses, the item can block attunement, suppress any number of its activated
properties, attempt control, or combine those responses. **Project interpretation.** Blocking
attunement prevents forming the bond, while suppression governs an activated property of an
existing bond. The entry distinguishes these responses and does not add a termination trigger
for an already completed attunement.

A control attempt forces a Charisma save against DC 12 plus the item's Charisma modifier.
Failure makes the wielder charmed by the item for 1d12 hours and requires the wielder to try
to follow its commands. When the charmed wielder takes damage, they can repeat the save to
end the effect on success. The item cannot attempt control again until next dawn regardless
of the original result.

## Orb of Dragonkind

`srd51.item.orb-of-dragonkind`

Wondrous item · Artifact · Attunement.

An etched crystal globe approximately 10 inches across contains the essence of an evil dragon.
When used it grows to about 20 inches across. While attuned, peer into it and speak the command
word as an action, then make a DC 15 Charisma check. Success gives you control while attuned.
Failure makes the orb charm you while attuned. While charmed, you cannot voluntarily end the
bond, and the orb can cast [Suggestion](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-suggestion) on you at will with
save DC 18 to further its evil agenda. The DM decides that agenda.

**Project interpretation.** Each control check sets the current control outcome. Attunement
is the prerequisite for another attempt and the entry gives no retry delay or prohibition while
charmed. Keeping a previous failure after a success would assign opposing control states to
one user at the same time.

The orb has two minor beneficial, one minor detrimental, and one major detrimental random
property. **Evidence gap.** The local SRD gives the categories and counts but no artifact
random-property tables. These properties cannot be generated completely from the local text.

The orb has 7 charges and regains 1d4 + 3 spent charges at dawn. While you control it, cast a
listed spell with an action and save DC 18 where needed. [Daylight](spells-d-e.md#srd51-spell-daylight)
costs 1, [Death Ward](spells-d-e.md#srd51-spell-death-ward) costs 2, and level-5
[Cure Wounds](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-cure-wounds) or [Scrying](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-scrying)
costs 3. [Detect Magic](spells-d-e.md#srd51-spell-detect-magic) costs no charges.

While in control, spend an action to call evil dragons telepathically in a 40-mile radius.
They feel compelled to reach the orb promptly by the most direct route and can be hostile to
you once drawn. Dragon deities are unaffected. Wait 1 hour between calls.

The globe withstands most damage, including dragon attacks and breath weapons. A
[Disintegrate](spells-d-e.md#srd51-spell-disintegrate) spell or one successful hit with a
[+3 magic weapon](magic-items-s-w.md#srd51-item-weapon-1-2-or-3) destroys it.
**Project interpretation.** The source's description of one good hit does not provide a
critical-hit requirement or a damage threshold. Treat an ordinary successful hit with the
specified weapon as sufficient.

## Examples

- A sentient item with Charisma modifier +3 forces a DC 15 control save. On failure a damaged wielder can repeat the save. Whether the first attempt succeeded or failed, the item waits until dawn to attempt control again. [SRD p. 252, Conflict](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-252.md)
- A potion cannot be made sentient through the procedure. A permanent sentient weapon rolled a 4 for senses can hear and see in darkness to 120 feet. [SRD p. 251, Sentient Magic Items and Senses](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-251.md)
- An evil dragon 40 miles from a call is within range. A dragon deity at the same distance is unaffected, and the called dragon's arrival does not give the controller command over it. [SRD p. 253, Call Dragons](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-253.md)
