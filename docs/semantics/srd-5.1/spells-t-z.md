# Spells T–Z

Each spell's casting line gives level and school, casting time, range, components, and duration in
that order. V, S, and M mean verbal, somatic, and material. A listed price requires the named
material, and materials are consumed only where specified. A ritual tag uses the general ritual
rules. Unmodified casting and resolution use the shared
[casting procedure](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-cast-spell),
[slots](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-slots),
[components](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-components),
[range](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-range),
[targets](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-targets),
[areas](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-area),
[spell saves](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-saving-throw),
[spell attack rolls](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-attack-roll),
[duration](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-duration), and
[concentration](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-concentration) rules.
HP means hit points, AC means Armor Class, and CR means challenge rating.
Find spell names and class access lists in the [spell index](spell-index.md).

## Telekinesis

`srd51.spell.telekinesis`

Level 5 transmutation · 1 action · 60 feet · V, S · Concentration up to 10 minutes.

At casting and as an action each round afterward, choose a creature or object you can see in range.
You can retain or change the target. Switching ends the effect on the former target.

- **Creature.** A Huge or smaller creature contests your spellcasting ability check with its Strength
  check. On your win, move it up to 30 feet in any direction, staying inside spell range. It is
  restrained until the end of your next turn and suspended if lifted. You may repeat the contest as an
  action on a subsequent round to maintain the hold.
- **Object.** An object of at most 1,000 pounds can move up to 30 feet in any direction within spell
  range. An object neither worn nor carried moves automatically. For one worn or carried, contest the creature's
  Strength check with your spellcasting ability check. A win pulls the object free and lets you move
  it.
- **Fine control.** Use the telekinetic hold to manipulate an object precisely. Examples include
  working a simple tool, opening a door or container, taking or stowing something in an open
  container, and pouring from a vial.

[SRD p. 185, Telekinesis](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-185.md)

## Teleport

`srd51.spell.teleport`

Level 7 conjuration · 1 action · 10 feet · V · Instantaneous.

Choose either to move yourself and up to eight willing creatures you can see in range, or to move one
object you can see in range. The object must fit inside a 10-foot cube and cannot be held or carried
by an unwilling creature. Specify a destination known to you on your current plane. The DM rolls
percentile dice using the category below. Treat 00 as 100.

- **Permanent circle.** You know the sigil sequence of a permanent teleportation circle. All rolls
  land on target.
- **Associated object.** You possess an object taken from the desired place within the previous six
  months. All rolls land on target.
- **Very familiar.** Often visited, carefully studied, or visible during casting. Mishap 01–05,
  similar area 06–13, off target 14–24, on target 25–100.
- **Seen casually.** Seen more than once without strong familiarity. Mishap 01–33, similar area 34–43,
  off target 44–53, on target 54–100.
- **Viewed once or described.** Seen once, including by magic, or known in location and appearance
  from a description or map. Mishap 01–43, similar area 44–53, off target 54–73, on target 74–100.
- **False destination.** Nonexistent, including a former location that no longer exists or an illusory
  place mistaken for the real thing. Mishap 01–50 and similar area 51–100.

On target, appear at the desired place. Off target, appear 1d10 × 1d10 percent of the intended travel
distance away from the destination. The DM rolls a d8 for direction, counting clockwise from north on
1, northeast on 2, and east on 3. A similar-area result places travelers in a place visually or
thematically like the intended destination, usually the nearest one but potentially anywhere on that
plane. A mishap damages the payload before the DM rolls again. Apply 3d10 force damage to each traveler
or to the object being teleported. Every further mishap incurs that damage before another roll.

[SRD p. 185, Teleport](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-185.md) · [SRD p. 186, Teleport continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-186.md) · [SRD p. 186, Teleportation Circle](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-186.md)

## Teleportation Circle

`srd51.spell.teleportation-circle`

Level 5 conjuration · 1 minute · 10 feet · V, M (rare gem-infused chalk and ink worth 50 gp, consumed) · 1 round.

Draw a 10-foot-diameter sigil circle on the ground during casting. Link it to a permanent circle on
your plane whose sigil sequence you know. A portal opens within the drawn circle until the end of your
next turn. A creature entering the portal appears within 5 feet of the destination circle, or at the
nearest unoccupied space if that space is occupied.

Each permanent circle has a unique rune sequence. When you first gain the ability to cast this spell,
the DM assigns two known sequences for Material Plane destinations. You can learn further sequences by
studying each for 1 minute. Casting this spell at the same site each day for a year makes a permanent
circle there. You need not use the circle to travel during those castings.

**Project interpretation.** The material sentence prints “with 50 gp” where a component value is
expected. Read the figure as the value of the consumed chalk and ink. No alternative price or separate
coin requirement is specified.

## Thaumaturgy

`srd51.spell.thaumaturgy`

Transmutation cantrip · 1 action · 30 feet · V · Up to 1 minute.

Choose one manifestation in range.

- Amplify your voice up to threefold for 1 minute.
- Make flames flicker, brighten, dim, or change color for 1 minute.
- Create harmless ground tremors for 1 minute.

## Tiny Hut

`srd51.spell.tiny-hut`

Level 3 evocation (ritual) · 1 minute · Self (10-foot-radius hemisphere) · V, S, M (a small crystal bead) · 8 hours.

Create a stationary force dome around and above you. It ends early if you leave its area. Creatures
and objects inside at casting can cross freely. Others cannot cross. Spells and other magical effects
cannot extend or be cast through the dome. The interior is comfortable and dry regardless of outdoor
weather. You can make the interior dim or dark. The dome is transparent from inside and opaque from
outside in a color of your choice.

**Project interpretation.** Allow up to nine other creatures of Medium size or smaller inside with you
at casting. Any creature larger than Medium or a tenth companion in the area makes the spell fail. The
source gives nine places “with you,” then gives a limit of nine creatures without repeating that
distinction. Counting the caster among nine would contradict the explicit capacity statement.

## Tree Stride

`srd51.spell.tree-stride`

Level 5 conjuration · 1 action · Self · V, S · Concentration up to 1 minute.

Once per round, spend 5 feet of movement to enter a living tree at least your size. Immediately learn
the locations of all trees of the same kind within 500 feet. In the same movement you can pass to an
eligible tree of that kind or step back out of the first. Both trees must be living and at least your
size. Spend another 5 feet of movement to appear at a chosen point within 5 feet of the tree you exit.
If you have no movement left, appear within 5 feet of the tree you entered. You must end each of your turns outside a tree.

**Project interpretation.** If fewer than 5 feet of movement remain after entry, use the entry-tree
fallback. The source only names having no movement left, but the other exit requires the full 5 feet
and staying inside is barred at the end of the turn.

<a id="srd51-spell-true-polymorph"></a>

## True Polymorph

`srd51.spell.true-polymorph`

Level 9 transmutation · 1 action · 30 feet · V, S, M (a drop of mercury, a dollop of gum arabic, and a wisp of smoke) · Concentration up to 1 hour.

Choose one visible creature or nonmagical object in range. An object must be neither worn nor carried
by another creature. Shapechangers and creatures with 0 HP are unaffected. An unwilling creature
avoids the spell with a successful Wisdom save. The transformation lasts until spell end, the subject
reaches 0 HP, or it dies. Full-duration concentration extends the transformation until dispelled.

- **Creature to creature.** Choose a new form of no greater CR, using the target's level if it has no
  CR. The new statistics replace the original's including mental scores, but alignment and personality
  persist. Use the new form's HP. On reverting restore previous HP and carry over any damage beyond
  the amount that reduced the form to 0. There is no unconsciousness from the transition if the
  original form stays above 0 HP. Actions, speech, and spellcasting are limited by the body's
  capabilities. Gear merges and gives no benefits or ability to wield, activate, or use it.
- **Object to creature.** Choose a creature no larger than the object and with CR at most 9. It is
  friendly to you and your companions and acts on your turns. You select its action and movement. The
  DM has its statistics and resolves what it does. If the transformation becomes lasting, you no
  longer control it. Its continued friendliness depends on how you treated it.
- **Creature to object.** The creature and its worn and carried gear become the object. Use the
  object's statistics. On reverting it remembers none of its time as an object.

**Project interpretation.** Full-duration concentration removes the time limit and the concentration
requirement. Reaching 0 HP or dying remains a separate ending trigger. Reading “until it is dispelled”
as the sole remaining trigger would remove those earlier subject-state triggers without an express
statement.

[SRD p. 188, True Polymorph](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-188.md)

## Wall of Stone

`srd51.spell.wall-of-stone`

Level 5 evocation · 1 action · 120 feet · V, S, M (a small granite block) · Concentration up to 10 minutes.

Place a wall of nonmagical stone at a point in range. It must merge with and be supported by existing

wall alone or together with another solid surface can make a Dexterity save. On success it may use its
reaction to move up to its speed and avoid enclosure.

may have connected panels collapse. If you concentrate for the full duration the wall stays
permanently and cannot be dispelled. Otherwise it vanishes at spell end.

Level 3 evocation · 1 action · 120 feet · V, S, M (a tiny fan and a feather of exotic origin) · Concentration up to 1 minute.

At a point in range, raise a continuous ground-following wall of wind in a shape you choose. It can be
up to 50 feet long and 15 feet high and is 1 foot thick. On creation every creature inside makes a
Strength save against 3d8 bludgeoning damage, taking half on success.

Fog, smoke, gases, creatures in gaseous form, and flying creatures or objects of Small size or smaller
cannot pass. Loose lightweight material in it flies upward. Ordinary missiles such as arrows and bolts
launched at targets behind it deflect upward and miss automatically. Boulders thrown by giants or
siege engines and similar projectiles are unaffected.

<a id="srd51-spell-wish"></a>

## Wish

`srd51.spell.wish`

Level 9 conjuration · 1 action · Self · V · Instantaneous.

Speak a wish. You may duplicate any other spell of level 8 or lower, ignoring its requirements
including costly components. The copied spell takes effect.

Alternatively choose one of the following effects.

- **Object.** Create one nonmagical object worth at most 25,000 gp and measuring at most 300 feet in
  every dimension. Put it on unoccupied ground you can see.
- **Restoration.** Up to twenty visible creatures regain full HP and lose all effects described by
  [Greater Restoration](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-greater-restoration).
- **Resistance.** Up to ten visible creatures gain resistance to a damage type you choose.
- **Immunity.** Up to ten visible creatures gain immunity to one spell or other magical effect for 8
  hours.
- **Reroll.** Choose a roll within the last round, including your last turn, to undo a recent event.
  Force a reroll, optionally imposing advantage or disadvantage, and decide whether the original or
  replacement stands. Reality changes to fit the chosen result.

You can request an effect outside these options. State it precisely. The DM has broad discretion about
the outcome. Larger wishes carry a higher chance of going wrong. The effect can fail, occur in part,
or produce unforeseen consequences from the wording.

**Stress.** A casting seeking an effect other than duplication weakens you. Until your next long rest,
each later spell you cast deals you 1d10 necrotic damage per spell level, which cannot be reduced or
prevented. Your Strength becomes 3 if higher for 2d4 days. Each day spent resting with at most light
activity reduces remaining recovery time by 2 days. After suffering the stress, there is a 33 percent
chance you can never cast Wish again.

**Project interpretation.** Use two total days of Strength recovery per qualifying rest day. The
source gives that day a two-day decrease, rather than explicitly adding two extra days beyond ordinary
elapsed time. A failed open-ended wish still causes stress because the trigger is casting to produce a
nonduplicated effect.

[SRD p. 193, Wish](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-193.md) · [SRD p. 150, Greater Restoration](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-150.md) · [SRD p. 101, Cantrips](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-101.md)

<a id="srd51-spell-word-of-recall"></a>

## Word of Recall

`srd51.spell.word-of-recall`

Level 6 conjuration · 1 action · 5 feet · V · Instantaneous.

When establishing a sanctuary, cast this spell in a place dedicated or strongly linked to your deity.
If the location is ineligible, the preparation has no effect. **Project interpretation.** Accept a
strong link as eligibility, because the designation clause expressly lists that alternative. The later
sentence referring to a place not dedicated does not specify removal of the listed alternative.

To return, cast with a prepared sanctuary. You and up to five willing creatures within 5 feet teleport
to the nearest unoccupied spaces by the designated spot. A return cast without a sanctuary has no
effect.

[SRD p. 193, Word of Recall](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-193.md)

<a id="srd51-spell-zone-of-truth"></a>

## Zone of Truth

`srd51.spell.zone-of-truth`

Level 2 enchantment · 1 action · 60 feet · V, S · 10 minutes.

Center a 15-foot-radius sphere on a point in range. A creature first entering on a turn or starting
its turn there makes a Charisma save. You know the outcome of each save. Failure prevents deliberate
lies while the creature is in the zone. It knows it is affected and may refuse to answer or reply
evasively within the truth.

**Project interpretation.** A failure persists for this casting and restricts speech whenever the
creature is within the zone. A subsequent success does not undo that failure. The source specifies a
restriction on failure and gives no cancellation on success, although a new save still occurs at each
qualifying trigger.

[SRD p. 193, Zone of Truth](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-193.md) · [SRD p. 194, Zone of Truth continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-194.md)

## Examples

- A Teleport cast using the seen-casually category and a roll of 53 lands off target, but a 54 lands on target. A subsequent reroll after a mishap does not undo the mishap damage. [SRD p. 186, Teleport continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-186.md)
- If five feet of movement remain on entering a Tree Stride tree, the caster can spend them to exit within 5 feet of an eligible destination tree. With four feet left, the entry-tree fallback applies under the stated reading. [SRD p. 188, Tree Stride](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-188.md)
- A creature entering the hot Wall of Fire and ending its turn inside it takes the first-entry damage and one end-of-turn damage. A creature ending outside on the cool side takes neither end-of-turn application. [SRD p. 190, Wall of Fire](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-190.md)
- A caster duplicated a 7th-level spell with Wish while ignoring an expensive component. This uses the duplication branch and incurs no new stress. A later nonduplicating Wish that grants spell immunity lasts 8 hours for at most ten targets and incurs stress. [SRD p. 193, Wish](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-193.md)
- When a protected creature takes 5 fire damage after its own resistance, Warding Bond gives the caster a separate 5-fire-damage event before the caster’s defenses. Moving from exactly 60 feet apart to 61 feet ends the spell. [SRD p. 191, Warding Bond](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-191.md) · [SRD p. 97, Damage Resistance and Vulnerability](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-097.md)
- A creature in Zone of Truth can decline to answer. After it failed one Charisma save, a later successful save does not permit a deliberate lie inside the same zone under the stated reading. [SRD p. 194, Zone of Truth continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-194.md)
