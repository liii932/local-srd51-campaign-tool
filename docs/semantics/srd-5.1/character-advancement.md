# Character advancement and multiclassing

<a id="srd51-character-advancement"></a>

## Experience and levels

`srd51.character.advancement`

Experience points represent growth through adventures and overcoming challenges. When a character reaches a threshold, the character advances to that level and gains the features at the new level of the class advanced. The following are cumulative XP thresholds. Each entry is paired with character level in order, starting from the first level named for the group. [SRD p. 56, Beyond 1st Level and Character Advancement](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-056.md)

| Character levels | Cumulative XP thresholds in level order | Proficiency bonus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–4 | 0, 300, 900, 2,700 | +2 |
| 5–8 | 6,500, 14,000, 23,000, 34,000 | +3 |
| 9–12 | 48,000, 64,000, 85,000, 100,000 | +4 |
| 13–16 | 120,000, 140,000, 165,000, 195,000 | +5 |
| 17–20 | 225,000, 265,000, 305,000, 355,000 | +6 |

Use [proficiency bonus](abilities.md#srd51-core-proficiency-bonus) at total character level. Consult the chosen class for particular feature grants. [SRD p. 56, Character Advancement](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-056.md) [SRD p. 57, Proficiency Bonus and Class Features](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-057.md)

<a id="srd51-character-hit-point-advancement"></a>

### Hit points and Hit Dice

`srd51.character.hit-point-advancement`

Use the initial class's level-1 hit point formula only at character level 1. On each later gain in level, gain one Hit Die of the class advanced and add its roll plus your Constitution modifier to maximum hit points. The player may use the class's fixed value in place of the roll, calculated by rounding the die's average up. For d6, d8, d10, and d12 the fixed values are 4, 5, 6, and 7 respectively. [SRD p. 56, Beyond 1st Level](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-056.md) [SRD p. 57, Hit Points and Hit Dice](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-057.md)

When the Constitution modifier rises, also increase maximum hit points for each character level attained, by the modifier increase per level. For example, if the modifier increases by 1 during advancement from 7th to 8th level, its contribution increases by 8 hit points across those levels. Count each level's new modifier once. Hit Dice from multiple classes form one resource pool grouped by die type. A paladin 5/fighter 5 has ten d10s, whereas a paladin 5/cleric 5 tracks five d10s and five d8s. [SRD p. 56, Beyond 1st Level](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-056.md) [SRD p. 57, Hit Points and Hit Dice](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-057.md)

<a id="srd51-character-ability-score-improvement"></a>

### Ability Score Improvement

`srd51.character.ability-score-improvement`

When the class grants an Ability Score Improvement, choose to increase one ability score by 2 or two distinct scores by 1 each. An improvement cannot take an ability score above 20. Under the optional [feat acquisition rule](feats.md#srd51-feat-acquisition), you can take an eligible feat in place of the improvement. Classes define the levels at which the choice occurs. [SRD p. 56, Beyond 1st Level](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-056.md) [SRD p. 75, Feats](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-075.md)

<a id="srd51-character-multiclassing"></a>

## Multiclassing

`srd51.character.multiclassing`

When using multiclassing, on advancing a level you may add a level in another class instead of advancing a class you already have. The sum of all class levels is your character level. Determine advancement XP and proficiency bonus by this sum, and the features you gain by your new level in the selected class. When gaining a level in a new class, use that class's post-1st-level hit point formula. You gain its features for the new class level subject to the specific feature rules below, and the limited starting proficiencies listed here. Class starting equipment is granted by your initial class. [SRD p. 56, Multiclassing](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-056.md) [SRD p. 57, Experience Points through Class Features](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-057.md)

<a id="srd51-character-multiclass-prerequisites"></a>

### Ability-score prerequisites

`srd51.character.multiclass-prerequisites`

To enter a new class, meet the ability score requirements of both your current class and the new class. All thresholds in the groups below are scores of 13 or higher. [SRD p. 56, Prerequisites](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-056.md)

| Requirement at 13 or higher | Class |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | Barbarian |
| Dexterity | Rogue |
| Intelligence | Wizard |
| Wisdom | Cleric, druid |
| Charisma | Bard, sorcerer, warlock |
| Strength or Dexterity | Fighter |
| Dexterity and Wisdom | Monk, ranger |
| Strength and Charisma | Paladin |

**Project interpretation.** On entering a further new class, check the prerequisites for every class already held plus the class being entered. The source's reference to your current class uses the singular and gives a one-class example. Extending the check to all already held classes treats each of them as current and avoids selecting an arbitrary current class. This gate is for entering a class. [SRD p. 56, Multiclassing and Prerequisites](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-056.md)

<a id="srd51-character-multiclass-proficiencies"></a>

### Proficiencies on class entry

`srd51.character.multiclass-proficiencies`

When entering a class other than the initial class, receive the listed starting proficiencies once. Additional grants from class features are applied under their own rules. [SRD p. 57, Multiclassing Proficiencies and Class Features](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-057.md)

| Class or classes entered | Starting proficiencies granted |
| --- | --- |
| Barbarian | Shields, simple weapons, martial weapons |
| Bard | Light armor, one chosen skill, one chosen musical instrument |
| Cleric, druid | Light armor, medium armor, shields. Druids will not wear metal armor or use metal shields. |
| Fighter, paladin | Light armor, medium armor, shields, simple weapons, martial weapons |
| Monk | Simple weapons, shortswords |
| Ranger | Light armor, medium armor, shields, simple weapons, martial weapons, one chosen skill from its class list |
| Rogue | Light armor, one chosen skill from its class list, thieves' tools |
| Warlock | Light armor, simple weapons |
| Sorcerer, wizard | No starting proficiencies |

<a id="srd51-character-multiclass-channel-divinity"></a>

### Channel Divinity across classes

`srd51.character.multiclass-channel-divinity`

A new class granting Channel Divinity adds its effects as available choices for the feature. It does not itself add a use when you already have Channel Divinity. Uses increase only at a class level that expressly increases the capacity. Each use may select any of the effects available from either class, applying the chosen effect's rules and class DC. A cleric 6/paladin 4 has two uses between rests, each with access to both classes' choices. [SRD p. 57, Channel Divinity](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-057.md) [SRD p. 16, Cleric Channel Divinity](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-016.md) [SRD p. 32, Paladin Channel Divinity](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-032.md)

<a id="srd51-character-multiclass-extra-attack"></a>

### Extra Attack across classes

`srd51.character.multiclass-extra-attack`

Extra Attack grants from different classes do not stack. Apply the greatest applicable number of attacks granted by the feature, normally two, including a fighter's class-specific higher count where applicable. The Thirsting Blade invocation does not increase the number of attacks supplied by Extra Attack. [SRD p. 57, Extra Attack](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-057.md)

<a id="srd51-character-multiclass-unarmored-defense"></a>

### Unarmored Defense across classes

`srd51.character.multiclass-unarmored-defense`

Once you have the Unarmored Defense class feature, another class cannot grant it again. Apply the calculation and equipment restrictions of the version you acquired. [SRD p. 57, Unarmored Defense](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-057.md) [SRD p. 8, Barbarian Unarmored Defense](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-008.md) [SRD p. 26, Monk Unarmored Defense](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-026.md)

<a id="srd51-character-multiclass-spellcasting"></a>

### Spellcasting across classes

`srd51.character.multiclass-spellcasting`

If you have the Spellcasting feature from only one class, use its ordinary Spellcasting rules even if you have levels in other classes. Once you have it from multiple classes, determine each class's known and prepared spells separately using only that class's levels as if single-classed. Associate each spell known or prepared with its granting class. Use that class's spellcasting ability for that casting, and a focus from a class only for spells associated with that class. [SRD p. 57, Spellcasting](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-057.md) [SRD p. 58, Spells Known and Prepared](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-058.md)

Calculate an effective caster level for the shared spell slots. Add all bard, cleric, druid, sorcerer, and wizard levels. Add half the qualifying paladin and ranger levels, rounded down. Consult the [full-caster slot progression](#srd51-character-full-caster-slots) with the resulting total. You can spend a slot above the highest spell level you know or can prepare to cast an eligible lower-level spell and get any listed higher-slot effect. Slot level by itself grants no spell learning or preparation permission. [SRD p. 58, Spell Slots and Multiclass Spellcaster](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-058.md)

**Project interpretation.** Count paladin and ranger only after each has granted Spellcasting, add the qualifying paladin and ranger levels together, and round their combined half down once. The section is triggered by possession of Spellcasting and groups the two classes in one half-level term. Rounding each class individually would change the result when both are odd. [SRD p. 30, The Paladin](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-030.md) [SRD p. 35, The Ranger](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-035.md) [SRD p. 57, Spellcasting](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-057.md) [SRD p. 58, Spell Slots](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-058.md)

<a id="srd51-character-multiclass-pact-magic"></a>

#### Pact Magic with Spellcasting

`srd51.character.multiclass-pact-magic`

Pact Magic has its own slot count, slot level, and recovery. With both features, a Pact Magic slot can cast a known or prepared spell from a Spellcasting class, and a Spellcasting slot can cast a known warlock spell. The spell's class continues to determine its casting ability and focus. The slot's source continues to govern its recovery. [SRD p. 47, Pact Magic Spell Slots](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-047.md) [SRD p. 58, Spells Known and Prepared and Pact Magic](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-058.md)

<a id="srd51-character-full-caster-slots"></a>

## Full-caster slot progression

`srd51.character.full-caster-slots`

Bard, cleric, druid, sorcerer, and wizard use their class level as input for single-class Spellcasting. Use the effective caster level calculated above when combining Spellcasting from classes. For each spell level the entries below give input level → slot capacity. A listed capacity remains until the next change, and there are zero slots of that level before its first change. [SRD p. 11, The Bard](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-011.md) [SRD p. 15, The Cleric](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-015.md) [SRD p. 19, The Druid](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-019.md) [SRD p. 42, The Sorcerer](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-042.md) [SRD p. 52, The Wizard](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-052.md) [SRD p. 58, Multiclass Spellcaster](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-058.md)

| Spell level | Slot-capacity changes |
| --- | --- |
| 1st | 1 → 2, 2 → 3, 3 → 4 |
| 2nd | 3 → 2, 4 → 3 |
| 3rd | 5 → 2, 6 → 3 |
| 4th | 7 → 1, 8 → 2, 9 → 3 |
| 5th | 9 → 1, 10 → 2, 18 → 3 |
| 6th | 11 → 1, 19 → 2 |
| 7th | 13 → 1, 20 → 2 |
| 8th | 15 → 1 |
| 9th | 17 → 1 |

## Source-checked cases

- **Ordinary.** A fighter 3/rogue 2 is a 5th-level character and has +3 proficiency bonus. The character needs 14,000 XP to reach 6th level, regardless of the class selected for the advancement. [SRD p. 56, Character Advancement](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-056.md) [SRD p. 57, Experience Points and Proficiency Bonus](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-057.md)
- **Boundary.** A barbarian with Strength 13 and Wisdom 13 satisfies the ability gate for entering druid. Wisdom 12 would fail it. [SRD p. 56, Prerequisites](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-056.md)
- **Exception.** A ranger 4/wizard 3 combines four ranger levels and three wizard levels for effective caster level 5 and slots of 4 at 1st, 3 at 2nd, and 2 at 3rd. Ranger spells known are still limited by ranger level 4, and wizard learning and preparation by wizard level 3. Either class's eligible spells can use a 3rd-level slot. [SRD p. 58, Spells Known and Prepared and Spell Slots](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-058.md)
- **Invalid.** A monk with Unarmored Defense cannot acquire the barbarian version by gaining a barbarian level. [SRD p. 57, Unarmored Defense](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-057.md)
