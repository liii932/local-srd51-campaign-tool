<a id="srd51-item-apparatus-of-the-crab"></a>
## Apparatus of the Crab

`srd51.item.apparatus-of-the-crab` · Wondrous item · legendary. [SRD p. 208, Apparatus of the Crab](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-208.md)

The device is a Large, sealed iron barrel weighing 500 lb. A DC 20 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds the hidden catch. Release the catch to unlock a hatch through which up to two Medium or smaller creatures can enter. There are ten levers inside. Extending the relevant parts changes the barrel to a lobsterlike form.

It is a Large object with AC 20, 200 HP, immunity to poison and psychic damage, and walking and swimming speeds of 30 feet while legs and tail are extended. With them retracted, both speeds are 0 and speed bonuses cannot help. Operating as a vehicle requires one pilot. It floats and can submerge to a depth of 900 feet. Below 900 feet it takes 2d6 bludgeoning damage per minute. With the hatch closed it is airtight and watertight. Divide ten hours of breathable air by the number of breathing occupants to obtain their available time.

An occupant can use an action to operate up to two levers in either direction. A used lever returns to neutral. Number levers from left to right. The paired results below give the up and down directions respectively.

- **1, locomotion:** Extend the legs and tail to walk and swim, or retract them to disable movement.
- **2, front shutter:** Open it, or close it.
- **3, side shutters:** Open, or close, the two shutters on each side.
- **4, claws:** Extend two front claws, or retract them.
- **5, claw attacks:** In either direction, each extended claw makes one melee weapon attack, +8 to hit, reach 5 feet, one target. Up deals 7 (2d6) bludgeoning on a hit. Down instead grapples its target on a hit, escape DC 15.
- **6, translation:** Walk or swim forward, or backward.
- **7, rotation:** Turn 90 degrees left, or 90 degrees right.
- **8, lamps:** Turn on bright light within 30 feet and dim light 30 feet farther, or turn the light off.
- **9, depth:** Sink up to 20 feet in liquid, or rise up to 20 feet.
- **10, hatch:** Unseal and open the rear hatch, or close and seal it.

**Project interpretation.** Lever 6 moves the apparatus up to its remaining movement in the chosen direction and mode. Cap that movement at the apparatus's 30-foot speed per round across uses by its occupants. The source gives the directions and the object's speed but gives no distance for one lever use or independent vehicle turn. Treating every activation as a fresh 30 feet would let multiple occupants exceed the stated round speed. Retracted legs and tail give no movement via this lever. [SRD p. 208, Apparatus of the Crab and its Levers](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-208.md) · [SRD p. 84, Speed](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-084.md)

[SRD p. 208, Apparatus of the Crab](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-208.md)

The cloth bag holds 3d4 dry beans and weighs ½ lb plus ¼ lb for each bean inside. Dumping its contents on the ground explodes the beans in a 10-foot radius measured from them. Everyone in the area, including the user, makes a DC 15 Dexterity save against 5d4 fire damage, half on success. The flames ignite flammable objects in the area that are not worn or carried.

For a single planted bean, remove it, plant it in dirt or sand, then water it. An effect appears at that spot after 1 minute. The GM can select one of the following outcomes, roll d100, or devise another.

- **01:** Grow 5d4 toadstools. For each one eaten, roll any die. On odd, the eater makes a DC 15 Constitution save. Failure causes 5d6 poison damage and Poisoned for 1 hour. On even, the eater gains 5d6 temporary HP for 1 hour.
- **02–10:** A geyser jets 30 feet high for 1d12 rounds. The GM chooses water, beer, berry juice, tea, vinegar, wine, or oil.
- **11–20:** A [treant](creatures-m-z.md#srd51-creature-treant) appears, with a 50% chance of being chaotic evil and attacking.
- **21–30:** An immobile animated stone likeness of the planter emerges and threatens the planter. Once the planter leaves, it defames the planter to visitors and directs them to attack. It knows the planter's location while both are on the same plane. It becomes inanimate after 24 hours.
- **31–40:** A blue campfire burns for 24 hours or until extinguished.
- **41–50:** Spawn 1d6 + 6 [shriekers](creatures-d-m.md#srd51-creature-shrieker).
- **51–60:** Spawn 1d4 + 8 bright pink toads. Touching one transforms it into a monster of the GM's choice, Large or smaller. After 1 minute it disappears in pink smoke.
- **61–70:** A hungry [bulette](creatures-a-d.md#srd51-creature-bulette) emerges and attacks.
- **71–80:** A tree grows with 1d10 + 20 fruit, of which 1d8 act as randomly determined magic potions and one as an ingested poison chosen by the GM. The tree vanishes in 1 hour. Picked fruit remains and keeps its magic for 30 days.
- **81–90:** A nest appears with 1d4 + 3 eggs. An eater makes a DC 20 Constitution save for an egg. Success permanently raises its lowest ability score by 1, randomly selecting among ties. Failure deals 10d6 force damage internally.
- **91–99:** A pyramid rises with a 60-foot-square base. Treat it as a [mummy lord](creatures-m-z.md#srd51-creature-mummy-lord)'s lair, with the lord in a sarcophagus containing treasure chosen by the GM.
- **100 (00):** A giant beanstalk grows to the GM's chosen height. Its top leads to a location the GM chooses, which can be on another plane.

[SRD p. 209, Bag of Beans](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-209.md) · [SRD p. 210, Bag of Beans continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-210.md)

## Bag of Devouring

`srd51.item.bag-of-devouring` · Wondrous item · very rare. [SRD p. 210, Bag of Devouring](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-210.md)

The bag is an opening into an extradimensional creature's feeding passage. Turning it inside out closes that opening. The creature senses what enters. Animal or vegetable matter placed wholly inside is eaten and permanently lost. Inserting any part of a living creature has a 50% chance of pulling that creature inside. A creature inside can spend its action to escape with a DC 15 Strength check. A rescuer can spend its action and reach in to pull a captive out with a DC 20 Strength check, if the rescuer is not first pulled inside. A creature starting its turn inside is eaten and its body destroyed.

**Project interpretation.** Apply immediate consumption to noncreature animal or vegetable matter. A living creature, even if placed wholly inside, follows the specific creature procedure and is devoured at its turn start if still inside. Immediate consumption of living creatures would remove the escape and rescue window that the entry expressly gives. [SRD p. 210, Bag of Devouring](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-210.md)

The bag holds up to 1 cubic foot of inanimate material. Once each [campaign day](exploration.md#srd51-core-day-boundary) the bag swallows any objects still inside and ejects them onto a plane chosen by the GM, at the time the GM chooses. Piercing or tearing destroys the bag and transports its contents to a random location on the Astral Plane.

[SRD p. 210, Bag of Devouring](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-210.md)

<a id="srd51-item-candle-of-invocation"></a>
## Candle of Invocation

`srd51.item.candle-of-invocation` · Wondrous item · very rare · attunement. [SRD p. 213, Candle of Invocation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-213.md)

The candle is associated with a deity and shares that deity's alignment. [Detect Evil and Good](spells-d-e.md#srd51-spell-detect-evil-and-good) reveals the alignment. The GM chooses the deity and alignment or randomly selects the alignment on a d20.

- **Good:** Chaotic on 5–7, neutral on 12–13, lawful on 18–20.
- **Neutral on the good–evil axis:** Chaotic on 3–4, true neutral on 10–11, lawful on 16–17.
- **Evil:** Chaotic on 1–2, neutral on 8–9, lawful on 14–15.

Spend an action to light the candle and activate it. It is destroyed after 4 hours of total burning. You can extinguish it for later use, deducting burn time in 1-minute increments. While it burns, it sheds dim light in a 30-foot radius. Any creature inside the light sharing its alignment gains advantage on attacks, checks, and saves. A matching cleric or druid in the light can cast a prepared 1st-level spell without a slot, with its 1st-level effect. This provision removes the slot cost for their own casting. Their ordinary casting time and component requirements still apply.

As an alternative on the first lighting, cast [Gate](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-gate) from the candle, destroying it.

[SRD p. 213, Candle of Invocation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-213.md)

## Crystal Ball

`srd51.item.crystal-ball` · Wondrous item · very rare standard, legendary variants · attunement. [SRD p. 214, Crystal Ball](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-214.md) · [SRD p. 215, Crystal Ball continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-215.md)

The suggestion requires no separate concentration and ends when scrying ends if it has not already expired. The suggestion use returns at the next dawn.
- **True Seeing:** While scrying, gain truesight within 120 feet of the sensor, measured from the sensor.

<a id="srd51-item-cube-of-force"></a>
## Cube of Force

`srd51.item.cube-of-force` · Wondrous item · rare · attunement. [SRD p. 215, Cube of Force](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-215.md)

The marked cube is about 1 inch across and has 36 charges. It restores 1d20 expended charges each dawn. As an action press a face and spend its listed charges. If insufficient, the face does nothing. A successful activation makes a 15-foot cube of invisible force centered on you that moves with you. It lasts up to 1 minute, or until you use an action on face 6 or the item runs out of charges. Pressing a different face and paying its cost switches the effect and restarts the duration. When your movement would bring the barrier into contact with a solid object it blocks, you cannot approach that object farther while the barrier remains.

- **Face 1, 1 charge:** Block gas, wind, and fog.
- **Face 2, 2 charges:** Block nonliving matter. You may allow walls, floors, and ceilings through.
- **Face 3, 3 charges:** Block living matter.
- **Face 4, 4 charges:** Block spell effects.
- **Face 5, 5 charges:** Block everything. You may allow walls, floors, and ceilings through.
- **Face 6, no charge:** End the barrier.

Targeting the barrier with the listed effects or bringing them into contact with it drains additional charges: [Disintegrate](spells-d-e.md#srd51-spell-disintegrate) 1d12, [Horn of Blasting](magic-items-d-m.md#srd51-item-horn-of-blasting) 1d10, [Passwall](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-passwall) 1d6, [Prismatic Spray](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-prismatic-spray) 1d20, and [Wall of Fire](spells-t-z.md#srd51-spell-wall-of-fire) 1d4.

**Project interpretation.** Depletion ends the barrier immediately even if the final charge was spent to activate a face. The source lists running out of charges as a termination without limiting it to externally drained charges. Another reading lets the new effect run if the face cost itself caused depletion.

[SRD p. 215, Cube of Force](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-215.md)

## Cubic Gate

`srd51.item.cubic-gate` · Wondrous item · legendary. [SRD p. 215, Cubic Gate](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-215.md)

The 3-inch cube has six faces keyed to six distinct planes, one the Material Plane and five chosen by the GM. Use an action to press a face once to cast [Gate](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-gate) and open a portal to its plane. Alternatively use an action to press one face twice to cast [Plane Shift](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-plane-shift) to that plane, save DC 17. Each use spends 1 of its 3 charges. It restores 1d3 expended charges each dawn.

[SRD p. 215, Cubic Gate](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-215.md)

A 2-pound object left inside a Bag of Holding that is inverted spills out unharmed, whereas piercing the bag destroys it and scatters the object on the Astral Plane. The magic of +2 ammunition ends on a hit and its +2 rarity is rare.
