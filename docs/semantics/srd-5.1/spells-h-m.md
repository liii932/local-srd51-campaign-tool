# Spells H–M

Each spell's casting line gives level and school, casting time, range, components, and duration in
that order. V, S, and M mean verbal, somatic, and material. A listed price requires the named
material, and materials are consumed only where specified. A ritual tag uses the general ritual
rules. Unmodified casting and resolution use the shared
[casting procedure](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-cast-spell),
[slots](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-slots),
[components](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-components),
[range](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-range),
[targets](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-targets),
[areas](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-area),
[spell saves](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-saving-throw),
[spell attack rolls](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-attack-roll),
[duration](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-spell-duration), and
[concentration](spellcasting.md#srd51-core-concentration) rules.
HP means hit points, AC means Armor Class, and CR means challenge rating.
Find spell names and class access lists in the [spell index](spell-index.md).

## Hallow

`srd51.spell.hallow`

Level 5 evocation · 24 hours · Touch · V, S, M (herbs, oils, and incense worth at least 1,000 gp, consumed) · Until dispelled.

Touch the center of an area up to 60 feet in radius and imbue it with holy or unholy power. The
casting fails if its area overlaps an existing Hallow. Celestials, elementals, fey, fiends, and undead
cannot enter or charm, frighten, or possess creatures inside. On entering, a creature loses any charm,
fear, or possession imposed by those types. You may exempt one or more types from these restrictions.

You may also bind one additional effect from the choices below or one the DM offers. For effects
applying to creatures, designate all creatures, followers of a specific deity or leader, or creatures
of a specified sort. A creature subject to the additional effect may make a Charisma save on its first
entry on a turn or when it starts its turn inside. Success lets it ignore the additional effect until
it leaves. The save addresses the extra effect rather than the basic entry ward.

- **Courage.** Affected creatures inside cannot be frightened. **Project interpretation.**
  Suppress an existing frightened condition while Courage applies. The wording describes
  the condition as unavailable, while the basic ward separately says when a qualifying
  fear effect ends on entry. A suppressed effect can resume if still active after Courage
  stops applying.
- **Darkness.** The area is dark. Neither ordinary light nor a light spell lower than the Hallow slot
  can illuminate it.
- **Daylight.** The area has bright light. Darkness from a spell lower than the Hallow slot cannot
  extinguish it.
- **Energy Protection.** Affected creatures inside gain resistance to one chosen damage type other
  than bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing.
- **Energy Vulnerability.** Affected creatures inside gain vulnerability to one chosen damage type
  other than bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing.
- **Everlasting Rest.** Bodies interred inside cannot become undead.
- **Extradimensional Interference.** Affected creatures cannot teleport or use extradimensional or
  interplanar movement or travel.
- **Fear.** Affected creatures are frightened inside.
- **Silence.** Sound cannot originate within the area or reach it from outside.
  **Project interpretation.** Speech and verbal spell components are unavailable within
  the area because no sound can emanate from it. Reading “emanate” as only barring sound at
  the boundary would allow speech inside. The text describes an area of silence and grants
  no separate immunity to thunder damage.
- **Tongues.** An affected creature can communicate with any other creature inside even without a
  shared language.

## Hallucinatory Terrain

## Imprisonment

`srd51.spell.imprisonment`

Level 9 abjuration · 1 minute · 30 feet · V, S, M (a vellum picture or carved statuette of the target and a mode-specific component worth at least 500 gp per target Hit Die) · Until dispelled.

Choose a creature you can see in range. Success on its Wisdom save gives it immunity to future
castings of this spell by you. Failure imprisons it in the chosen mode. While imprisoned it needs no
food, water, or air and does not age. Divination spells cannot find or perceive it.

- **Burial.** A force sphere just large enough to hold the creature entombs it far underground.
  Nothing passes through, and teleportation and planar travel cannot cross in either direction. The
  required component is a small mithral orb.
- **Chaining.** Rooted chains restrain the target and prevent it from moving or being moved by any
  means until release. Use a fine chain of precious metal.
- **Hedged Prison.** Transport the target to a tiny demiplane warded against teleportation and planar
  travel. Choose a confined structure such as a labyrinth, cage, or tower. Use a jade miniature of
  that prison.
- **Minimus Containment.** Reduce the target to 1 inch tall inside a gemstone or similar object. Light
  passes through, so the prisoner and outsiders can see through it, but nothing else can pass,
  including by teleportation or planar travel. The gemstone cannot be cut or broken during the spell.
  Use a large transparent gemstone.
- **Slumber.** The target falls asleep and cannot be awakened while imprisoned. Use rare
  sleep-inducing herbs.

During casting you may set a release condition. The DM must agree it is reasonable and has a chance to
occur. It may depend on a name, identity, or deity. Otherwise it must use observable actions or
qualities rather than an abstraction such as level, class, or HP. Meeting the condition releases the
creature and ends the spell.

Only slot-9 [Dispel Magic](spells-d-e.md#srd51-spell-dispel-magic) can dispel the effect, targeting
the prison or the specific mode component. A mode component maintains only one prison at a time.
Reusing it for another casting immediately frees the previous prisoner.

[SRD p. 156, Imprisonment](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-156.md) · [SRD p. 157, Imprisonment continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-157.md) · [SRD p. 136, Dispel Magic](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-136.md)

- A Magic Missile roll of 3 on the d4 gives 4 force damage per dart. Under the shared-roll and simultaneous-damage interpretation, two darts on one creature cause one 8-damage event and a third dart on another causes a 4-damage event. The first creature makes one concentration save if concentrating, or takes one damage-triggered death save failure if already at 0 HP and the damage is below its instant-death threshold. [SRD p. 161, Magic Missile](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-161.md) · [SRD p. 98, Damage at 0 Hit Points](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-098.md) · [SRD p. 102, Concentration](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-102.md)
- A creature can save to ignore Hallow’s extra Fear effect until it leaves, but that save does not allow a barred fiend to enter. A casting that overlaps another Hallow fails outright. [SRD p. 152, Hallow](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-152.md)
- After Haste ends, the target loses the ability to move or act through its next turn even if it never used the extra action. [SRD p. 153, Haste](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-153.md)
- A guard who succeeds on Heat Metal’s Constitution save but keeps the heated object has disadvantage on attack rolls and checks until the start of the caster’s next turn. [SRD p. 153, Heat Metal](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-153.md) · [SRD p. 154, Heat Metal continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-154.md)
- The lid of an Arcane Lock chest can open normally while Knock suppresses that spell for 10 minutes. The knock can be heard 300 feet away. [SRD p. 119, Arcane Lock continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-119.md) · [SRD p. 158, Knock](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-158.md)
- An Imprisonment release condition based on reaching character level 10 is invalid. A specific observable action may qualify if the DM finds it reasonable and plausible. A slot-8 Dispel Magic cannot end this prison. [SRD p. 156, Imprisonment](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-156.md) · [SRD p. 157, Imprisonment continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-157.md)
- A Magic Jar caster in a dying host dies even on a successful save if the jar is 101 feet away. With a living original body within 100 feet, ending the spell instead returns the caster to that body. [SRD p. 161, Magic Jar continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-161.md)
- A Move Earth casting that affects a 40-foot square may dig a trench up to 20 feet deep in 10 minutes. Solid stone cannot be reshaped as soil. [SRD p. 166, Move Earth](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-166.md) · [SRD p. 167, Move Earth continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-167.md)
