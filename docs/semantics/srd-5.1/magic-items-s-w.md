# Magic items S–W

Each item states category, rarity, and any attunement requirement. Apply the shared
[wearing and wielding](magic-items.md#srd51-item-rules-wearing-and-wielding),
[attunement](magic-items.md#srd51-item-rules-attunement),
[activation](magic-items.md#srd51-item-rules-activation),
[consumable](magic-items.md#srd51-item-rules-consumables),
[item spell](magic-items.md#srd51-item-rules-spells), and
[charge](magic-items.md#srd51-item-rules-charges) rules except where an entry specifies a
different procedure. HP means hit points and AC means Armor Class. Find other named items
in the [magic-item index](magic-item-index.md).

`srd51.item.scarab-of-protection`

Wondrous item · Legendary · Attunement.

<a id="srd51-item-spell-scroll"></a>

## Spell Scroll

`srd51.item.spell-scroll`

Scroll · Rarity varies by spell level · No attunement.

The scroll holds one spell in a magical cipher. It is readable for casting only if the spell is on one
of your class spell lists. Read it and cast using the spell's normal casting time and no material
components. Under the [general item spell rule](magic-items.md#srd51-item-rules-spells), reading is
the activation and the entry specifies no additional verbal or somatic requirements. Keep the spell's
concentration requirement. Completion makes the writing fade and the scroll turn to dust. Interrupted
casting leaves the scroll intact.

If the spell is higher-level than you can normally cast, make a spellcasting ability check against DC
10 + the scroll's spell level. Success allows the cast. On failure the spell disappears from the
scroll without effect. Use the scroll's fixed save DC and attack bonus, if relevant.

- **Common.** Cantrip and level 1 both have save DC 13 and +5 to hit.
- **Uncommon.** Level 2 has DC 13 and +5 to hit, level 3 DC 15 and +7.
- **Rare.** Level 4 has DC 15 and +7, level 5 DC 17 and +9.
- **Very rare.** Level 6 has DC 17 and +9, and levels 7 and 8 both have DC 18 and +10.
- **Legendary.** Level 9 has DC 19 and +11.

A wizard spell on a scroll can be [copied into a
spellbook](classes-warlock-wizard.md#srd51-class-wizard-spellbook) under the usual copying procedure,
plus an Intelligence (Arcana) check against DC 10 + spell level. Success copies it. Either result
destroys the scroll.

<a id="srd51-item-sphere-of-annihilation"></a>

## Sphere of Annihilation

`srd51.item.sphere-of-annihilation`

Wondrous item · Legendary · No attunement.

The hovering 2-foot-diameter sphere obliterates matter wholly engulfed when passing into it or when it
passes through that matter. An artifact not susceptible to sphere damage passes through unharmed.
Other things touching without full engulfment take 4d10 force damage.

The sphere is stationary without control. Within 60 feet, an action and DC 25 Intelligence (Arcana)
check can gain control of an uncontrolled sphere. Success moves it in a chosen direction by up to 5
feet times your Intelligence modifier, minimum 5 feet. Failure moves it 10 feet toward you. A creature
whose space it enters makes a DC 13 Dexterity save, taking 4d10 force damage from contact on failure.
Attempting to take control from another creature instead contests Intelligence (Arcana) checks. The
winner controls it and can move it as normal. A [Talisman of the
Sphere](#srd51-item-talisman-of-the-sphere) can modify the check and movement.

**Project interpretation.** Use the same action and 60-foot reach to contest control. Once in control,
use an action for another movement without another acquisition check. The source defines the action to
take control and says the winner can move it normally, with no separate per-movement check. This
retains a cost for movement without repeatedly reacquiring something still under your control.

Contact with a planar portal or extradimensional space has a separate percentile outcome chosen by
roll. On 01–50 the sphere is destroyed. On 51–85 it passes through the portal or enters the space. On
86–100 a rift sends the sphere and every creature and object within 180 feet to a random plane.

[SRD p. 242, Sphere of Annihilation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-242.md) · [SRD p. 243, Sphere of Annihilation continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-243.md) · [SRD p. 247, Talisman of the Sphere](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-247.md) · [SRD p. 148, Gate](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-148.md) · [SRD p. 233, Portable Hole](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-233.md)

## Staff of Charming

`srd51.item.staff-of-charming`

Staff · Rare · Attunement by a bard, cleric, druid, sorcerer, warlock, or wizard.

Usable as a magic quarterstaff. It has 10 charges and regains 1d8 + 2 spent charges each dawn. On the
last charge roll d20. A 1 leaves a nonmagical quarterstaff. While holding it, spend an action and 1
charge to cast [Charm Person](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-charm-person),
[Command](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-command), or [Comprehend
Languages](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-comprehend-languages) with your spell save DC.

While holding it, you can change a failed save against an enchantment spell that targets only you into
success, once per dawn. After succeeding on such a save, whether naturally or by the staff, you can
spend a reaction and 1 charge to turn the spell against its caster as if you cast it.

## Staff of the Magi

`srd51.item.staff-of-the-magi`

Staff · Legendary · Attunement by a sorcerer, warlock, or wizard.

It is a magic quarterstaff with +2 to attack and damage. Holding it grants +2 to spell attack rolls
and advantage on saves against spells. It has 50 charges and regains 4d6 + 2 spent charges each dawn.
Spending the last charge calls for a d20. On 20 regain 1d12 + 1 charges.

While holding it, when another creature casts a spell targeting only you, you can spend a reaction to
absorb the spell. It has no effect and the staff gains charges equal to the spell's level. If the
total exceeds 50, it immediately explodes as a [Retributive
Strike](#srd51-item-rules-retributive-strike). **Project interpretation.** The over-cap total powers
the blast. The rule first adds the spell's levels and then tests the total, so clamping to 50 would
lose the overflow that caused the explosion.

While holding the staff, spend an action and the indicated charges to cast one spell using your spell
save DC and spellcasting ability.

- **2 charges.** [Flaming Sphere](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-flaming-sphere),
  [Invisibility](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-invisibility), [Knock](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-knock), or
  [Web](spells-t-z.md#srd51-spell-web).
- **3 charges.** [Dispel Magic](spells-d-e.md#srd51-spell-dispel-magic).
- **4 charges.** [Ice Storm](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-ice-storm) or [Wall of
  Fire](spells-t-z.md#srd51-spell-wall-of-fire).
- **5 charges.** [Passwall](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-passwall) or
  [Telekinesis](spells-t-z.md#srd51-spell-telekinesis).
- **7 charges.** [Conjure Elemental](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-conjure-elemental), level-7

<a id="srd51-item-rules-retributive-strike"></a>

### Retributive Strike

`srd51.item-rules.retributive-strike`

Break the staff across your knee or on a solid surface as an action, destroying it and releasing a
30-foot-radius explosion centered on it. You have a 50 percent chance to move instantly to a random
plane and avoid the blast. Otherwise you take force damage equal to 16 times the remaining charges
without a save. Each other creature in range makes a DC 17 Dexterity save. Failure deals 8 times the
charges within 10 feet, 6 times the charges beyond 10 through 20 feet, or 4 times the charges beyond
20 through 30 feet. Success halves the damage.

**Project interpretation.** Use force damage for the whole blast. The prose specifies force for the
wielder and supplies no distinct damage type for other creatures in the same explosion.

there without contradicting the spell's origin. The general centering instruction governs freely
placeable areas.

## Well of Many Worlds

`srd51.item.well-of-many-worlds`

Wondrous item · Legendary · No attunement.

The folded black cloth unfolds to a 6-foot circle. As an action place it unfolded on a solid surface
to open a two-way portal. The DM decides its destination in another world or plane every time. As an
action take its edges and fold it to close the portal. Once it opens a portal it cannot open another
for 1d8 hours.

[SRD p. 250, Well of Many Worlds](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-250.md)

## Wind Fan

`srd51.item.wind-fan`

Wondrous item · Uncommon · No attunement.

While holding it, use an action to cast [Gust of Wind](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-gust-of-wind) with
save DC 13. Each repeat use before dawn increases the chance of failure and destruction by 20
percentage points. If this occurs, it produces no effect and becomes torn nonmagical cloth.

[SRD p. 250, Wind Fan](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-250.md) · [SRD p. 251, Wind Fan continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-251.md) · [SRD p. 152, Gust of Wind](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-152.md)

## Winged Boots

`srd51.item.winged-boots`

Wondrous item · Uncommon · Attunement.

While wearing them, gain flying speed equal to walking speed. Spend up to 4 hours of flight in one or
several flights, each drawing at least 1 minute of capacity. If airborne when that capacity expires,
descend at 30 feet per round until landing. Every 12 hours without use restores 2 hours of flying
time.

[SRD p. 251, Winged Boots](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-251.md) · [SRD p. 86, Falling](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-086.md)

## Wings of Flying

`srd51.item.wings-of-flying`

Wondrous item · Rare · Attunement.

While wearing the cloak, speak the command word as an action to turn it into bird or bat wings and
gain flying speed 60 feet. It lasts 1 hour or until you speak the word again as an action. Once the
wings disappear, wait 1d12 hours before using them again.

[SRD p. 251, Wings of Flying](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-251.md)
