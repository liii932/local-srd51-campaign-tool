# Dragons

The [creature rules](creatures.md#srd51-creature-rules-stat-block) apply to each
of these 41 profiles. In each damage entry the first value is the listed average
and the parenthesized value is the dice expression. Movement distances, reach,
and areas are in feet. Each profile grants only the listed traits and actions,
including the presence or absence of a particular ability at that age.

## Shared dragon abilities

<a id="srd51-dragon-rules-ice-walk"></a>

### Ice Walk

`srd51.dragon-rules.ice-walk`

A white dragon with this trait can cross and climb ice without an ability check. Difficult terrain consisting of ice or snow imposes no extra movement cost. **Project interpretation.** The source prints “extra moment.” Read this as extra movement, the quantity normally added by [difficult terrain](combat.md#srd51-core-combat-difficult-terrain). [SRD p. 288, Ancient White Dragon, Ice Walk](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-288.md) [SRD p. 289, Adult White Dragon, Ice Walk](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-289.md) [SRD p. 290, Young White Dragon, Ice Walk](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-290.md) [SRD p. 92, Difficult Terrain](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-092.md)

<a id="srd51-dragon-rules-frightful-presence"></a>

### Frightful Presence

`srd51.dragon-rules.frightful-presence`

Choose any creatures within 120 feet that are aware of the dragon. Each chosen creature rolls a Wisdom save against the profile's DC. Failure frightens the creature for up to 1 minute. Each time its own turn ends, the frightened creature may attempt another save; success removes this fear. A successful save or the end of the effect for that creature grants it immunity to that dragon's Frightful Presence for 24 hours. A listed Multiattack can incorporate the use before making the attacks. [SRD p. 280, Ancient Black Dragon, Multiattack and Frightful Presence](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-280.md) [SRD p. 299, Adult Gold Dragon, Multiattack and Frightful Presence](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-299.md)

<a id="srd51-dragon-rules-wing-attack"></a>

### Wing Attack

`srd51.dragon-rules.wing-attack`

Spend two legendary actions. Every creature in the profile's radius makes the indicated Dexterity save. On failure it takes the listed bludgeoning damage and falls prone. After that resolution, the dragon may fly a distance no greater than half its flying speed. [SRD p. 281, Ancient and Adult Black Dragon, Wing Attack](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-281.md) [SRD p. 260, Legendary Actions](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-260.md)

<a id="srd51-dragon-rules-breath-weapons"></a>

### Breath Weapons

`srd51.dragon-rules.breath-weapons`

A single-breath profile uses its listed action with [Recharge 5–6](creatures.md#srd51-creature-rules-limited-usage). A metallic dragon with Breath Weapons has one Recharge 5–6 use shared by its two options. On each use choose one option, and apply its own area and save. Expending either option spends the shared use. [SRD p. 280, Ancient Black Dragon, Acid Breath](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-280.md) [SRD p. 291, Ancient Brass Dragon, Breath Weapons](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-291.md) [SRD p. 259, Limited Usage](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-259.md)

#### Damage Breath

`srd51.dragon-rules.damage-breath`

Every creature in the listed line or cone makes the listed save. Failure takes the stated damage of the stated type, and success takes half that damage. The profile specifies the applicable save ability and DC. Dragon Turtle's Steam Breath applies a specific underwater exception. [SRD p. 280, Ancient Black Dragon, Acid Breath](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-280.md) [SRD p. 285, Ancient Green Dragon, Poison Breath](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-285.md) [SRD p. 304, Dragon Turtle, Steam Breath](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-304.md)

#### Sleep Breath

`srd51.dragon-rules.sleep-breath`

Each creature in the listed cone rolls the listed Constitution save. On failure it falls unconscious for the profile's duration. Taking damage or being awakened by someone spending an action ends this effect for that creature. `srd51.dragon-rules.repulsion-breath`

#### Slowing Breath

`srd51.dragon-rules.slowing-breath`

Each creature in the listed cone makes the listed Constitution save. On failure, for 1 minute it cannot take reactions, its speed is halved, and on each of its turns it can make at most one attack and can take an action or a bonus action, but cannot take both. It may repeat the save at the end of each of its own turns, ending the effect on success. [SRD p. 296, Ancient Copper Dragon, Slowing Breath](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-296.md) [SRD p. 297, Adult Copper Dragon, Slowing Breath](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-297.md) [SRD p. 298, Young Copper Dragon and Copper Dragon Wyrmling, Slowing Breath](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-298.md)

<a id="srd51-dragon-rules-change-shape"></a>

### Change Shape

`srd51.dragon-rules.change-shape`

A dragon with this action magically takes the form of a humanoid or beast with CR no higher than its own, or returns to its true form. Death restores true form. Choose whether worn and carried equipment is absorbed into the form or remains borne by it. In the new form retain alignment, hit points, Hit Dice, speech, proficiencies, Legendary Resistance, any existing lair actions, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma, and the Change Shape action. Other statistics and capabilities are replaced with the new form's, except that its class features and legendary actions are unavailable. The [general form rule](creatures.md#srd51-creature-rules-legendary-creatures) also precludes acquiring the new form's lair actions or regional effects. [SRD p. 291, Ancient Brass Dragon, Change Shape](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-291.md) [SRD p. 293, Ancient Bronze Dragon, Change Shape](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-293.md) [SRD p. 294, Adult Bronze Dragon, Change Shape](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-294.md) [SRD p. 296, Ancient Copper Dragon, Change Shape](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-296.md) [SRD p. 299, Ancient Gold Dragon, Change Shape](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-299.md) [SRD p. 300, Adult Gold Dragon, Change Shape](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-300.md) [SRD p. 301, Ancient Silver Dragon, Change Shape](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-301.md) [SRD p. 302, Adult Silver Dragon, Change Shape](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-302.md) [SRD p. 260, Legendary Creatures](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-260.md)

The local dragon entries provide no optional innate spellcasting variant,
dragon-specific lair action options, or dragon-specific regional effects.
The general dragon type description mentions innate magic, and the Change Shape
action can retain existing lair actions. Those references supply no missing
ability parameters. [SRD p. 254, Type, Dragons](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-254.md) [SRD p. 260, Lair Actions and Regional Effects](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-260.md) [SRD p. 291, Ancient Brass Dragon, Change Shape](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-291.md) [SRD p. 304, Dragon Turtle](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-304.md)

## Source-checked cases

- **Ordinary.** An Adult Blue Dragon's Lightning Breath is a 90-foot line 5 feet wide. A creature inside that line takes 66 (12d10) lightning on a failed DC 19 Dexterity save and half on success. [SRD p. 283, Adult Blue Dragon, Lightning Breath](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-283.md)
- **Boundary.** A Brass Dragon Wyrmling's Sleep Breath is a 15-foot cone, DC 11 Constitution. A failed save causes unconsciousness for 1 minute unless damage or an action to wake the creature ends it sooner. Using Sleep Breath expends the same recharge use as its Fire Breath. [SRD p. 293, Brass Dragon Wyrmling, Breath Weapons](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-293.md)
- **Exception.** A creature fully underwater ordinarily has fire resistance. That underwater resistance does not apply to Dragon Turtle's Steam Breath. A successful Constitution save still halves the damage. [SRD p. 99, Underwater Combat](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-099.md) [SRD p. 304, Dragon Turtle, Steam Breath](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-304.md)
- **Invalid.** Adult Brass Dragon has no Change Shape action in its profile. Ancient Brass Dragon has the action but cannot take a CR 21 humanoid or beast form because its own CR is 20. [SRD p. 290, Ancient Brass Dragon, Challenge](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-290.md) [SRD p. 291, Ancient Brass Dragon, Change Shape and Adult Brass Dragon](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-291.md) [SRD p. 292, Adult Brass Dragon continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-292.md)
