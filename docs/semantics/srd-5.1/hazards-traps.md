# Traps

<a id="srd51-hazard-traps"></a>
## Trap operation

`srd51.hazard.traps`

Mechanical traps operate through physical mechanisms. Magical device traps activate spell effects, while spell traps are spells that function as traps, such as [Glyph of Warding](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-glyph-of-warding) and [Symbol](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-symbol). A trap's description supplies what activates it, its effects, and ways to detect, disarm, or bypass it. Typical triggers include entering an area, stepping onto a plate or false floor, touching an object, pulling a wire, or using a door or lock. A magical trigger can have additional criteria, such as a password that lets a creature pass safely. [SRD p. 195, Traps, Traps in Play, and Triggering a Trap](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-195.md)

<a id="srd51-hazard-detecting-and-disabling-traps"></a>
## Detecting, disabling, and bypassing traps

`srd51.hazard.detecting-and-disabling-traps`

An active search can use Wisdom (Perception) against the trap's detection DC. Compare that DC with each creature's passive Wisdom (Perception) to see whether it notices the trap in passing. A description can require other checks, such as Intelligence (Investigation) to identify how to disable it and a Dexterity check with thieves' tools for the actual work. Successful detection can allow permanent disarming or a temporary bypass. [SRD p. 195, Detecting and Disabling a Trap](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-195.md)

Any character may attempt Intelligence (Arcana) to detect or disarm a magic trap in addition to its stated check options, using the same DC for the corresponding task. [Dispel Magic](spells-d-e.md#srd51-spell-dispel-magic) can disable most magic traps using the check DC given by the trap. [SRD p. 195, Detecting and Disabling a Trap](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-195.md)

Resolve physically decisive actions directly. For example, lifting a rug that visibly exposes a hidden plate discovers it without a roll. Triggering a trap from safety or putting a barrier in the path of its effect can foil harm even if activation still occurs. A trap may also have a built-in disabling control or route around it. The GM uses the description and the participants' actions to adjudicate unlisted methods. [SRD p. 195, Detecting and Disabling a Trap](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-195.md)

<a id="srd51-hazard-trap-severity"></a>
## Trap severity

`srd51.hazard.trap-severity`

The GM can use the following suggested save DCs, attack bonuses, and damage when designing a trap. Existing traps use their own values. For each severity below, damage dice are listed in character level order **1–4, 5–10, 11–16, 17–20**. [SRD p. 196, Trap Effects, Trap Save DCs and Attack Bonuses, and Damage Severity by Level](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-196.md)

- **Setback.** Serious harm is unlikely at the intended level. Save DC 10–11, attack bonus +3 to +5, damage 1d10 / 2d10 / 4d10 / 10d10.
- **Dangerous.** Likely serious injury and possible death. Save DC 12–15, attack bonus +6 to +8, damage 2d10 / 4d10 / 10d10 / 18d10.
- **Deadly.** Likely fatal at the intended level. Save DC 16–20, attack bonus +9 to +12, damage 4d10 / 10d10 / 18d10 / 24d10.

<a id="srd51-hazard-complex-traps"></a>
## Complex traps

`srd51.hazard.complex-traps`

When activated, a complex trap rolls initiative with the bonus in its description. It operates again on its turn each round, typically taking an action, making attacks, or changing an ongoing hazard. Its description supplies the repeated behavior and termination. Detection, disabling, and bypass otherwise work as for other traps. [SRD p. 196, Complex Traps](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-196.md)

## Sample traps

Each damage expression below gives the source's listed fixed value and dice when the source supplies both.

<a id="srd51-hazard-collapsing-roof"></a>
### Collapsing Roof

`srd51.hazard.collapsing-roof`

Mechanical. A trip wire runs between supporting beams 3 inches above the ground and can be spotted at DC 10. Disable it safely with a DC 15 Dexterity check using thieves' tools. Without thieves' tools, attempt the check with disadvantage using an edged tool or weapon. A failed disabling check triggers collapse. Inspection readily shows the beams are merely wedged in place, and the visibly damaged ceiling is plainly unstable. A creature can trigger the collapse deliberately by using an action to knock over a beam. [SRD p. 196, Collapsing Roof](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-196.md)

On activation, each creature under the unstable area makes a DC 15 Dexterity save. The collapse deals 22 (4d10) bludgeoning damage on failure or half on success. Rubble makes the affected floor difficult terrain after activation. [SRD p. 196, Collapsing Roof](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-196.md)

<a id="srd51-hazard-falling-net"></a>
### Falling Net

`srd51.hazard.falling-net`

Mechanical. The concealed overhead net has a trip wire 3 inches off the ground between trees or columns. Detection DC is 10 for the wire and net. Break the wire safely with a DC 15 Dexterity check using thieves' tools, or with disadvantage using an edged tool or weapon if you lack thieves' tools. Failure releases the net. [SRD p. 196, Falling Net](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-196.md)

Activation covers a 10-foot square and restrains creatures in it. Each affected creature makes a DC 10 Strength save and also falls prone on failure. A creature can spend its action on a DC 10 Strength check to free itself or another captive within reach. The net has AC 10 and 20 HP. Dealing 5 slashing damage to the net destroys a 5-foot-square section and frees the creatures under that section. This is the trap's own damage rule. [SRD p. 196, Falling Net](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-196.md) · [SRD p. 197, Falling Net continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-197.md)

<a id="srd51-hazard-fire-breathing-statue"></a>
### Fire-Breathing Statue

`srd51.hazard.fire-breathing-statue`

Magical. Detection DC 15 reveals the hidden plate and faint scorch marks. Magic detection reveals an evocation aura about the statue. More than 20 pounds of weight on the plate makes the statue emit a 30-foot fire cone. Affected creatures make DC 13 Dexterity saves, taking 22 (4d10) fire damage on failure or half on success. Wedging the plate with an iron spike or other suitable object prevents activation. A successful DC 13 Dispel Magic check on the statue destroys the trap. [SRD p. 197, Fire-Breathing Statue](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-197.md)

<a id="srd51-hazard-pits"></a>
### Pits

`srd51.hazard.pits`

These mechanical traps use the ordinary [falling](exploration.md#srd51-core-falling) rules for the actual depth, plus any listed additional effects. [SRD p. 197, Pits](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-197.md) · [SRD p. 86, Falling](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-086.md)

<a id="srd51-hazard-simple-pit"></a>
#### Simple Pit

`srd51.hazard.simple-pit`

Spot the cloth camouflage over the hole at DC 10. A creature stepping onto it falls and pulls the cloth into the pit. The usual depth is 10 feet, with deeper examples possible. [SRD p. 197, Simple Pit](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-197.md)

<a id="srd51-hazard-hidden-pit"></a>
#### Hidden Pit

`srd51.hazard.hidden-pit`

A DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check spots the absence of traffic across the cover, and DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) confirms it is a pit lid. A creature stepping onto it falls when the cover swings open. Typical depth is 10 or 20 feet, with deeper examples possible. After detection, wedge a spike or similar object between cover and floor or hold the cover shut with [Arcane Lock](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-arcane-lock) or similar magic to cross safely. [SRD p. 197, Hidden Pit](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-197.md)

<a id="srd51-hazard-locking-pit"></a>
#### Locking Pit

`srd51.hazard.locking-pit`

Use the Hidden Pit rules except that the lid springs shut after the victim falls. Pry it open with DC 20 Strength or smash it. A trapped character can disable the spring with a DC 15 Dexterity check using thieves' tools if able to see and reach the mechanism. Some versions also have an opening control, often concealed nearby. [SRD p. 197, Locking Pit](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-197.md)

<a id="srd51-hazard-spiked-pit"></a>
#### Spiked Pit

`srd51.hazard.spiked-pit`

Use a simple, hidden, or locking pit and add bottom spikes that deal 11 (2d10) piercing damage to a falling creature in addition to the fall's damage. Some versions coat the spikes with poison. A creature taking piercing damage from those spikes makes a DC 13 Constitution save and takes 22 (4d10) poison damage on failure or half on success. [SRD p. 197, Spiked Pit](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-197.md)

<a id="srd51-hazard-poison-darts"></a>
### Poison Darts

`srd51.hazard.poison-darts`

Mechanical. Concealed tubes have DC 15 detection. A DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check reveals the pressure plate by examining floor materials. Multiple plates can each have their own darts. Wedging the plate stops activation and plugging the tube holes with cloth or wax stops the contained darts from firing. [SRD p. 197, Poison Darts](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-197.md)

More than 20 pounds on a plate fires four darts. For each dart, randomly select a target within 10 feet of the plate and roll a ranged attack with +8. Ignore vision for those attacks. With no eligible target, the darts hit nothing. A hit deals 2 (1d4) piercing damage and calls for a DC 15 Constitution save, with 11 (2d10) poison damage on failure or half on success. [SRD p. 197, Poison Darts](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-197.md) · [SRD p. 198, Poison Darts continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-198.md)

<a id="srd51-hazard-poison-needle"></a>
### Poison Needle

`srd51.hazard.poison-needle`

Mechanical. Opening the trapped lock without its proper key or unsuccessfully picking it triggers the needle. It extends straight out by 3 inches. A creature in that line within range takes 1 piercing and 11 (2d10) poison damage, and a failed DC 15 Constitution save also poisons it for 1 hour. The damage does not depend on the save. A DC 20 Intelligence (Investigation) check reveals the altered lock, and a DC 15 Dexterity check using thieves' tools disarms it by removing the needle. [SRD p. 198, Poison Needle](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-198.md)

<a id="srd51-hazard-rolling-sphere"></a>
### Rolling Sphere

`srd51.hazard.rolling-sphere`

Mechanical. At least 20 pounds on the plate opens the ceiling trapdoor, releasing a solid 10-foot-diameter sphere. A DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check spots the plate and trapdoor. A DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check while inspecting the corresponding surface reveals the plate in the floor or the door in the ceiling. Wedging the pressure plate prevents activation. [SRD p. 198, Rolling Sphere](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-198.md)

On activation all creatures present roll initiative and the sphere rolls with +8. Each turn the sphere moves 60 feet in a straight line at its initial speed. It can cross a creature's space. A creature can cross its space as difficult terrain. Whenever either enters the other's space while the sphere is rolling, the creature makes a DC 15 Dexterity save. On failure it takes 55 (10d10) bludgeoning damage and falls prone. A wall or similar barrier stops the sphere. It can follow a gentle curve but cannot turn a corner. A creature within 5 feet can spend an action attempting a DC 20 Strength check. Success reduces sphere speed by 15 feet, and at speed 0 it stops and ceases to be a threat. [SRD p. 198, Rolling Sphere](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-198.md)

<a id="srd51-hazard-sphere-of-annihilation"></a>
### Sphere of Annihilation trap

`srd51.hazard.sphere-of-annihilation`

Magical. The stone face's roughly circular mouth is 2 feet wide, with impenetrable magical darkness inside and no sound issuing from it. Matter entering the sphere is immediately obliterated. A successful DC 20 Intelligence (Arcana) check identifies the contained [Sphere of Annihilation](magic-items-s-w.md#srd51-item-sphere-of-annihilation). This sphere cannot be controlled or moved. The remaining item rules apply. An optional enchantment on the stone face makes designated creatures want to approach and enter its mouth, otherwise following the Sympathy part of [Antipathy/Sympathy](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-antipathy-sympathy). Successful Dispel Magic against DC 18 removes that enchantment. [SRD p. 198, Sphere of Annihilation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-198.md)

**Project interpretation.** Read “entering” as passing into the sphere, and use the normal item's specific rules for partial contact and artifacts. Partial contact of something other than a protected artifact deals 4d10 force damage, and an artifact ordinarily passes through unharmed unless vulnerable to the sphere. Reading “any matter” as overriding both item exceptions would conflict with the trap's express retention of its normal item behavior beyond loss of movement and control. [SRD p. 198, Sphere of Annihilation trap](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-198.md) · [SRD p. 242, Sphere of Annihilation item](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-242.md)

**Acceptance cases.** Exactly 20 pounds triggers the Rolling Sphere but fails the strict weight predicate of Fire-Breathing Statue and Poison Darts. A creature that succeeds on the Falling Net's Strength save remains restrained but does not fall prone from the trap. A successful Poison Needle Constitution save prevents Poisoned and leaves the piercing and poison damage unchanged. A pressure plate clearly exposed by moving its cover is found without a roll. [SRD p. 195, Detecting and Disabling a Trap](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-195.md) · [SRD pp. 196–197, Falling Net](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-196.md) · [SRD p. 197, Fire-Breathing Statue and Poison Darts](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-197.md) · [SRD p. 198, Poison Needle and Rolling Sphere](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-198.md)
