## Customizing NPCs

`srd51.creature-rules.customizing-npcs`

These humanoid stat blocks can represent humans and other ancestries. Use the
[general creature rules](creatures.md#srd51-creature-rules-stat-block) with the
profile and any modifications. The GM can customize an NPC through the following
choices. [SRD p. 395, Nonplayer Characters and Customizing NPCs](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-395.md)

- Add [ancestry traits](ancestries.md#srd51-ancestry-traits). For example, a halfling NPC druid can use a 25-foot speed and Lucky. Adding ancestry traits does not change the NPC's challenge rating.
- Replace an NPC spellcaster's spell with a spell of the same level from the same list. A swap under this rule does not change the NPC's challenge rating.
- Change armor, switch weapons, or add weapons. Adjustments to AC and damage may change the NPC's challenge rating.
- Give an NPC magic items. Powerful NPCs are more likely to have them, such as an archmage with a magic staff or wand and potions or scrolls. A potent damage-dealing magic item can change the NPC's challenge rating. [SRD p. 395, Customizing NPCs](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-395.md)

The profile's caster level and slot capacities describe that NPC. In a damage
entry the value before parentheses is the source average and the parenthesized
dice expression is the rolling alternative. Attack ranges give normal and long
range when two numbers appear. The listed target kind distinguishes a creature
from a target that can also be an object. [SRD p. 258, Spellcasting](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-258.md) [SRD p. 259, Melee and Ranged Attacks](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-259.md)

## Acolyte

`srd51.creature.acolyte`

**Profile.** Medium humanoid (any race) · any alignment · CR 1/4 (50 XP) · 9 average HP (2d8) · AC 10 · walk 30 ft.

**Abilities.** Str 10 (+0) · Dex 10 (+0) · Con 10 (+0) · Int 10 (+0) · Wis 14 (+2) · Cha 11 (+0). Skill bonuses Medicine +4, Religion +2.

**Awareness and defenses.** Passive Perception 12. Languages any one language (usually Common). [SRD p. 395, Acolyte](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-395.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-acolyte-spellcasting"></a> **Trait — Spellcasting.** `srd51.creature.acolyte.spellcasting` Use [creature spellcasting](creatures.md#srd51-creature-rules-spellcasting). A 1st-level spellcaster with Wisdom, save DC 12, and +4 on spell attacks. It has the following cleric spells prepared. Cantrips at will are [Light](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-light), [Sacred Flame](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-sacred-flame), and [Thaumaturgy](spells-t-z.md#srd51-spell-thaumaturgy). Level 1 has three slots and the spells [Bless](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-bless), [Cure Wounds](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-cure-wounds), and [Sanctuary](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-sanctuary). [SRD p. 395, Acolyte, Spellcasting](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-395.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-acolyte-club"></a> **Action — Club.** `srd51.creature.acolyte.club` Melee weapon, +2, reach 5 feet, one target. A hit deals 2 (1d4) bludgeoning damage. [SRD p. 395, Acolyte, Club](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-395.md)

**Role in the campaign.** A junior member of a clergy, generally answerable to a priest, who conducts temple duties with minor divine spellcasting. [SRD p. 395, Acolyte](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-395.md)

## Archmage

`srd51.creature.archmage`

**Profile.** Medium humanoid (any race) · any alignment · CR 12 (8,400 XP) · 99 average HP (18d8 + 18) · AC 12 (15 with [Mage Armor](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-mage-armor)) · walk 30 ft.

**Abilities.** Str 10 (+0) · Dex 14 (+2) · Con 12 (+1) · Int 20 (+5) · Wis 15 (+2) · Cha 16 (+3). Save bonuses Int +9, Wis +6. Skill bonuses Arcana +13, History +13.

**Awareness and defenses.** Passive Perception 12. Languages any six languages. Damage resistance to damage from spells. [Stoneskin](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-stoneskin) supplies resistance to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing. [SRD p. 395, Archmage](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-395.md) [SRD p. 396, Archmage continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-396.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-archmage-magic-resistance"></a> **Trait — Magic Resistance.** `srd51.creature.archmage.magic-resistance` Apply [Magic Resistance](creatures.md#srd51-creature-rules-magic-resistance). [SRD p. 395, Archmage, Magic Resistance](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-395.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-archmage-spellcasting"></a> **Trait — Spellcasting.** `srd51.creature.archmage.spellcasting` Use [creature spellcasting](creatures.md#srd51-creature-rules-spellcasting). An 18th-level spellcaster with Intelligence, save DC 17, and +9 on spell attacks. It casts [Disguise Self](spells-d-e.md#srd51-spell-disguise-self) and [Invisibility](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-invisibility) at will. Its prepared wizard cantrips at will are [Fire Bolt](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-fire-bolt), [Light](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-light), [Mage Hand](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-mage-hand), [Prestidigitation](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-prestidigitation), and [Shocking Grasp](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-shocking-grasp). Level 1 has four slots for [Detect Magic](spells-d-e.md#srd51-spell-detect-magic), [Identify](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-identify), [Mage Armor](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-mage-armor), and [Magic Missile](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-magic-missile). Level 2 has three slots for [Detect Thoughts](spells-d-e.md#srd51-spell-detect-thoughts), [Mirror Image](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-mirror-image), and [Misty Step](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-misty-step). Level 3 has three slots for [Counterspell](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-counterspell), [Fly](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-fly), and [Lightning Bolt](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-lightning-bolt). Level 4 has three slots for [Banishment](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-banishment), [Fire Shield](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-fire-shield), and [Stoneskin](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-stoneskin). Level 5 has three slots for [Cone of Cold](spells-a-c.md#srd51-spell-cone-of-cold), [Scrying](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-scrying), and [Wall of Force](spells-t-z.md#srd51-spell-wall-of-force). Level 6 has one slot for [Globe of Invulnerability](spells-e-g.md#srd51-spell-globe-of-invulnerability). Level 7 has one slot for [Teleport](spells-t-z.md#srd51-spell-teleport). Level 8 has one slot for [Mind Blank](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-mind-blank). Level 9 has one slot for [Time Stop](spells-t-z.md#srd51-spell-time-stop). It casts [Mage Armor](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-mage-armor), [Stoneskin](spells-n-s.md#srd51-spell-stoneskin), and [Mind Blank](spells-h-m.md#srd51-spell-mind-blank) on itself before combat. Spend the appropriate slots and apply each spell’s components, duration, and concentration rules. The listed slot counts are capacities, so the slots spent before combat are already expended unless recovered. [SRD p. 396, Archmage, Spellcasting continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-396.md) [SRD p. 395, Archmage, Spellcasting](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-395.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-archmage-dagger"></a> **Action — Dagger.** `srd51.creature.archmage.dagger` Melee or ranged weapon, +6, reach 5 feet or normal/long range 20/60 feet, one target. A hit deals 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage. [SRD p. 396, Archmage, Dagger](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-396.md)

**Role in the campaign.** A learned arcane caster. A benevolent archmage may advise a ruler, an evil one may rule as a tyrant or pursue lichdom, and a neutral one may practice in isolation. The archmage commonly has apprentices and a home with wards and guardians. [SRD p. 396, Archmage](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-396.md)

## Assassin

`srd51.creature.assassin`

**Profile.** Medium humanoid (any race) · any non-good alignment · CR 8 (3,900 XP) · 78 average HP (12d8 + 24) · AC 15 (studded leather) · walk 30 ft.

**Abilities.** Str 11 (+0) · Dex 16 (+3) · Con 14 (+2) · Int 13 (+1) · Wis 11 (+0) · Cha 10 (+0). Save bonuses Dex +6, Int +4. Skill bonuses Acrobatics +6, Deception +3, Perception +3, Stealth +9.

**Awareness and defenses.** Passive Perception 13. Languages Thieves’ cant plus any two languages. Damage resistance to poison. [SRD p. 396, Assassin](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-396.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-assassin-assassinate"></a> **Trait — Assassinate.** `srd51.creature.assassin.assassinate` On the assassin's first turn, a creature that has not taken a turn yet is an eligible target for its attack-roll advantage. Any hit it scores against a surprised creature is critical. [SRD p. 396, Assassin, Assassinate](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-396.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-assassin-evasion"></a> **Trait — Evasion.** `srd51.creature.assassin.evasion` Apply the [Evasion rule](classes-fighter-monk.md#srd51-class-monk-evasion). [SRD p. 396, Assassin, Evasion](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-396.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-assassin-sneak-attack"></a> **Trait — Sneak Attack.** `srd51.creature.assassin.sneak-attack` Once per turn, add 14 (4d6) damage to a hit with a weapon attack if the roll has advantage, or if a non-incapacitated ally is within 5 feet of the target and the roll does not have disadvantage. The assassin's trait applies to weapon attacks generally. [SRD p. 396, Assassin, Sneak Attack](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-396.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-assassin-multiattack"></a> **Action — Multiattack.** `srd51.creature.assassin.multiattack` Make two Shortsword attacks. [SRD p. 396, Assassin, Multiattack](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-396.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-assassin-shortsword"></a> **Action — Shortsword.** `srd51.creature.assassin.shortsword` Melee weapon, +6, reach 5 feet, one target. A hit deals 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage. The target also makes a DC 15 Constitution save, taking 24 (7d6) poison damage on failure or half on success. [SRD p. 396, Assassin, Shortsword](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-396.md)

<a id="srd51-creature-assassin-light-crossbow"></a> **Action — Light Crossbow.** `srd51.creature.assassin.light-crossbow` Ranged weapon, +6, normal/long range 80/320 feet, one target. A hit deals 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage. The target also makes a DC 15 Constitution save, taking 24 (7d6) poison damage on failure or half on success. [SRD p. 396, Assassin, Light Crossbow](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-396.md)

**Role in the campaign.** A trained killer hired by patrons such as sovereigns and guildmasters, with specialized use of poison. [SRD p. 396, Assassin](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-396.md)

- **Ordinary.** A guard can use its spear at range 20/60 feet for 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing damage on a hit. A two-handed melee hit instead deals 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing. [SRD p. 399, Guard, Spear](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-399.md) [SRD p. 400, Guard, Spear continuation](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-400.md)
- **Boundary.** A veteran can add the shortsword attack to Multiattack if it has the shortsword drawn. The two longsword attacks are its ordinary Multiattack. [SRD p. 403, Veteran, Multiattack](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-403.md)
- **Exception.** The 1st-level Acolyte NPC has three 1st-level spell slots in its profile. Use those listed slots for the NPC, alongside its three prepared 1st-level spells. [SRD p. 395, Acolyte, Spellcasting](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-395.md)
- **Weapon category.** A gladiator’s spear hit at 20 feet uses the printed 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing damage. The two-handed 13 (2d8 + 4) branch is for melee. [SRD p. 399, Gladiator, Brute and Spear](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-399.md)
- **Invalid.** Knight Leadership cannot add its d4 for a creature that cannot hear or understand the knight. Changing a Knight's armor is not a spell swap and can change CR through AC. [SRD p. 395, Armor and Weapon Swaps](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-395.md) [SRD p. 400, Knight, Leadership](../../references/srd-5.1/pages/page-400.md)
